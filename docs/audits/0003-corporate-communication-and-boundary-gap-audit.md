# Corporate design, communication design, and boundary gap audit

Date: 2026-09-02  
Status: revised scope proposal awaiting final SOL review  
Research: [`corporate-communication-and-boundary-research.md`](../research/corporate-communication-and-boundary-research.md)

## Executive verdict

The successor is materially stronger than RC7, but the newly stated all-round
designer goal exposes seven gaps that must be addressed before open
qualification continues:

1. Corporate Design/CI is present but not explicit or strict enough for
   incumbent conformance.
2. Standalone logo and identity-mark design is intentionally outside the
   accepted 23-leaf depth and must now become a real capability.
3. General communication intent is distributed correctly, but the Core lacks a
   compact communication contract and Brief/Concept do not yet expose enough
   interpretation, counter-reading and proof structure.
4. Instructional/explanatory and advertising/campaign art direction have
   independent tasks and causes not owned at professional depth.
5. Packaging graphics has a flat-to-assembled, panel and SKU system that Fixed
   Media cannot own at professional depth without becoming a monolith.
6. Physical wayfinding has journey, destination, decision-point and
   post-occupancy causes absent from Fixed Media, UI navigation and cartography.
7. The package needs an executable split test for when overlap should stay soft
   and when a specialist must own the decision, mutation and proof.

Do **not** solve this with a universal `communication-design` expert. It would
load on most visual work and repeat Brief, Concept and craft rules. Preserve
the flat direct multi-select architecture and add only independently routable
specialists.

## Architecture decisions proposed

### A. Corporate Design stays with Brand

Do not add a duplicate Corporate Design leaf. Retain the stable ID
`brand-and-visual-systems` but change its visible title, route label and opening
to include `Corporate Design`, `corporate visual identity`, `visual identity`,
`brand manual`, `brand guidelines`, `brand portal`, `brand architecture`,
`on-brand`, `CI audit` and `rebrand/migration`.

Keep exactly one package-wide canonical mode and add a separate Brand operation:

- `create`
- `apply`
- `audit`
- `evolve`
- `document`

Map `create` to `generate` or bounded `style-direction`; `apply` to `generate`
or `repair`; `audit` to read-only `critique`; `evolve` to authorised
`generate`, `repair`, or `style-direction`; and `document` to `generate` or
`repair` without inventing decisions. Record a separate `incumbent relation:
conform | authorised change | no applicable incumbent | unresolved`.
`No applicable incumbent` requires an explicit declaration or a recorded
authority check; missing files are not evidence. Unknown currency, authority,
scope and equal-rank conflicts remain `unresolved` and block non-provisional
identity mutation. Validators must require one canonical mode and reject a
Brand operation used as that mode.

The `conform` condition is binding: a current approved CI outranks an aesthetically
preferred alternative. The agent may use only explicit freedom, request a
permitted variant, or record/block a conflict. Accessibility, law, rights,
safety, platform/provider conditions and missing authority are not aesthetic
exceptions.

Add a CI contract:

`rule | source/version/location | owner | scope | mandatory/recommended/open |
invariant | allowed variation | forbidden mutation | precedence |
exception owner/expiry | proof`.

Brand repairs a bad system rule; a craft/medium owner repairs a local
misapplication of a valid rule. Scoville UI continues to implement the current
framework/design system strictly. Design owns corporate-design/visual-identity
definition, ambiguity,
system exceptions and visual conformance judgment.

Generic Corporate Identity is not a Design route. Purpose, strategy,
behaviour, culture, naming and verbal identity remain authorised upstream
inputs unless a visual-system decision is actually open.

### B. Add a distinct identity-mark expert

Add `logo-and-identity-mark-design` because logo/wordmark work is independently
routable and the current Brand file would otherwise become a monolith.

**Positive route:** creating, comparing, critiquing or repairing an identity
mark, wordmark, monogram, emblem, combination mark, responsive mark family,
lockup form or optical identity variant.

**Negative route:** placement, scaling, export or strict use of a fully
specified approved canonical mark. Brand Conformance plus the actual craft or
Production owner is sufficient.

Brand owns the mark's role in architecture, the required configuration set,
inheritance and governance. The mark expert owns each configuration's formal
construction, relationships and optical variants. Typography owns the general type subsystem, not custom
wordmark form. Culture/Rights/Source/counsel own meaning authority, provenance,
similarity research and legal conclusions.

### C. No universal Communication Design leaf

Add a small Core communication record and strengthen existing owners:

- Brief: sender/authority, audience knowledge/context, communication job,
  required interpretation/action, likely misunderstanding and consequence,
  success evidence.
- Concept: literal content, intended inference, visual-verbal relation,
  rhetorical mechanism, plausible counter-reading, ethical risk, medium
  survival and falsifier.
- Core record: `job | sender | audience/context | interpretation/action |
  evidence | modal relation | misreading/consequence | protected content |
  proof | unknowns`.

Do not reload Brief or Concept after their decisions are accepted. A topic word
such as `communication` never selects a leaf by itself.

### D. Add five narrow specialists

#### `instructional-and-explanatory-design`

Select when a visual artifact must teach a concept, correct a misconception,
explain a mechanism, guide a procedure, communicate a warning, or prove
understanding. Own objective/prior knowledge, explanation sequence,
visual-verbal coordination, cognitive-load causes, step/referent/check-state
integrity, warning chain, symbol-comprehension boundary and comprehension test.

Do not select for a generic diagram with no learning/understanding objective,
a curriculum/lesson plan, long-form wording, UI onboarding mechanics, or source
truth alone.

For hazardous procedures, require an authoritative supplied procedure, hazard
model and warning content. The leaf may transform and test the explanation but
must not infer a safe sequence or warning. Comprehension evidence remains
separate from qualified safety, regulatory and domain acceptance.

#### `advertising-and-campaign-art-direction`

Select when an advertisement, persuasive key visual, campaign family,
paid-social/display/out-of-home creative or cross-placement campaign system is
created, critiqued or repaired. Own visual thesis, proposition mapping,
attention–comprehension–credibility–action relation, creative variants,
placement transformation and test contract.

Do not select for media buying, targeting, budget, attribution, copy-only work,
an ordinary identity application or platform-policy lookup. Brand invariants,
Source truth, Scribe wording and current platform authority remain binding.

#### `packaging-graphics-and-sku-systems`

Select when graphics on a supplied authoritative dieline must coordinate pack
faces/panels, assembled viewing/opening states, product hierarchy, SKU
invariants/variation, shelf or thumbnail recognition, or packaging-specific
e-commerce derivatives. Own those graphic relations and their mockup proof.

Do not select for dieline engineering, materials/protection, mandatory legal
content, barcode specification/certification, preflight/provider acceptance,
or an already approved pack that only needs technical export. Fixed Media keeps
ordinary pages, spreads, slides, posters, documents and folded publications;
it no longer owns packaging panel/SKU systems.

#### `physical-wayfinding-and-signage-systems`

Select for a physical journey, destination hierarchy, decision-point/location
schedule, sign family, environmental orientation system, route-integrated map,
or post-occupancy wayfinding audit. Own the journey model, approved-label
structure/application, wayfinding information sequence, spatial/sign/map cue
relationship, prototype and route-evaluation contract.

Do not select for website/app navigation, a standalone map, an isolated poster
or room sign, non-navigational exhibition/environmental graphic, regulated
escape/safety signage, traffic planning, authoritative destination naming,
spatial/GIS truth, structural/electrical installation or fabrication
acceptance.

The fifth new specialist is the Identity Mark expert in section B. Corporate
Design remains an expansion of Brand rather than a sixth new leaf.

### E. Use hard centres and soft edges

The following distinction applies to every expert:

- **Hard owner:** may define or change the canonical decision and parent cause.
- **Soft observer:** may detect a cross-domain effect and hand it to the owner.
- **Local repair:** the craft/medium owner fixes a valid rule's application.
- **System repair:** the system owner fixes a faulty or contradictory rule.
- **Proof boundary:** only the qualified evidence owner can claim compliance,
  comprehension, recognition, production acceptance, rights or safety.

The shared Design record is a boundary object, not proof of consensus. It must
preserve owner, authority, version, allowed variation, unknowns and dissent
while remaining compact enough to pass between phases without loading sibling
files. Observers add attributed observations only. The canonical field owner
alone updates a decision/status; source-version changes invalidate affected
fields until rechecked, and unresolved conflict propagates without a guessed
winner.

## Split versus overlap decision

The boundary research supports neither completely isolated experts nor one
universal communication module. The project therefore applies six admission
tests:

1. **Parent cause:** the candidate owns a decision no existing leaf may safely
   change.
2. **Contrast route:** positive and near-neighbour negative prompts identify it
   without relying on its topic name.
3. **Owned state:** it produces a distinct record, invariant set or
   transformation rather than repeating a general lens.
4. **Distinct proof:** professional judgment requires a different context,
   artifact, test or receiver.
5. **Stop boundary:** it must hand off a materially different authority or
   qualified profession.
6. **Token value:** loading it changes generation, diagnosis or repair enough
   to justify its cost.

If these tests fail, keep the concern as a soft observation or field in the
shared record. The proposed Packaging, Wayfinding, Advertising, Instructional
and Mark leaves pass all six at architecture level and must still pass routing
and behavioural falsification. General Communication fails the routability and
token-value tests and remains a non-owning Core record.

This produces a proposed **28-leaf** successor: the accepted 23 plus five new
specialists. The number becomes canonical only after SOL accepts this revision
and the source/routing artifacts are made executable.
