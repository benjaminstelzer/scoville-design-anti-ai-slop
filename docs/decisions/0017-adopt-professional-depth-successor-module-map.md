# Adopt the professional-depth successor module map

## Decision

Replace the current fourteen-leaf RC7 map with twenty-three flat, directly
routed expert leaves after the W-013 SOL and Fable review gate passes:

- `brief-framing-and-criteria`
- `concept-development-and-selection`
- `composition-and-layout`
- `typography-and-typesetting`
- `font-technology-and-script-safety`
- `colour-and-reproduction`
- `imagery-and-art-direction`
- `information-design-and-data-visualization`
- `cartography-and-spatial-data`
- `diagrams-and-relational-information`
- `brand-and-visual-systems`
- `ui-workflow-and-interaction-design`
- `web-and-responsive-design`
- `editorial-and-fixed-media-design`
- `motion-and-sequence`
- `media-production-and-handoff`
- `critique-and-validation`
- `culture-and-representation`
- `people-privacy-and-media-integrity`
- `sustainability-claims`
- `source-verification-and-evidence`
- `asset-rights-and-attribution`
- `style-direction`

Keep source bibliography and full provenance in a non-routed index. Every
expert remains a direct Core route with no sibling read, `requires`, or hidden
runtime dependency. Small local integrity and ownership floors may be repeated
where independent operation requires them.

Professional correctness and applied causal depth come before token size. The
first complete leaves may exceed the former 1,800-token target. Compression
occurs only after the complete route passes generation, discrimination,
critique, repair, exception, ownership, and proof tests.

## Problem

The RC7 map is selective and structurally sound, but several single leaves
contain independently selectable concerns with different failure models and
authorities. Other leaves are compact topic inventories rather than sufficient
applied expert instructions. Keeping the old map would either force large mixed
payloads or preserve the missing professional depth.

## Drivers

- The Skill must design, distinguish, and improve real work.
- Typography, composition, spacing, hierarchy, negative space, style, and
  medium translation are primary quality concerns.
- Responsive web, fixed/editorial design, cartography, diagrams, privacy,
  sustainability claims, source verification, and asset rights have distinct
  signals and proof contracts.
- Each Skill must remain independently usable and must not search for or
  simulate another Skill.
- Topic count, repository stars, or shorter prompts are not quality evidence.

## Considered alternatives

- Deepen all fourteen leaves without splits. This keeps routing simple but
  makes ordinary chart, UI, source, culture, or typography tasks load unrelated
  expert material.
- Split every topic into a leaf. This fragments causal ownership and creates
  routine co-loading for spacing, grids, image crop, and production.
- Put the full curriculum in Core. This defeats progressive disclosure and
  spends context on unrelated work.
- Keep RC7 and depend on frontier-model knowledge. The audits show that correct
  topic recall does not supply a stable applied repair and proof contract.

## Consequences

- `modules.yaml`, generated Core routing, references, source maps, maturity
  records, route fixtures, manifests, and evaluation cases change together.
- Split leaves repeat only the minimum integrity/ownership floor necessary for
  standalone use.
- Named style profiles remain withheld; the general style compiler stays one
  leaf.
- Brand, colour, imagery, motion, production, composition, critique, and style
  remain single leaves until route and outcome evidence justifies a split.
- Packaging and wayfinding remain bounded generalist floors, not specialist
  competence claims.

## Confirmation

Each direct route must pass model-free positive and near-neighbor negative
fixtures, source-resolution and read-graph validation, and an immediately
inspected Terra High applied canary. The full map must remain under the mixed
context ceiling in plausible combinations or use explicit phase splitting
without hiding dependencies.

## Revisit when

A route cannot be selected independently, routinely needs a sibling for the
same parent cause, or a smaller/larger grouping proves non-inferior across
applied outcomes and context cost.
