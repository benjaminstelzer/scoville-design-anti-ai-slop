This comparison uses the same ten-part Stage-two schema as the other domain
files. A repository entered the star ranking only when an exact design-brief,
concept-development, or visual-direction exploration Skill and an E1 or higher
artifact were inspectable. Repository popularity, exact-Skill adoption,
evidence strength, and design quality remain separate.

## 1. Decision

The three qualifying repositories found are `garrytan/gstack`,
`nexu-io/open-design`, and `LeoYeAI/openclaw-master-skills`. Gstack has the
strongest working rough-generation, comparison, feedback, and approval loop.
Open Design has the strongest explicit missing-field and deterministic token
resolution, but its `design-brief` is actually a closed visual-system parser,
not a communication brief. The mirrored `variant-design` Skill offers the most
granular variation and repair actions, but it mistakes aesthetic divergence
for conceptual divergence and carries extensive fixed recipes.

No ranked candidate covers the full professional chain. That chain separates
problem framing, concept divergence, comparable roughs, selection, and causal
repair. None freezes an exact content and claim manifest, derives hard gates
from owner evidence, handles the quiet utility-artifact exception, compares
format-set survival, or states a defensible originality ceiling at the depth of
the current Scoville audit.

Adopt only mechanisms through original synthesis. The useful gains are a
visible missing-field record, parallel comparable instantiation, a side-by-side
decision surface, preserved feedback, and named revision operations. Keep the
audit's recommendation to split future ownership into
`brief-framing-and-criteria` and `concept-development-and-selection`. The split
follows independent task signals and artifacts, not a token target.

## 3. Candidate 1: Gstack `design-shotgun`

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

The Skill gathers who, job, emotional outcome, constraints, and edge cases,
then records prior approved and rejected preferences. It writes several text
concepts, confirms them before paid generation, produces visual variants in
parallel, checks files, opens a side-by-side comparison board, accepts ratings
and comments, regenerates or remixes, confirms the interpreted feedback, and
saves an approved choice.

Its strongest mechanism is the physical decision loop. Options become
inspectable artifacts before selection, the user sees them together, failures
remain visible, and the selected branch plus feedback survives for downstream
work. The test suite supports the board, generation, retry, and feedback
infrastructure. It does not establish that the alternatives are conceptually
different or that the selected one best solves the brief.

### Better than current Scoville

- Parallel roughs and one comparison surface make unequal serial presentation
  less likely.
- Concept confirmation occurs before provider cost and high-fidelity work.
- Feedback is captured as ratings, comments, preferred direction, regeneration
  or remix intent, then confirmed before persistence.
- Approved and rejected history is available as project evidence rather than
  silent model preference.
- Generation failures, missing variants, and degraded paths remain explicit.

### Reject or revise

- Different fonts, palettes, and layouts do not prove different concepts. They
  are treatment variables. Scoville must compare organizing mechanism,
  subject carrier, order, action, relation, and invariant with style columns
  hidden.
- A high-fidelity image-generation call is often too expensive and too noisy
  for the first rough. Use the cheapest evidence-bearing artifact that can
  expose the decision.
- Preference ratings do not replace hard communication, access, rights, and
  production gates. A favored option can still be ineligible.
- Persistent taste history may help continuity but cannot override the current
  brief, incumbent system, intended audience, or evidence. It introduces
  privacy, retention, bias, and stale-preference concerns.
- Gstack-specific daemons, browser board, image service, telemetry, filesystem
  schema, subagents, and approval files are optional implementation, not a
  portable Design requirement.

## 4. Candidate 2: Open Design `design-brief`

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

The Skill parses an I-Lang record or a natural-language request into eight
closed visual dimensions. It maps palette, accent, body type, display type,
layout, mood, density, and exclusions to fixed tokens, writes a nine-section
`DESIGN.md`, specifies an HTML token preview, and reports every defaulted
dimension plus the rule that supplied it.

This is a deterministic visual-system resolver. It is not a complete creative
brief. Purpose, audience task, barrier, required message and content, action,
owner, claims, evidence status, scope, deliverable set, success criteria,
approval authority, and consequential unknowns are absent or secondary. It
also performs no concept divergence, selection, or concept repair.

### Better than current Scoville

- Unspecified fields and the applied default rule are reported rather than
  hidden.
- A symbolic record can be transformed into a previewable artifact with stable
  downstream fields.
- The preview separates colour, type, spacing, and components enough to expose
  some internal contradictions before a full artifact is built.
- A closed vocabulary can be useful inside an already approved design system
  when it expresses owner-controlled variants.

### Reject or revise

- Do not call a palette, type, layout, mood, and density map a communication
  brief. Style choices must not replace the problem frame or concept.
- Do not fill an unknown with `Inter`, a blue accent, one column,
  professional-minimal mood, balanced density, fixed breakpoints, card radius,
  shadow, or spacing merely because the table has a default.
- The closed vocabulary rejects legitimate brand, language, script, medium,
  audience, and production needs. Unknown fields should stay unknown or route
  to the owner when they can change the decision.
- Fixed font counts, accent counts, breakpoint stacks, layout changes, and
  token values cannot become universal Scoville rules.
- The exact path contains no finished preview or test. Template completeness
  does not prove design, responsiveness, accessibility, or selection quality.

## 5. Candidate 3: Mirrored `variant-design`

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

The Skill initializes audience, purpose, brand, preferences, constraints, and
scenario. It loads domain and design-system references, generates three
directions, implements them, runs a generic-slop check, and exposes named
operations. `Vary strong`, `Vary subtle`, `Distill`, `Change style`, `Remix
colors`, `Shuffle layout`, `Polish`, `Critique`, `Extract tokens`, and `See
other views` distinguish several kinds of revision that are often collapsed
into “make another version.”

The variation vocabulary is the main transferable mechanism. It can hold some
dimensions steady while changing another. Its divergence rule is still
surface-led. Directions are selected from preset aesthetic families, each is
required to use a different palette, type family, and layout, and the artifact
is then exported as HTML or React.

### Better than current Scoville

- Revision intent is named, which reduces accidental full redesign when only
  strength, colour, layout, polish, or viewpoint should change.
- Distillation asks whether removal breaks the core purpose.
- Change-style and shuffle-layout operations can become controlled variants
  when the thesis, content, and other variables are held constant.
- Additional-view prompts at least expose narrow, empty, state, and dark-mode
  conditions.
- Real content is preferred to lorem ipsum in the written workflow.

### Reject or revise

- Three outputs, seven aesthetic families, two typefaces, a 4-point grid,
  60-30-10 colour, 100/300/500 motion, eight states, fixed breakpoints, fixed
  target sizes, and named font bans are recipes, not concept reasoning.
- “Different studios” and distinct surface treatment do not establish
  materially different message construction.
- A generic “would someone believe AI made this” question is not a quality,
  user, originality, or authorship test.
- The Skill mixes Brief, Concept, Style, Typography, Colour, Composition, UI,
  Motion, Scribe, Code, and Accessibility ownership. Scoville should preserve
  flat direct owners and one compact decision record.
- The aggregation copy and stated Impeccable lineage require attribution and
  source review. Do not copy the catalog, examples, or rules into Scoville.

## 7. Domain coverage and failure-to-cause-to-repair map

The following distinctions are binding for the future architecture:

- **Framing** decides what problem, content, audience context, owner, evidence,
  unknown, hard gate, and comparative criterion governs the work.
- **Concept divergence** changes how subject evidence constructs meaning or
  action. It does not merely change presentation.
- **Roughing** gives every viable mechanism comparable real-content evidence at
  the cheapest fidelity that can reverse a decision.
- **Selection** removes gate failures first, then exposes trade-offs,
  uncertainty, authority, and the next test. It is not a popularity score.
- **Repair** localizes the parent cause and changes the smallest coherent
  layer while preserving the strong parts and fixed frame.

| Observed failure | Likely cause | Smallest coherent repair | Recheck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Territories are palette, font, or layout siblings | Surface variation was used as the diversity test | Hide treatment fields and change carrier, content order, action, relation, or meaning construction for weaker options | Pairwise mechanism matrix with real content |
| Brief repeats the requested artifact as the problem | Outcome, audience task, barrier, and action were never separated | Rewrite the frame without style words and attach source, owner, and unknown state to each material field | Owner review and problem-to-criterion trace |
| One option wins because it looks finished | Options have unequal fidelity or were shown serially | Reduce all survivors to the same decision-relevant fidelity or develop them equally | Side-by-side and order-balanced review where practical |
| Concept prose sounds strong but rough fails | No carrier or invariant was specified | Build the smallest carrier-specific rough and repair hierarchy or carrier before rewriting the territory | Same-content rough comparison |
| Required copy, claim, or data disappears | No manifest or mechanism assumes ideal brevity | Restore the exact item, adjust hierarchy and carrier, or reject the mechanism | Literal, data, asset, and source parity |
| Utility screen becomes theatrical | Distinctiveness was treated as a gate | Reframe around task clarity, familiarity, error prevention, system behavior, or progressive disclosure | Task, error, comprehension, and incumbent-system review |
| Concept collapses on mobile, print, crop, or reduced mode | Meaning depends on one canvas or sensory channel | Move meaning to a more adaptable carrier and define variant-specific composition or equivalent | Highest-risk format and access set |
| Selection matrix conceals disagreement | Unknowns were scored as facts or criteria appeared after a favorite | Restore hard gates, mark unknown or disputed fields, and prototype the uncertainty that can change the decision | Sensitivity review and decision-owner sign-off |
| Polishing cannot remove genericity | Frame is false or carrier lacks subject evidence | Correct the frame, insert verified subject relation, and regenerate affected mechanisms | Updated frame event plus subject-swap test |

## 8. Mechanisms to synthesize

### Future `brief-framing-and-criteria` leaf

1. Record purpose, audience task and viewing context, barrier, required
   message, action, exact content and claim manifest, deliverable set, medium,
   incumbent owner, hard constraints, evidence status, forbidden claims and
   assets, success criteria, approval authority, and consequential unknowns.
2. Distinguish supplied, verified, inferred, disputed, unknown, and
   owner-decision fields. Never complete the record with invented research.
3. Freeze non-negotiable gates and comparative criteria before seeing
   concepts. Record a frame-revision event when a rough exposes a false frame.
4. Produce a compact evidence-status brief. Do not pick fonts, palettes,
   styles, or layouts unless the owner already fixed them as constraints.

### Future `concept-development-and-selection` leaf

1. Generate materially different organizing mechanisms from the frame and
   subject evidence. Name primary and supporting carriers, invariant,
   hierarchy, medium advantage, likely failure, and preserved truth for each.
2. Include a quiet utility direction when familiarity, safety, speed, repeated
   use, or task clarity makes expression harmful.
3. Instantiate survivors in comparable roughs with the same real content and
   evidence level. Show them together and protect them from serial anchoring.
4. Apply hard gates first. Compare communication, specificity, feasibility,
   access, evidence, production, and distinctiveness as trade-offs, not a
   mathematically objective score.
5. Record the outcome as select, develop, test, combine, defer, reject, or
   reframe, with decision owner, uncertainty, rejected reasons, and next proof.
6. Repair the parent cause and preserve strong elements. Re-render only the
   affected format set before claiming improvement.

### Shared evidence package

- frozen brief and content manifest
- pairwise mechanism matrix with style fields hidden
- same-content comparable rough set and failure receipts
- gate and trade-off record with unknowns and authority
- highest-risk format, access, and production prototype
- selected thesis, invariant, rejected options, repair history, and claim
  ceiling

## 9. Reject and claim ceiling

Reject from the executable package:

- fixed question, section, concept, rough, finalist, reference, font, palette,
  layout, breakpoint, spacing, or signature counts
- aesthetic dials, preset families, token tables, or change-style actions
  presented as concept mechanisms
- mandatory image generation, comparison servers, proprietary providers,
  persistent taste telemetry, subagents, React, Tailwind, or one artifact
  schema
- user preference presented as audience evidence or a scored matrix presented
  as objective selection
- a polished option compared with labels or sketches
- style, mood board, reference collection, logo swap, or anti-slop vocabulary
  presented as concept or originality proof
- novelty, memorability, metaphor, motion, or expressive signature forced into
  utility artifacts
- `original`, `unprecedented`, `trademark-safe`, `non-infringing`, `validated`,
  `accessible`, `feasible`, or `production-ready` without the exact evidence
  and authority those claims require

Even after the proposed mechanism passes open tests, the claim ceiling remains
local. It may support improved framing, mechanism diversity, comparable
roughing, selection transparency, format survival, and repair for the tested
briefs, artifacts, media, languages, tools, and reviewers. It cannot establish
global originality, audience success, professional equivalence, market
strategy, cultural authority, rights clearance, or superiority over all
creative processes.

  web scope, and reference fixation are rejected.

  GitHub code search reached a rate limit after the targeted funnel. Private,

  content, a quiet utility option, three genuinely different mechanisms, a

  carrier. Compare framing accuracy, pairwise mechanism difference,
  same-content roughs, hard-gate selection, hierarchy, typography, spacing,
  negative space, subject specificity, repair, and evidence honesty under
  blinded or order-balanced review where practical.
