This comparison uses the same ten-part Stage-two schema as the other domain
files. A repository entered the ranking only when an exact named-style,
reference-to-visual-language, or broad style-steering Skill and an E1 or higher
artifact were inspectable. Broad frontend popularity did not qualify by itself.
Stars, exact-Skill use, evidence, historical authority, and design quality are
reported separately.

## 1. Decision

The three qualifying repositories found are `nexu-io/open-design`,
`Leonxlnx/taste-skill`, and `MengTo/Skills`. Open Design has the highest-starred
exact named-style templates and the strongest runnable example pairing. The
current Taste Skill supplies the broadest anti-default style steering plus an
exact Minimalist profile and visible examples. MengTo supplies the strongest
reference-grammar versus protected-signature decomposition and the clearest
multi-output subject variation.

None is a professionally sufficient Style compiler. Open Design and Taste
Skill reduce Minimalist and Brutalist directions to rigid web recipes. MengTo
works from contemporary brand-image references, not sourced movement history.
No candidate establishes label disambiguation, dominant versus secondary
lineage, production technology, anachronism, living cultural authority,
incumbent brand invariants, item-level rights, cross-medium equivalence, or
causal repair at the depth of the current Scoville audit.

The architecture decision remains one deeper `style-direction` compiler and no
runtime atlas. Named profiles are withheld until one has profile-specific
sources, history and production context, rights and cultural review,
generation, critique, repair, translation, several subjects and media, and
open evidence that it outperforms the compiler without more cargo cult.

## 3. Candidate 1: Open Design named-style templates

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

Open Design distills the light Swiss-print branch of an imported Brutalist
profile into a web prototype and the dark tactical-telemetry branch into a
fixed-ratio deck. The web template locks substrate, accent, type roles, grid,
negative-space pattern, geometry, ASCII devices, motion, required components,
and a preflight. The paired HTML implements a responsive page with a register
strip, giant type, manifest, index grid, specimen, alert, and colophon.

This is a strong recipe-to-artifact trace. It is not a style interpretation
method. The direction is selected before the user arrives, one branch is
declared authoritative, and nearly every trait is mandatory. The exact source
credit to Taste Skill is better than silent copying, but it does not supply
historical or cultural authority for Swiss design, Brutalism, military visual
language, or their relationship.

### Better than current Scoville

- A single broad upstream profile is split into two distinct production and
  medium branches instead of invisibly mixing light print and dark CRT cues.
- The example makes claims about layout, type, spacing, negative space, and
  responsive behavior inspectable in actual source.
- Source derivation is named in the template.
- The preflight is tied to exact output properties rather than vague style
  adjectives.
- Web and deck are treated as different artifacts rather than one screenshot
  resized to another ratio.

### Reject or revise

- Do not equate Swiss typographic print, architectural Brutalism, web
  Brutalism, punk print, military telemetry, and terminal nostalgia.
- Fixed newsprint, carbon ink, hazard red, type families, uppercase, tracking,
  leading, square corners, ASCII counts, section packs, marquee, and grid
  techniques are one costume recipe, not general DNA.
- Registration and trademark symbols cannot be decoration when their legal or
  semantic meaning would mislead.
- The HTML proves one fictional subject and one viewport-aware implementation.
  It does not prove cross-subject recognizability, typesetting quality,
  native-reader behavior, accessibility, print, or historical fidelity.
- Medium profiles must translate one sourced invariant and state what changes,
  not select a different trope bundle for each medium.

## 4. Candidate 2: Taste Skill and its Minimalist profile

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

Broad Taste reads page kind, audience, user wording, references, incumbent
brand assets, and quiet constraints before selecting a design direction. It
states a one-line Design Read, sets variance, motion, and density dials, maps
some briefs to official design systems, and applies a large anti-default rule
set with override paths. The exact Minimalist profile then prescribes warm
monochrome colour, editorial serif contrast, flat bento grids, muted pastels,
macro whitespace, sparse motion, and fixed component details.

The broad Skill is an anti-cliche frontend router. The Minimalist Skill is a
narrow premium-editorial web recipe. The three Floria images give the recipe a
visible target, but they show one subject and do not establish that the broad
router selected or produced them.

### Better than current Scoville

- Page kind, audience, references, incumbent assets, and consequential
  constraints precede aesthetic choice.
- Official design systems are distinguished from unofficial web aesthetics.
- Many default bans include an explicit override when the brief or incumbent
  system genuinely requires the trait.
- Redesign starts from existing assets rather than treating style as permission
  to replace them.
- The broad anti-default inventory identifies common convergence patterns that
  can become hypotheses for causal critique.

### Minimalist finding

The Minimalist profile confirms the current audit's warning. It defines
Minimalism as warm off-white, charcoal, editorial serif, bento cards, washed
pastels, large section padding, fixed borders, quiet scroll reveals, and
specific components. Its visible Floria example may be coherent for that one
web subject. It cannot define Minimalism across functional products, modernist
systems, reductive art, architecture, regional practices, print, motion, or
other subjects.

### Reject or revise

- Reject fixed design dials, preset tables, font pools, pairings, palette bans,
  one-accent maxima, spacing defaults, anti-centering thresholds, section
  architecture, breakpoints, target sizes, motion curves, and dependency lists
  as Style rules.
- Anti-cliche lists diagnose repeated model habits. They do not prove that a
  named trait is always wrong or that its opposite fits the subject.
- The broad Skill conflates style interpretation, framework selection,
  typography, layout, colour, UI, motion, accessibility, and implementation.
  Scoville must route those owners independently.
- No history, source confidence, dominant lineage, production cause,
  anachronism, living cultural authority, or item-level rights record supports
  the named labels.
- One attractive Minimalist page is E1 output, not a style atlas or evidence
  that its type, spacing, negative space, hierarchy, or responsiveness is
  professionally superior.

## 5. Candidate 3: MengTo reference-inspired brand worlds

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

The Skill inspects a supplied visual source, separates reusable grammar from
protected signatures, then changes brand meaning, ritual, environment, motif,
and named signature elements across several image outputs. It preserves
versions, reinspects the results, and asks whether the new brand world has its
own subject logic rather than being a recoloured reference.

This is closer to reference transformation than named-style interpretation.
The five JPG examples are the strongest visible evidence in the ranked set
that one mechanism can produce several distinct subjects. They remain image
outputs. They do not demonstrate a full text-bearing artifact, responsive
translation, print, interaction, motion, or an incumbent identity system.

### Better than current Scoville

- Reusable relations and source-specific signatures are separated before
  generation.
- Subject meaning, ritual, environment, motif, and brand behavior are treated
  as variables, not only palette and texture.
- Versions are preserved, which supports comparison and repair rather than
  silent overwrite.
- Several visible outputs make overfitting and repeated composition easier to
  inspect.
- Reinspection is required after generation.

### Reject or revise

- Percentage similarity is not a measurable or defensible design-distance
  control.
- Fixed numbers of constants, differences, signatures, or variants are
  recipes. The subject and decision determine how many distinctions matter.
- A source image can carry trade dress, copyrighted expression, trademark,
  people, places, cultural material, and generated-asset uncertainty. “Protected
  signature” wording does not replace an item-level rights record.
- Contemporary brand-image transformation does not establish movement
  history, production technology, cultural authority, or period accuracy.
- Image-only examples cannot prove typography, writing-system behavior,
  spacing rhythm, negative-space function in real content, accessibility, or
  medium translation.

## 7. Style coverage and failure-to-cause-to-repair map

Broad style steering and named-style profiles solve different problems.
Anti-default controls can expose model convergence, but they cannot tell which
historical or cultural sense a label names. A named profile can speed repeated
work, but only after the compiler has resolved source, context, production,
subject, brand, rights, and medium. Minimalist is the clearest negative case in
the inspected evidence.

| Observed failure | Likely parent cause | Smallest coherent repair | Recheck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimalist means cream, huge serif, bento cards, and decorative emptiness | One premium-editorial web recipe replaced a broad reduction method | Define what is reduced and why, restore required content and hierarchy, and give negative space a subject-specific job | Content parity, reading order, grouping, and several-size render |
| Brutalist work becomes terminal cosplay | Architectural, print, punk, raw-web, and military branches were blended | Select and source one intended lineage, restore structural honesty and useful density, and remove decorative terminal chrome | Trope-hidden style review plus task and access proof |
| “80s” always becomes synthwave and VHS | Period umbrella collapsed to later internet shorthand | Present a bounded sourced branch set, select by brief and context, and label revival or translation | Label record, audience recognition, and real-content render |
| Neoclassical becomes black, gold, and columns | Literal motif stands in for order, proportion, rhetoric, and material context | Strengthen one sourced structural relation and keep literal motifs only when they serve subject and medium | Motif-removed recognition review and subject-swap test |
| Hybrid resembles a random moodboard | No dominant system and secondary influences have no jobs | Choose one dominant lineage and assign every secondary influence a bounded role or remove it | Trait-to-source-to-job matrix and whole-artifact review |
| Distress or misregistration looks pasted on | Production effect has no historical cause or target-medium analogue | Recreate the causal relation with an appropriate process or remove it, then label simulation | Process record and final-output inspection |
| Direction claims period accuracy with later type or technology | Revival, translation, replica, and invention were not separated | Reclassify honestly or replace unsupported elements with documented choices | Dated object and production record |
| Named style damages incumbent identity | Style request overrode the owner and system invariants | Restore required mark, type, colour, content, access, and behavior or obtain an exception | Brand-owner review across representative touchpoints |
| Poster works but mobile, motion, or print collapses | Components and pixels were copied rather than relations translated | Map hierarchy, rhythm, production idea, and compensation to native carriers | Side-by-side media set, reduced mode, and print or provider proof |
| Anti-cliche repair makes work equally generic in the opposite direction | A ban list replaced subject and communication reasoning | Restore subject evidence and one causal structural relation, then permit familiar cues that still orient | Whole-artifact and intended-audience review |
| Repair adds more motifs but style remains weak | Weakest load-bearing relation was never identified | Remove unsupported signs and strengthen one composition, type, material, or production relation tied to the subject | Order-balanced review where practical |

## 8. Mechanisms to synthesize

Retain one directly routed Style compiler with a compact Style Record:

1. Record term, language, class, aliases, selected sense, alternatives,
   date/place/status, source, confidence, contradiction, and decision authority.
2. Choose one dominant lineage or incumbent system. Give every secondary
   influence or modifier a bounded structural, content, or medium job.
3. Connect purpose, context, and production cause to the smallest set of
   discriminating relations across hierarchy, composition, spatial rhythm,
   typography, colour, imagery, symbol, ornament, material, motion, sound, and
   interaction.
4. State what makes those relations serve this subject. Add a functional
   counterweight only to repair a conflict and a signature only when verified
   subject evidence supplies it.
5. Record familiar recognition cues, cliche risk, and what remains coherent
   when the most obvious cue is removed. Do not score motif counts.
6. Reconcile incumbent brand and design-system invariants, approved departure,
   access constraints, rights, and living cultural authority before generation.
7. Map each medium from invariant to native carrier, allowed variation, lost
   affordance, equivalent or compensation, and required proof.
8. Generate from the record, critique the whole real-content artifact, then
   repair observation to effect to cause to smallest change while preserving
   strong elements. Re-render affected media.

Style owns named and contextual visual grammar. Brief and Concept own the
communication problem and carriers. Brand owns durable identity and governance.
Composition, Typography, Colour, Imagery, Information, UI, and Motion own their
craft. Media and providers own actual production. Culture, Sources,
Accessibility, affected communities, historians, and rights owners govern
their consequential claims.

No named profile enters runtime from this comparison. Open Design and Taste
Skill are evidence for why an atlas can fixate the model. They are not evidence
that the Scoville compiler needs a Minimalist or Brutalist leaf.

## 9. Reject and claim ceiling

Reject from the executable package:

- style dials, motif counts, fixed trait counts, signature quotas, similarity
  percentages, house palettes, font lists, pairings, spacing scales, grids,
  density values, breakpoints, section packs, animation recipes, and universal
  bans
- Minimalist, Brutalist, Retro, Neoclassical, Swiss, Editorial, Luxury,
  regional, vernacular, or subcultural labels treated as one visual preset
- anti-cliche scores, opposite-of-trend choices, or attractive screenshots
  presented as taste, recognition, history, originality, or superiority proof
- a primary reference treated as permission, a museum page treated as an item
  license, or repository licensing treated as rights to every embedded asset
- named products, brands, designers, communities, military or government
  visual languages used as costume without authority and context
- style allowed to override communication, incumbent identity, exact content,
  writing-system behavior, accessibility, user task, or production truth
- `authentic`, `period-accurate`, `community-approved`, `rights-cleared`,
  `accessible`, `responsive`, `production-ready`, `original`, or
  `non-infringing` without the specific authority and evidence each claim needs

Passing future open cases may support only that the compiler improved label
disambiguation, source trace, structural synthesis, subject and brand fit,
cross-medium translation, and causal repair for the tested prompts, sources,
languages, artifacts, media, tools, and reviewers. It cannot establish an
exhaustive atlas, universal recognizability, authenticity, cultural authority,
permission, one canonical DNA, taste superiority, originality, or
professional equivalence.
