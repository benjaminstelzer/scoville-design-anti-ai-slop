### Current operational rules

**Generation.** The short operating instruction requires source ID, exact claim
or decision, URL/version/date, license/status, adapted-expression status,
attribution, asset exceptions, and current verification. The remainder is a
useful compact key for all package sources, not a procedure for generating a
claim or asset record.

**Critique.** The leaf supplies two excellent high-severity checks—“A link is
not permission” and a model citation is not proof that a source was read—but no
claim-to-source audit, source-independence check, asset/package inventory diff,
or license-condition diagnosis.

**Repair.** It says restricted comparison material stays outside the
repository. It does not define how to repair a stale claim, broken URL,
misapplied license, missing credit, incompatible adaptation, uncovered asset,
or generated-output record while preserving prior evidence.

**Exception.** Per-source notes identify embedded-item and reuse exceptions,
but the leaf has no general exception discipline for anonymous evidence,
unavailable originals, lawful exceptions/limitations, CC0 credit, archival
copies, private receipts, or counsel-approved use.

**Verification.** Living sources must be rechecked and individual source notes
often give good boundaries. There is no receipt schema proving what was opened,
which part supported which claim, which asset/version was examined, or whether
the final package and attribution output match the ledger.

## 2. What is already strong

1. **Original synthesis is explicit.** The package does not imply that public
   availability allows third-party prose, Skills, screenshots, datasets, or
   comparison artifacts to be vendored.
2. **Knowledge sources and shipped assets are distinguished.** The research
   ledger correctly states that permission to learn from a source is separate
   from permissions for fonts, images, icons, templates, data, brands, and
   other assets.
3. **The minimum record fields are directionally correct.** Claim/decision,
   source identity, URL/version/date, license/status, adapted expression,
   attribution, exceptions, and current verification are all material.
4. **A URL is neither semantic support nor permission.** This prevents two of
   the most common provenance failures: citing a page that does not support the
   statement and treating access as a reuse grant.
5. **A model citation is not an inspection receipt.** The leaf correctly
   refuses to turn generated citation-shaped text into evidence.
6. **Reuse status is bounded per source.** Entries distinguish CC BY,
   ShareAlike, NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, public-domain/CC0, official
   specifications, copyrighted reference-only works, and unknown terms rather
   than calling everything “open.”
7. **Embedded and item-level exceptions are repeatedly visible.** L-01, L-23,
   L-27, L-33, L-34, E-04, E-05, E-12, and others warn that a page, book,
   dataset, or collection-level label need not govern every embedded item.
8. **C2PA is bounded correctly.** L-39 says association and tamper evidence do
   not prove truth or permission, which matches the C2PA specification's own
   non-goal. [SA-09]
9. **Generated-media claims trigger current jurisdiction/provider checks.**
   L-38 and L-40 avoid making a remembered provider or copyright rule global.
10. **Normal application is intended to survive link failure.** The current
    rule-to-source map says the operational instruction stays local and only
    the external fact requiring a current check becomes unverified. That is the
    correct runtime-independence principle.

These strengths justify retaining a binding floor. They do not show that the
1,762-token bibliography changes source-verification or asset-rights behavior.

## 4. Rule-quality audit

### Classification

| Class | Rules that belong here | Override condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Binding constraints | Do not invent a source, retrieval, permission, attribution, rightsholder, release, fact, version, quote, verdict, or C2PA status; preserve supplied claims/assets; keep restricted evidence private; do not ship an unresolved required right or material falsehood. | Only verified replacement evidence, an authorized content change, or a competent authority's documented decision. |
| Evidence-bounded rules | Independent sources improve corroboration; primary/original sources reduce transmission loss; citations near claims improve traceability; persistent/versioned identifiers and hashes support reproducibility. | Source role, question, jurisdiction, confidentiality, medium, evidence availability, and the exact standard determine applicability. None proves truth by itself. |
| Contextual conventions | Claim verdict vocabulary, citation style, TASL ordering, SPDX identifiers, notice location, archival receipt format, confidence labels, review cadence, and file-level annotation scheme. | Publisher, discipline, legal instrument, asset provider, repository, medium, locale, or counsel may mandate a different valid form. |
| Heuristics and starting points | Start with load-bearing atomic claims; follow the citation chain to origin; seek contrary evidence; prefer stable/versioned identifiers; classify use before reading rights; default unresolved consequential rights to `unverified/escalate`. | A single authoritative record may be the only evidence; safety/privacy may constrain disclosure; a lawful exception or approved contract may change the route. |
| Preferences | Footnotes versus endnotes, JSON/YAML/CSV ledger, DOI display style, visible versus consolidated credit, record field order, archive provider, and human-readable wording. | Preference never overrides exact license, source, accessibility, privacy, contract, jurisdiction, or production requirements. |
| Justified exceptions | Anonymous/confidential sources, inaccessible originals, local/private evidence copies, one-source findings, omitted voluntary CC0 credit, or use under an exception/limitation. | State the reason and authority; preserve maximum safe metadata; distinguish access from support and permission; add corroboration, counsel/editor review, expiry, or other compensation proportionate to consequence. |

### Slogans and unsupported universals to reject

- **“Primary sources are always best.”** A source is primary for a specific
  event, claim, dataset, contract, or study. It may still be self-interested,
  incomplete, wrong, superseded, or unable to establish a broader conclusion.
- **“Two sources confirm it.”** Two URLs may share one wire story, press
  release, dataset, quote, or circular citation. Independence is an origin
  property, not a link count. [SA-01]
- **“The link works, so the fact is current.”** Availability proves access to
  a representation. It does not prove semantic support, valid time, latest
  version, or authority.
- **“The citation proves the sentence.”** A citation can be real and irrelevant,
  overgeneralized, snippet-only, or contrary to the sentence.
- **“No source found means false.”** It means unsupported after the recorded
  search unless affirmative evidence contradicts the claim.
- **“The repository is MIT, so every file is MIT.”** Embedded assets,
  submodules, fixtures, fonts, screenshots, data, generated outputs, and copied
  snippets can have different or unknown terms. [SA-07, SA-08]
- **“Public,” “free,” “royalty-free,” “open access,” or “CC” means cleared.**
  Each label needs the exact instrument, item, rights holder, intended use,
  conditions, and non-copyright rights. [SA-02, SA-04]
- **“BY means write the author's name.”** CC 4.0 may also require supplied
  notices, license and source references, and an indication of modifications,
  in a reasonable medium-appropriate form. [SA-02]
- **“NC forbids anything connected to money,” “ND forbids every technical
  change,” or “SA relicenses the whole surrounding project.”** Apply the exact
  legal-code definitions to the material, adaptation, sharing, purpose, and
  compatible license; do not replace them with a mnemonic. [SA-02]
- **“CC0 removes every restriction.”** It addresses specified copyright and
  related rights; trademark, privacy, publicity, cultural responsibility,
  contract, and endorsement questions may remain. [SA-02, SA-04]
- **“Using an OFL font makes the artwork OFL.”** Ordinary use/embedding does not
  license the surrounding document, while distributing or modifying the font
  file has its own notice, naming, and package consequences. [SA-03]
- **“A DOI, commit, archive URL, or checksum freezes the truth.”** These help
  identify or detect change. They do not prove accuracy, current applicability,
  legal permission, or that the inspected object was complete. [SA-05, SA-11]
- **“SPDX or REUSE compliance clears the project.”** They can make declared
  licensing information machine-readable and coverage-testable; they do not
  prove rightsholder authority, interpretation, compatibility, or missing
  undeclared assets. [SA-07, SA-08]
- **“C2PA proves the picture is true, human-made, owned, or licensed.”** C2PA
  validates association, structure, signatures, and tamper evidence under a
  trust model. Its specification explicitly avoids judging whether assertions
  are good or bad, and provenance can be incomplete. [SA-09]
- **“Generated means copyright-free” or “the provider grants commercial use,
  therefore no rights risk exists.”** Authorship, input/reference rights,
  provider contract, disclosure, likeness, trademark, infringement, and
  jurisdiction remain separate. [SA-10]
- Fixed source counts, citation-density percentages, universal confidence
  thresholds, or one recheck interval. Consequence and volatility determine
  effort; one canonical legal code can own its terms, while a contested causal
  claim may remain unresolved after many sources.

| SA-11 | [DOI Handbook](https://www.doi.org/doi-handbook/html/), living handbook inspected 2026-09-02 | Site content CC BY 4.0 | Persistent identifier separated from mutable location/metadata; versioned identification | A DOI is not a content hash, access guarantee, latest-version decision, truth or permission. Attributed synthesis allowed. |

### Contradictions and evidence limits

- A source can be primary for its own rule, statement, measurement, or dataset
  and still need independent challenge for a broader truth claim. “Primary” is
  a role, not a universal credibility score. [SA-01]
- Exact quotes and hashes can prove that a record matches inspected bytes. They
  cannot prove the source is correct, complete, independent, current, or
  authorized. [SA-05]
- A canonical license deed aids comprehension; the legal code and exact
  asset/use govern. NC, ND, SA, database, moral, trademark, privacy/publicity,
  and warranty effects cannot be collapsed into one open/closed flag. [SA-02]
- File-level SPDX/REUSE coverage is useful deterministic evidence. It is not a
  legal opinion and does not discover every untracked, visually embedded,
  contractual, or generated ingredient. [SA-07, SA-08]
- Persistent identifiers, archives, local receipts, and checksums address
  different failure modes. None converts an old source into current authority
  or an accessible copy into permission. [SA-06, SA-11]
- C2PA can make assertions tamper-evident and validate them under a trust model;
  the standard intentionally does not decide whether the asserted history is
  good, bad, true, legal, complete, or sufficient. [SA-09]
- Fact-checking codes are professional process evidence, not proof that an
  automated model, fixed verdict taxonomy, or required source count is
  accurate for design, science, law, history, or local knowledge. [SA-01]

## 7. Applied Dos and Don’ts

| Concern | Do | Don't | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Freeze the exact claim or asset, intended use, reference time, audience, medium and consequence before searching or clearing. | Do not verify a nearby claim or decide rights before the use is known. | SA-01, SA-02; bounded professional rationale |
| Claims | Split compound claims; preserve original wording; classify fact, quote, definition, causal inference, prediction, opinion and authority-owned conclusion. | Do not give truth verdicts to preferences, predictions, or legal questions outside authority. | SA-01; specialist prior art |
| Retrieval | Register sources when retrieved; record final URL/ID, version/date, inspected scope and locator; label snippet/abstract/metadata-only inspection. | Do not cite a constructed URL, search snippet, inaccessible full text, or remembered page as inspected evidence. | SA-01; specialist prior art |
| Support | State how each evidence item supports, contradicts, qualifies, contextualizes, or fails to address the atomic claim. | Do not treat a citation, quote, official domain, or checksum as semantic entailment. | SA-01, SA-05 |
| Independence | Trace repeated claims to origin and seek the strongest relevant counterevidence; collapse mirrors and syndication. | Do not count URLs, outlets, or citations without checking common origin. | SA-01; specialist prior art |

| Generated assets | Record provider/model/version/date, terms, inputs/references, human edits/selection, output hash, disclosure and jurisdiction review; validate any C2PA manifest separately. | Do not claim generated output is owned, copyright-free, non-infringing, truthful or permissioned from provider access or C2PA alone. | SA-09, SA-10 |
| Attribution output | Generate credits/notices from verified records into the actual visible/metadata/package location; inspect legibility, completeness and change notes. | Do not leave compliance in an internal spreadsheet while the shipped artifact omits it. | SA-02, SA-03, SA-06 |
| Broken sources | Keep the operational rule local; preserve stable ID/version/receipt and dependent-claim map; mark current facts unverified until a source-equivalent replacement is inspected. | Do not silently delete a dead source, cite an archive as current, or substitute model memory. | SA-05, SA-06, SA-11; current package boundary |
| Repair | Fix the unsupported claim or incompatible asset first, then update every dependent caption, credit, export, manifest and correction event. | Do not repair citation style while preserving the false claim or unauthorized material. | L-18, SA-01, SA-02 |

## 8. Architecture recommendation

### Considered mechanisms

1. **Deepen the current single leaf.** It could become professionally complete
   and may exceed 1,800 tokens. It would still load Creative Commons, font,
   image, data, generated-asset, and package-license reasoning for a factual
   claim audit, while loading claim decomposition and source contradiction for
   a simple font-credit task. The global bibliography would continue to occupy
   most of the route without supplying the workflow.
2. **Split by the two existing independent signals.** `source_audit` selects a
   claim/evidence method; `license_attribution` selects asset/use/rights and
   credit output. They share only a small stop/provenance floor, while their
   causal failures, authoritative sources, records, authorities, and tests are
   distinct. This matches the exact-domain Skill landscape.
3. **Keep only a bibliography/stop stub and defer to web search, Research, or
   counsel.** Cheapest, but it breaks standalone use and leaves ordinary claim
   and asset records unspecified. External authority is still required for
   legal/high-stakes conclusions, not as a substitute for a local method.

### Recommendation: two directly routed flat leaves plus a non-routed source index (`P1`)

Professional coverage and correctness take precedence over token cost. The
split is justified by the already independent signals, not by the 1,800-token
target.

**A. `source-verification-and-evidence`**

- Route directly on `source_audit` for claim checking, current-source audit,
  quotation/statistic verification, evidence reconciliation and correction.
- Own `claim_registry`, `source_inspection`, `evidence_relation`,
  `current_verification`, `contradiction_record`, and `correction_history`.
- Include the claim record, actual-inspection states, origin/independence,
  valid-time/version logic, verdict calibration, runtime-offline stop behavior,
  critique/repair, and authority ceiling.
- Initial cost estimate: about 1,500–2,200 tokens; exceed it rather than remove
  a claim type, contradiction mechanism, repair path, or proof boundary.

**B. `asset-rights-and-attribution`**

- Route directly on `license_attribution` for external assets, permissions,
  license compatibility/conditions, credit compilation and package handoff.
- Own `license_boundary`, `asset_rights_record`, `intended_use`,
  `attribution_output`, `package_rights_coverage`, and `license_recheck`.
- Include exact instrument/material/use analysis and separate branches for
  images/objects, fonts, data, generated assets and embedded exceptions,
  without pretending to replace counsel.
- Initial cost estimate: about 1,900–2,800 tokens; the 1,800 figure is not a
  ceiling and the asset classes are inseparable inside one clearance record.

Both leaves repeat a small local floor: do not invent inspection, truth,
permission, rightsholder, attribution, or currentness; a link is neither
support nor permission; unresolved consequential facts/rights remain
unverified and cause narrowing, replacement, escalation, or stop. They remain
flat, direct, standalone, and never require one another.

The current 1,762-token file also acts as the distributable global source key.
That registry is maintenance/audit metadata, not a routed professional method.
W-012 should keep the full canonical ledger and rule map in `docs/research/`,
and, if public runtime-ID resolution remains a package promise, generate one
non-routed compact source index. It must not be selected as a third expert or
become a dependency edge. README/source-resolution contracts would need an
explicit coordinated change at implementation time; this audit changes none.

Expected load frequency: claim source audits are occasional and often pair
with Scribe/Research outside this standalone Design package; asset rights are
common in imagery, style, brand, data, and production tasks. Common Design
combinations are asset rights plus imagery/culture/provenance/media production,
and source verification plus data/style/culture. Both leaves should load
together only when a deliverable contains material external claims and assets.

The smallest tested non-inferior payload is unknown. No direct SOL or Terra
case exists. Test full coverage first, then remove repetition or move audit-only
bibliography out of the active route; do not compress record fields, exceptions,
repair, authority, or proof before those cases pass.

Run changed routes one open Terra High case at a time. Compare Core-only,
selected expert, and only the strongest relevant public mechanism where its
license permits local evaluation. More links, fields, or “cleared” verdicts are
not positive scores. Useful behavior is the smallest correct record, honest
stop, causal critique, complete repair, and no loss of privacy or restricted
evidence.

### Deterministic checks

- every atomic claim and external asset has one stable unique ID; parent/child
  claims and composite/ingredient assets resolve;
- every cited source ID was registered from an actual retrieval and has final
  URL/canonical identifier, version/status/date, inspected scope and locator;
- every evidence relation points to an existing claim and source; no citation
  ID or URL is hand-invented; unsupported/inaccessible states remain explicit;
- a source block generated from the ledger contains exactly the cited sources;
  structural success is not labeled semantic verification;
- origin-chain duplicate detection flags mirrored URLs, syndication and shared
  datasets/press releases for human review;
- dates separate observed/valid/effective/publication/retrieval/recheck events;
  corrections/retractions/supersessions append rather than overwrite;
- every distributable external file or snippet in the package inventory maps
  to an asset record by stable path/item ID and checksum; no orphan record is
  called coverage;
- license identifiers/expressions parse where SPDX applies; exact license text
  or `LicenseRef`/contract receipt resolves; root license and asset licenses are
  not conflated;
- intended-use fields are complete before `cleared_for_stated_use`; NC/ND/SA,
  change notice, attribution, embedding, modification, distribution and
  compatibility decisions record their exact basis;
- font file/version/hash, copyright/license notice, embedding/subsetting,
  modification/build state and Reserved Font Names are preserved where relevant;
- dataset/distribution/version, producer/publisher, provenance, transformation,
  database rights and third-party field exceptions resolve;
- generated assets record provider/model/version/date, terms receipt,
  references/inputs, output hash, disclosure and authority; C2PA validation is
  a separate field and never sets truth/permission automatically;
- compiled credits/notices in HTML, PDF, package, metadata or end credits match
  the record and remain complete, legible and accessible in the delivered form;
- broken/redirected/mutated URLs produce an availability/change event and
  dependent-claim review, never silent deletion or memory substitution;
- evidence labels distinguish retrieved, source-supported, mechanically
  covered, rights-reviewed, counsel-approved and production-shipped states.

Deterministic checks can prove schema, identity, coverage, exact-byte lineage,
declared relationships, link state, notice consistency and append-only history.
They cannot prove a source truthful, an inference valid, a source independent,
a license interpretation correct, a rightsholder authorized, an omitted asset
absent, an attribution legally sufficient in every jurisdiction, or an output
non-infringing.

### Required human/domain authority

- author/editor or qualified fact checker for claim selection, fair framing,
  semantic support, contradiction, correction and publication judgment;
- statistician, scientist, historian, journalist, local/community authority or
  other subject expert for domain claims and primary-source interpretation;
- data owner/steward for dataset definitions, lineage, version and corrections;
- rights holder/provider/commissioner for asset identity, grant, receipts and
  contract-specific facts;
- qualified IP/licensing counsel for consequential copyright, license
  compatibility, NC/ND/SA, exception/limitation, trademark, privacy/publicity,
  generated-output and jurisdiction decisions;
- font foundry/author or license specialist for unusual font modification,
  redistribution, Reserved Font Name or proprietary EULA questions;
- archivist/records owner for lawful evidence retention, privacy, authenticity,
  retention schedule and successor-source policy;
- production/access owner for notice placement, metadata survival, legibility,
  accessible reading order and final package coverage.

### Claim ceiling after passing

Passing these cases can support only that the changed leaves improved the
tested claim inspection, record completeness, rights gating, attribution,
critique, correction, runtime fallback and package reconciliation for the exact
sources, assets, uses, jurisdictions, providers, languages and reviewers. It
cannot support:

- universal factual truth, exhaustive research or absence of contrary evidence;
- automatic fact-checker, journalist, scholar, archivist, rights manager or
  lawyer equivalence;
- legal clearance, non-infringement, ownership or license compatibility in
  unreviewed jurisdictions/uses;
- that public access, CC0, open source, SPDX, REUSE, DOI, archive, checksum or
  C2PA alone proves permission, truth, authenticity, authorship or completeness;
- provider/model terms, laws, standards, licenses, URLs or asset metadata
  remaining unchanged after the recorded inspection;
- discovery of private contracts, undisclosed rightsholders, releases,
  confidential sources, culturally sensitive claims, or invisible embedded
  assets;
- installer attribution, browser/PDF/metadata survival, or receiver
  reproducibility outside the exact package and production path tested;
- lower total inference cost or non-inferiority of a compressed payload before
  the routed cases and human reviews pass.

## 10. Priority

**Overall priority: `P1`.** The current leaf preserves several high-value
binding floors and source-specific warnings, so it is not a general `P0` defect.
Its missing claim/evidence process, asset-use ledger, attribution compiler,
version/correction model, runtime split, and behavior evidence nevertheless
block the all-round designer goal.

| Priority | Gap clusters | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Atomic claim verification; actual-inspection/evidence relation; source independence/currentness/corrections; intended-use and asset-level rights; CC/font/image/data/generated branches; attribution output; version/link resilience; record/package reconciliation; causal repair | These determine whether external claims and assets can be used, corrected, credited, handed off, and honestly bounded. |
| P2 | Interoperable PROV/SPDX/REUSE export, automated origin graphs, configurable recheck scheduling, repository-wide receipt tooling, richer archive migration and provider adapters | Valuable after the P1 record and authority boundaries work across open cases. |
| P3 | Large citation-style catalogue, universal source score, automatic confidence formula, provider/license cheat sheet, or extra bibliography without a behavior gap | Such material increases maintenance and cargo-cult risk without qualifying correctness. |

Conditional escalation: a publication containing a consequential unsupported
claim, unresolved mandatory right, incompatible license, undisclosed restricted
asset, privacy/publicity harm, deceptive generated-media claim, or required
current legal/provider fact is `P0`. The Skill must remove/replace/narrow, mark
unverified, obtain authority, or stop; appearance, attribution polish, C2PA,
repository license, or a model's confidence cannot compensate.
