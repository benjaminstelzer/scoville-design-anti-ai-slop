### Current operational rules

**Generation.** Interpret the label before making; distinguish period,
ideology, production technology, composition, typography, image method,
material finish, revival, and internet shorthand. Compile purpose/context,
technology, composition, type, colour, imagery/ornament, material, and relevant
motion/sound/interaction. Select three to five load-bearing traits, a
functional counterweight, and a subject-specific move; define invariants,
variation, and cliché collapse.

**Discrimination and selection.** The leaf rejects palette swaps, font swaps,
presets and symbol bundles. It prefers a coherent lineage/logic, subject fit,
function, and whole-artifact application. It does not yet state how to choose
between contested label senses, evidence statuses, dominant and secondary
influences, incumbent brand constraints, or historical fidelity versus
declared revival.

**Critique.** It checks the whole artifact for coherent traits, medium fit and
costume-like accumulation. It lacks a record that separates factual error,
unsupported association, superficial cliché, weak structural DNA, production
anachronism, brand conflict, cultural authority, rights, and execution craft.

**Repair.** It strengthens the weakest load-bearing trait or removes an
unsupported signifier instead of adding nostalgia. This is a strong parent-
cause rule, but it has no complete failure-to-cause map or cross-medium,
accessibility, brand, rights, and cultural repair paths.

**Exception.** Familiar requested signifiers may remain when they improve
recognition or concept, and relationships may change form across media. The
leaf does not explicitly cover deliberate revival/hybridization, useful
stylistic neutrality, incumbent-brand precedence, culturally restricted
material, or cases where the correct repair is to stop and obtain authority.

**Verification.** History and cultural claims are verified when material;
historical collections require item-level rights checks. There is no required
style record, source/confidence state, artifact-rights record, representative
render set, production inspection, affected-community review, or profile-
admission evidence.

## 2. What is already strong

1. **The label is treated as ambiguous, not self-executing.** Separating
   period, ideology, technology, grammar, technique, finish, revival and
   shorthand prevents invisible blending of incompatible meanings. This agrees
   with structured-vocabulary and museum practice, where preferred/variant
   terms sit in defined scopes and histories rather than a flat mood list.
   [L-10; SD-01, SD-02]
2. **Style is compiled as a causal system.** Purpose, context, production,
   structure, type, colour, imagery, material and temporal behavior are a much
   stronger model than a palette plus motifs. [L-01, L-10]
3. **Production technology belongs in the DNA.** Screen-print limits,
   phototypesetting, chromolithography, Ben-Day dots, photocopy degradation or
   CRT behavior can explain form; this allows translation of the relation
   rather than arbitrary distress. [L-01, L-34, L-35; SD-02]
4. **Structural traits outrank decoration.** Composition, hierarchy, grid,
   density and spatial behavior can carry a lineage even when literal symbols
   disappear. This aligns with the adoption priority for hierarchy, grouping,
   negative space and subject-specific composition.
5. **Recognition and cliché are not falsely opposed.** Familiar requested
   signifiers may be useful. The relevant question is whether they work in a
   coherent, subject-specific system, not whether a familiar cue is forbidden.
6. **The counterweight and subject move resist costume.** A functional
   counterweight preserves the communication job, while a subject-specific
   relation prevents an era/movement from becoming a replaceable skin.
7. **Repair removes unsupported signs.** Adding more period symbols is not the
   default answer to weak recognizability; strengthening a causal trait or
   deleting noise is more discriminating.
8. **Cross-medium translation preserves logic, not pixels.** Translating print
   misregistration into timed channel separation or state-layer offset is a
   useful worked mechanism, provided the new medium's access and production
   constraints are tested.
9. **The atlas boundary is explicit.** No named-style lessons ship merely
   because common style prompts tested well. This matches non-decisive SOL
   ablations and prevents a thin Western list from masquerading as global
   expertise.
10. **Source and rights limits are unusually honest.** Museum context prose is
    not a reusable asset license; public-domain/CC0 status is item-specific;
    the historical *Grammar of Ornament* is evidence, not neutral cultural
    authority. [L-33, L-34, L-35]

The current leaf therefore has the right model. Its gap is applied
professional discrimination: it cannot yet leave a traceable style record,
resolve label/source conflicts, diagnose production and cultural failure,
reconcile a strong lineage with an incumbent system, or prove translation
across real formats.

## 4. Rule-quality audit

### Classification

| Class | Rules that belong here | Override condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Binding constraints | Preserve supplied facts, exact content, incumbent owner/invariants and required access; do not invent history, community authority, authenticity, rights, approval or production proof; do not use a reference asset without item-level permission/status; do not present a current revival as a period fact. | Only verified evidence or the authorized rights/brand/community/domain owner changes the constraint. |
| Evidence-bounded rules | Label senses, dates, geographies, purposes, production methods and characteristic relations must be supported by sources whose scope is stated. Museum/open-book evidence supports only the documented term/object/process, not a global style law. | Newer/specialist/community evidence may correct the record; contradictions remain visible rather than averaged. [L-01, L-10, L-33, L-34, L-35; SD-01 through SD-04] |
| Contextual conventions | Dominant lineage plus bounded secondary influence; a Style Record; mood/reference boards; vocabulary labels; revival/hybrid declarations; cross-medium mapping; a reference lock; a design-system exception record. | The brief, available evidence, incumbent system, community protocol, medium and consequence determine whether each artifact is useful. |
| Heuristics and starting hypotheses | Start with structural DNA and production cause; use a small discriminating trait set; remove an unsupported cue before adding one; test recognizability after removing the most obvious sign; adapt relationships across media; use familiar cues when they serve recognition. | A supplied mature style system, exact reproduction commission, tiny reversible task, unfamiliar living tradition, or evidence-backed exception may require another sequence. Record why. |
| Preferences | Particular labels, palettes, type families, motif density, symmetry, texture, historical fidelity, revival intensity, reference-board format and how many traits/influences are recorded. | Preference never overrides subject, content, access, rights, cultural authority, incumbent ownership, production, or the user's explicit direction. |
| Justified exceptions | Deliberate cliché, anachronism, hybrid, faithful reproduction, stylistic neutrality, one-medium optimization, unfamiliar cue, or controlled brand departure. | Declare purpose and authority; preserve the communication/access floor; identify the broken convention; add the compensating relation; expose limits; obtain rights/community/brand approval where required. |

### Slogans, cargo-cult numbers and unsupported universals to reject

- **“Use three to five style traits.”** A short trait set is a compression
  heuristic, not proof of coherence. Use the smallest set that discriminates
  this direction and carries the job; do not force a count.
- **“One counterweight and one signature.”** Add only a causal repair or
  subject relation. Some systems need none; others express the relation through
  several coordinated carriers.
- **“Minimalism means cream, serif display type and lots of whitespace.”**
  Reduction is relative to function, material, culture and content; generic
  luxury-web tokens do not establish a lineage.
- **“Brutalism means monospace, black/yellow, thick borders and terminal
  chrome.”** Architectural, print/punk, graphic and web usages differ; rawness
  cannot excuse arbitrary boxes, weak grouping or unreadable type.
- **“The 1980s means synthwave neon, VHS noise and a sunset grid.”** It is a
  period umbrella spanning materially different technologies, regions,
  subcultures and commercial languages.
- **“Neoclassical means black, gold, columns and Didone.”** Load-bearing order,
  proportion, hierarchy, rhetoric, material and historical scope must carry
  the direction; literal columns are one possible cue.
- **“A famous motif makes it recognizable.”** Recognition depends on the
  audience, label sense, cue bundle, subject and medium; one motif can create a
  stereotype or false period claim.
- **“Historic-looking means authentic.”** Visual resemblance cannot prove
  date, geography, process, community authority or faithful reproduction.
- **“Translate the look exactly across media.”** Pixel identity discards each
  medium's affordances and can reproduce defects without their cause.
- **Similarity percentages, fixed influence counts, fixed font counts,
  universal pairings, universal spacing scales, mandatory whitespace, style
  bans and unexplained numeric palettes.** These communicate preference at
  best; they are not measurable historical/style accuracy or quality.

| SD-03, [UNESCO 2003 Convention](https://ich.unesco.org/en/convention) and [Safeguarding without freezing](https://ich.unesco.org/en/safeguarding-00012) | 2003 Convention; living official explanatory pages; © UNESCO/reference-only | Communities/groups/individuals produce, maintain and recreate living heritage; recognition, participation, customary access and consent matter, and safeguarding does not freeze a “pure” form. | A heritage framework, not a universal style manual, private-rights clearance or substitute for the affected community's authority. Apply only when the requested material is actually within scope. |
| SD-04, [WIPO Traditional Cultural Expressions](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/traditional-knowledge/traditional-cultural-expressions/index) | Living official policy page; WIPO terms/reference-only; inspected 2026-09-02 | Designs, signs, symbols, performances, architecture and crafts can form community identity/heritage; copyright, trademark, GI, customary and sui-generis questions may coexist. | International protection remains legally and jurisdictionally complex, with negotiations ongoing. It cannot determine permission or give legal clearance for one asset/use. |

### Resulting learning, without an atlas

1. A label must resolve to a **sourced sense**, not a word-to-preset lookup.
   Structured vocabularies and museums are useful for aliases, scope and
   chronology; primary/community and specialist sources outrank them for a
   living or contested vernacular. [SD-01 through SD-04]
2. Style DNA should connect **purpose and production to observable form**.
   Historical associations that cannot be traced to a place, community,
   artifact, practice or production relation remain hypotheses, not rules.
3. A **dominant lineage** makes a hybrid intelligible; secondary influences
   receive bounded jobs. This is a professional synthesis heuristic, not a
   historical law or fixed count.
4. **Revival and anachronism are not inherently wrong.** They become failures
   when undeclared, falsely period-accurate, culturally deceptive, functionally
   harmful or unable to explain what has been translated and why.
5. **Recognizability and cliché require audience/context evidence.** Familiar
   cues can orient; structural DNA, subject evidence and medium craft decide
   whether the result remains more than a costume.
6. **Open access is item-specific and incomplete as permission.** Even CC0
   assets can retain third-party, trademark, privacy/publicity or cultural
   restrictions; context prose and collection landing pages are not asset
   licenses. [L-33, L-34]
7. **Living cultural expression is not an archive style pack.** Community
   recognition, maintenance, customary access and authority can make the
   appropriate action consult, narrow, attribute, seek consent, or not use the
   material. [SD-03, SD-04]

## 7. Applied Dos and Don’ts

### Operational rules

| Stage | Do | Don't | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interpret | Classify the requested label, record aliases/sense/date/place/status, expose plausible alternatives and ask only the ambiguity that would change the artifact. | Do not silently choose the currently fashionable meaning or average incompatible senses. | L-10; SD-01, SD-02 |
| Establish authority | Mark supplied, verified, inferred, contested, unknown and community-authority-required claims; name who can decide. | Do not present model familiarity, a search result, museum taxonomy or visual resemblance as fact/authenticity. | L-18, L-19; SD-03, SD-04 |
| Select lineage | Choose one dominant lineage/incumbent system; assign each secondary influence or modifier a bounded structural/content/medium job. | Do not create an equal-weight “X meets Y meets Z” bundle or fixed influence quota. | Bounded synthesis heuristic; Refero comparator |
| Compile DNA | Connect purpose/context and production cause to composition, hierarchy, spacing, type, colour, imagery/symbol/ornament, material and temporal behavior. Select only relations that discriminate the direction and serve this subject. | Do not fill every category with a trope or stop at palette, type, texture or motif. | L-01, L-10, L-35; SD-02 |
| Preserve design quality | Make hierarchy, grouping, reading order, spatial rhythm and negative space do a subject-specific job; route detailed typography/spacing to their owners and inspect real content. | Do not let a style recipe impose fixed fonts, pairings, spacing scale, whitespace amount, grid, density or decorative composition. | Adoption priority; L-01, L-14, L-24 |
| Handle recognition | Keep familiar cues when they orient the intended audience, but test whether a coherent structural bundle survives without the most obvious trope. | Do not ban clichés categorically or confuse one famous symbol with reliable recognition. | Empirical/human-review requirement; current leaf |
| Counterweight/signature | Add a counterweight only to repair a concrete functional conflict; derive any signature from verified subject content/process/object/language/data. | Do not force one twist, motif or memorable flourish into every direction. | Current leaf; subject-specificity boundary |
| Reconcile incumbent system | Preserve, extend, reinterpret or document an owner-approved exception to existing brand/design-system invariants. | Do not treat a named style as permission to replace a mature identity or accessibility token system. | L-18, L-21 |
| Translate medium | Map invariant relation to medium-native carrier, allowed change, lost affordance and equivalent; recompose for content, size, distance, interaction, motion/reduced mode and production. | Do not shrink a hero, copy pixels, or simulate a historical defect when its causal role is absent or harmful. | L-01, L-19; current cross-medium rule |
| Screen references | Use object-level title/creator/date/ID/URL/license/status and record transformations plus non-copyright caveats. | Do not download from an OA collection or moodboard and infer that the exact work, logo, person, motif or context is cleared. | L-33, L-34 |
| Handle living vernaculars | Seek affected/community authority, current terminology, customary-use constraints and consent/consultation when the request touches living identity or restricted expression. Stop or narrow when the evidence/authority is missing. | Do not universalize outsider description, freeze a living practice, or call a derivative “authentic.” | SD-03, SD-04 |
| Generate | Emit a compact Style Record, then let routed composition/type/colour/image/UI/motion/production owners implement their concerns from the same invariants. | Do not duplicate full domain curricula or let each medium invent a different historical story. | Local ownership architecture |
| Critique | Compare artifact evidence with the selected label sense, dominant lineage, causal traits, subject, brand, access, cultural and rights record; localize observations. | Do not grade “vibes,” count motifs, or condemn an exception before checking its purpose and compensation. | L-01, L-10, L-18 |
| Repair | Fix the parent cause: relabel, re-source, restructure, strengthen one causal trait, remove/replace a false cue, restore subject/brand/access, remap medium, obtain authority, or stop. Re-render the affected formats. | Do not add nostalgia, distress, ornament or more references to conceal missing structure. | Current repair rule plus audit synthesis |
| Prove | Inspect real-content outputs at representative media/conditions; retain evidence and reviewers; qualify every history, rights, access, cultural and production claim. | Do not report a prompt, style board or attractive hero as cross-medium, accessible, production-ready, historically accurate or culturally authorized. | L-14, L-17, L-19, L-33, L-34 |

## 8. Architecture recommendation

### Considered mechanisms

1. **Deepen one directly routed style compiler.** One Style Record keeps label
   interpretation, structural DNA, production causality, anti-cliché logic,
   incumbent constraints, rights/cultural state and medium translation
   coherent. It loads some interpretation fields for pure translation tasks,
   but translation still needs the same invariant/source record.
2. **Compiler plus named-style profile leaves.** A direct Minimalist,
   Brutalist, 80s, Neoclassical or regional profile can reduce repeated
   research only after a demonstrated behavior gap and full profile
   qualification. The current evidence does not admit any profile; public
   recipe Skills show the fixation, cargo-cult and Western-atlas risks.
3. **Split interpretation from cross-medium translation.** `named_style` and
   `cross_medium_style` are distinct signals, but the second concern cannot be
   applied safely without the first concern's selected sense, invariants,
   production causes, rights and cultural state. Flat independent leaves would
   duplicate that reasoning or require an impermissible hidden dependency.
4. **Retain the 539-token stub.** This best respects non-decisive ablations, but
   leaves source/confidence, anachronism, living authority, brand invariants,
   rights records, causal failure repair and cross-medium proof to implicit
   model knowledge. That blocks the all-round goal.

### Recommendation: deepen one compiler; withhold profiles (`P1`)

Retain the current direct route and extend it to the smallest professionally
sufficient **Style Record + generate/critique/repair/translate protocol**.
Exact coverage outranks the 1,800-token target; an initial complete payload is
likely about **1,800–2,700 `o200k_base` tokens**, but no size should be treated
as a target or quality measure. Compress only repetition, examples and model-
prior exposition after symmetric evidence. Do not shorten away source states,
production cause, exceptions, ownership, cultural/rights stops, repair or
proof.

Required compiler payload:

- term/language/class/aliases, selected sense and alternatives;
- source/date/status/confidence/contradiction and authority owner;
- dominant lineage/incumbent system, bounded secondary influences/modifiers;
- purpose/context and production/material cause;
- structural DNA across hierarchy, composition, spacing/negative space,
  typography/script, colour/reproduction, imagery/symbol/ornament/material and
  relevant motion/sound/interaction;
- subject-specific relation, optional functional counterweight/signature,
  recognizability/cliché hypothesis and anachronism/revival/hybrid state;
- incumbent brand/design-system invariants and approved degree of departure;
- source-item rights/cultural records and stop/escalation conditions;
- medium mappings, accessible equivalents and representative proof plan;
- observation → effect → cause → repair protocol and claim ceiling.

Core still owns ordinary subject thesis, mode, domain routing and rendering
loop. Brief/Concept owns the communication problem and concept carriers; Brand
owns durable identity/governance; Style owns named/contextual visual grammar;
Composition, Typography, Colour, Imagery, Information, UI and Motion own craft
within their domains; Media Production/provider owns technical deliverability;
Culture/Ethics, Sources/Attribution, accessibility and relevant community/domain
authorities own consequential verification.

The existing `named_style` and `cross_medium_style` signals both select this
compiler directly. Expected load frequency is occasional: common for explicit
movement/era/vernacular/retro requests and campaigns translated across media,
rare for normal system-consistent utility work. Common combinations are Style
+ Typography + Composition; Style + Imagery/Colour; Style + Brand for an
incumbent identity; Style + UI for a named web direction; and Style + Media
Production for print/motion/fabrication. Phase the task when more than two
specialist craft domains are needed and hand off only the compact Style Record.

Named profiles remain **withheld**. Admit one only when an independently
selectable exact label repeatedly exposes a compiler gap and the profile has a
sourced scope/history/production record, current drift review, gen–critique–
repair–translate rules, rights-screened examples, multiple subjects and media,
relevant cultural/historical authority, and open Terra/human evidence that it
beats the compiler without more cargo cult. The working atlas in
`style-direction-system.md` remains maintainers' audit coverage, never runtime
authority.

The smallest tested non-inferior payload is unknown. The current 539-token
stub has no isolated benefit result; the proposed complete compiler and any
later compression both require the cases below.

## 9. Tests and claim ceiling

### Smallest open Terra High falsifiers

| Case | Seed and task | Falsifies if | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1: ambiguous “80s” generation | A conference web/poster brief says only “80s,” supplies exact content, a non-synthwave subject, incumbent accessibility constraints and two rights-cleared artifacts from materially different 1980s contexts. Interpret, select and generate one direction. | It defaults to synthwave; invents historical facts; averages branches; treats cue counts as recognition; changes content/brand; ignores real typography/spacing; or calls a modern translation period-authentic. | Style Record with term branches, dominant/source decision, production causes, subject relation, rights IDs, desktop/mobile/poster renders and intended-reviewer recognition comments. |
| G2: cross-medium Neoclassical translation | Translate one sourced Neoclassical direction across an A2 poster, dense editorial spread and responsive event page while retaining an incumbent mark and without requiring columns/black/gold. | It copies layout pixels, substitutes literal motifs, breaks hierarchy/real copy, imposes fixed type pairing, loses script/access behavior, or cannot explain the invariant across media. | Same source/invariant record; composition/type/spacing decisions per medium; full-content print/screen renders; access checks; brand-owner and designer review. |
| C1: unfamiliar living vernacular critique | Read-only critique of a supplied concept that labels a living regional/Indigenous visual language “authentic,” cites a museum collection landing page, uses a culturally sensitive motif, a modern unlicensed image and an outsider-written taxonomy. | It improves style without challenging authority; infers permission from access; freezes the tradition; invents meaning; fails to stop/escalate; or gives legal/cultural clearance. | Claim/source/status trace, exact item records, community/rights unknowns, localized observations, stop/narrow/consult recommendation and preserved safe elements. |
| R1: readable Brutalist repair | Repair a supplied responsive service UI whose terminal chrome, fixed monospace, uniform boxed spacing, flicker and low contrast make tasks inaccessible, while preserving a declared web-Brutalist direction and subject-specific raw-data relation. | It neutralizes all style, adds more trope, changes task/content, retains access failure, uses fixed spacing/font recipes, or only fixes desktop. | Before/after Style Record and renders; hierarchy/grouping/typesetting review; keyboard/zoom/text-spacing/reduced-motion/contrast checks; mobile/desktop and brand regression; reviewer rationale for preserved recognition. |

Run each open case with Core-only, the smallest selected leaf set and the
coverage-complete compiler. Use a later compiler+qualified-profile comparator
only when a profile exists; never seed the evaluator with an unqualified atlas.
Score source/label accuracy, subject specificity, hierarchy/spacing/type
quality, whole-artifact coherence, medium preservation, access, rights/cultural
honesty and causal repair—not motif count, prose volume or fashionable drama.

### Deterministic checks

- the Style Record contains requested term/language, class, aliases, selected
  sense, rejected/alternative senses, dominant lineage/system, bounded
  influences/modifiers and source/status/confidence;
- all factual dates, places, purposes, technologies, communities, symbols and
  “authentic/period-accurate” claims resolve to an inspected source or remain
  explicitly unknown/contested;
- every load-bearing trait maps to purpose/context, production cause or
  evidenced lineage and an observable subject/communication job; no minimum
  trait count auto-passes;
- dominant and secondary roles do not conflict invisibly; hybrids and
  anachronisms declare what is historical, revival, translated and newly
  invented;
- exact content, language/script and incumbent mark/type/colour/access
  invariants match the supplied owner record; exceptions name authority;
- typography/spacing/negative-space decisions are relational and real-content
  based; there is no fixed family count, universal pairing, spacing scale,
  mandatory whitespace or style-derived access waiver;
- reference assets carry item ID, creator/title/date/source URL, captured
  license/status, transformation, attribution and unresolved third-party,
  cultural, privacy/publicity/trademark flags;
- living cultural material has an authority/consultation/consent/customary-
  access state; `unknown` cannot serialize as `cleared` or `authentic`;
- each medium maps invariant, native carrier, allowed variation, lost
  affordance, equivalent/compensation and evidence; assets/content are not
  silently dropped;
- critique/repair connects localized observation, communication/access effect,
  parent cause, smallest repair, preserved strength and new render/authority;
- intended-format files parse and render; supplied text/data/assets remain
  present; responsive variants have no unintended clipping/overflow; print and
  motion evidence is labeled simulated, inspected or provider-approved;
- claim language distinguishes `recognizable to tested reviewers`,
  `source-grounded`, `historically inspired`, `period-grounded`,
  `rights-screened`, `community-reviewed`, `accessible in tested conditions`
  and `production-approved`.

These checks can prove record completeness, content/invariant parity, source
links, declared lineage and influence roles, item-status capture, artifact
syntax and presence of tested variants. They cannot prove historical truth,
correct cultural interpretation, permission, authenticity, audience
recognition, aesthetic quality, accessibility in use, production behavior,
originality or that a direction is the best choice.

### Required render, production and human/domain authority

- inspect real-content output at intended size, distance, viewport, density,
  substrate/gamut and motion/reduced conditions rather than only a prompt or
  mood board;
- use a design historian/curator or specialist primary sources for consequential
  lineage, date, technology and period-fidelity claims;
- use relevant community/cultural authority and affected audiences for living
  vernacular, restricted/sacred material, terminology, customary use and
  representation;
- use native readers and script/typography specialists for multilingual/
  non-Latin hierarchy, type and writing-system behavior;
- use a communication designer/creative director for structural DNA,
  recognizability/cliché trade-offs, subject signature and causal repair;
- use the brand/design-system owner for incumbent invariants and departures;
- use accessibility specialists and relevant disabled users for consequential
  equivalent-use claims;
- use printer/fabricator/developer/motion/production owners for actual medium
  behavior and provider approval;
- use source/rights owners and qualified counsel for permission, trademark,
  publicity/privacy, traditional-cultural-expression and jurisdictional legal
  questions.

### Claim ceiling after passing

Passing the open cases can support only that the changed compiler improved the
tested label disambiguation, source trace, structural/production synthesis,
subject and brand fit, cross-medium translation, access/rights/cultural
honesty, and repair for the exact prompts, sources, languages, artifacts,
media, tools and reviewers. It cannot support:

- an exhaustive movement, period, genre, regional, vernacular, material,
  subculture or internet-aesthetic atlas;
- global history, cultural mastery, authenticity, community consent or the
  authority to use a living/restricted expression;
- one canonical DNA, cue bundle, palette, type system, motif, grid, spacing
  logic or production method for any named style;
- universal recognizability, absence of cliché, taste superiority, originality,
  non-copying, trademark safety, non-infringement or rights clearance;
- period accuracy, production fidelity, access, responsiveness or print/motion
  readiness outside inspected artifacts and named authorities;
- that a popular/high-star Skill or one attractive example improves quality;
- named-profile admission, independent evaluator agreement, business/audience
  outcome, professional equivalence or lower total inference cost;
- non-inferiority of any compressed compiler until symmetric Terra, render and
  human/domain review passes.

## 10. Priority

**Overall priority: `P1`.** The current stub is safe, causal and honestly
bounded; its lack of an atlas is a strength, not a missing feature. However,
the absent Style Record, source/confidence states, dominant/influence control,
production causality, anachronism/revival handling, incumbent invariants,
cultural/right stops, cross-medium contract and full repair/proof spine block
the all-round designer goal.

| Priority | Gap clusters | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Label/sense/source record; dominant lineage and bounded influences; historical purpose and production cause; structural DNA tied to subject; anachronism/revival/hybrid state; brand/design-system reconciliation; rights/cultural authority; medium-native translation; generation/critique/repair trace; representative proof | These determine whether a direction is intelligible, sourced, functional, transferable and repairable rather than a trope bundle. |
| P2 | Broader term/alias discovery, contradiction handling, drift/version governance, richer reviewer protocols, reference-de-fixation comparisons and provider-specific production mappings | Valuable after the P1 compiler passes across several labels, subjects, scripts and media. |
| P3 | Large movement catalogues, famous-designer emulation, fixed “style DNA” recipes, similarity scores, universal motif/palette/font/spacing tables, novelty signatures and unsourced trend taxonomies | These increase maintenance, fixation, copying and false-authority risk without closing a demonstrated outcome gap. |

Conditional escalation: a false historical/cultural claim, fabricated
authenticity/community consent, culturally restricted use, unresolved rights,
access exclusion, deceptive replica, unauthorized incumbent-brand change or
destructive production assumption is `P0` in a live consequential task. The
Skill must remove/narrow, relabel, preserve the owner, obtain authority, test,
or stop; recognizability and style fidelity cannot compensate.

### Open limits

- No historical object set, living community practice, brand system, style
  board, generated artifact, cross-medium suite, typesetting, accessibility
  session, print/fabrication proof or rights-clearance workflow was executed.
- No historian, curator, relevant community/cultural authority, native reader,
  creative director, brand owner, disabled user, producer/provider or counsel
  reviewed an output from the proposed compiler.
- The public Skill search was targeted, rate-limited and may miss private,
  non-English, newly published, differently named, registry-only or
  service-integrated Skills. Stars and repository state are a dated capture.
- E1 examples demonstrate only that examples/artifacts are inspectable; they
  are not independent evidence of historical accuracy, routing, typography,
  spacing, responsive behavior, originality, rights or professional quality.
- Getty, MoMA, museum collection records and open textbooks have institutional,
  geographic, historical and taxonomy limits. UNESCO/WIPO frameworks are not
  item permission, legal advice or substitutes for community authority.
- No named profile is recommended from this audit. A working coverage map may
  guide future research, but no atlas should ship without profile-specific
  sources, rights/cultural review and generation/critique/repair/translation
  evidence.
