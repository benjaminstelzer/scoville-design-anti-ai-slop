# Corporate design, communication design, and expert-boundary research

Date: 2026-09-02  
Status: W-014 research input; no runtime rule is changed by this document

## Research question

Determine whether the successor has executable professional depth for:

1. creating and governing a corporate/visual identity;
2. conforming to an incumbent mandatory identity;
3. making visual communication findable, understandable, and actionable;
4. assigning responsibility where design disciplines overlap; and
5. routing only the expert knowledge that can change a task.

Public Skills were inspected for mechanisms and evidence, not treated as
authorities on design. Repository stars below were sampled on 2026-09-02 and
belong to the whole repository, never to one Skill. Public availability is not
permission to copy prose, assets, examples, marks, or manuals.

## Direct findings

- [`brand-and-visual-systems.md`](../../references/brand-and-visual-systems.md)
  already owns much of visual identity: thesis, invariants, controlled
  variation, marks/lockups, icon families, templates, campaign inheritance,
  asset governance, migration, and lifecycle. Corporate design is therefore
  **under-signalled and incomplete, not absent**.
- The Brand expert lacks a complete incumbent-CI intake, precedence and
  conformance procedure; formal brand architecture; contradiction handling;
  and an auditable rule-to-output compliance record.
- Standalone logo and identity-mark creation was intentionally bounded in Audit
  0002. That is incompatible with the expanded all-round identity-design goal.
- Communication design is the umbrella purpose of visual communication, not a
  clean universal route. Brief, Concept, Composition, Typography, Imagery,
  Information, UI, Brand and media experts already own different causal parts.
  A generic always-selected Communication expert would duplicate them.
- The package still lacks five independently routable practices:
  instructional/explanatory design, advertising/campaign art direction,
  identity-mark design, packaging graphics/SKU systems, and physical
  wayfinding/environmental graphics.
- Overlap should be soft for observation and effect, but hard for canonical
  decisions, mutation rights, authority, and proof claims. One parent cause has
  one owner; other experts receive a compact boundary record.

## Working definitions

The literature uses `corporate visual identity`, `visual brand identity`,
`visual identity`, and `corporate design` inconsistently. The package needs an
operational vocabulary rather than pretending the terms are settled. Gregersen
and Johansen's [integrative review](https://pure.au.dk/portal/en/publications/organizational-level-visual-identity-an-integrative-literature-re/)
documents this terminological fragmentation.

- **Corporate identity:** the organisation's approved purpose, strategy,
  behaviour, culture and communications. Design consumes this as authorised
  input; it does not infer it from mood adjectives.
- **Corporate design:** the governed visual expression system through which an
  organisation identifies itself across media and touchpoints.
- **Visual/brand identity:** the visual and, when applicable, temporal or
  sensory identity of an organisation, product, service, place or campaign.
- **Brand image/reputation:** what audiences actually perceive. A coherent
  design does not prove this.
- **Communication design:** designing how a medium constructs meaning for
  particular people and situations. ICoD places semiotics, typography, layout,
  information hierarchy and perception inside a broad visual-communication
  profession spanning publications, web/apps, wayfinding, illustration,
  motion, identity and strategy ([ICoD design disciplines](https://www.theicod.org/en/professional-design/what-is-a-designer/design-disciplines)).
- **Information/data design:** the truthful organisation and encoding of
  information or data for a question and task. It is a constituent of
  communication design, not its synonym.
- **Content design:** planning and structuring content around evidenced user
  needs. Exact wording and editorial transformation remain Scribe's authority;
  visual role, order, allocation and pressure remain Design concerns.

## Corporate design and CI rule clusters

### Identity operation and canonical mode

The package still resolves exactly one canonical mode: `generate`, `critique`,
`repair`, or `style-direction`. Brand work records a separate operation and
incumbent relation; neither creates a fifth canonical mode.

Resolve one primary identity operation when Brand is selected:

- `create`: no applicable identity exists, or redesign is authorised;
- `apply`: use an incumbent identity without redefining it;
- `audit`: compare artifacts with the current authoritative identity without
  silently editing either;
- `evolve`: preserve, retire or migrate equity under explicit authority;
- `document`: encode an already accepted system without inventing missing
  design decisions.

Record `incumbent relation: conform | authorised change | no applicable
incumbent | unresolved`. `No applicable incumbent` requires an explicit owner
statement or a documented search of the supplied/current authority sources; it
is never inferred from missing files. `Unresolved` covers unknown currency,
authority, scope or equal-rank conflict and blocks non-provisional identity
mutation. `Conform` is not a weaker creative mode. It can govern `generate`,
`critique`, `repair`, or `style-direction`; the open design space exists only
inside areas the CI leaves open. A visually preferable deviation is still a
defect when the governing identity forbids it and no exception was authorised.

Mapping: `create` uses `generate` or a bounded `style-direction`; `apply` uses
`generate` or `repair`; `audit` uses read-only `critique`; `evolve` uses
authorised `generate`, `repair`, or `style-direction`; and `document` uses
`generate` or `repair` while preserving already accepted decisions. Validators
must reject an operation reported as a canonical mode.

### Authority and precedence

Create a source record for each applicable rule:

`source/version/location | authority | scope | mandatory/recommended/open |
invariant | allowed variation | forbidden mutation | exception owner/expiry |
proof`.

Use the task's actual authority, not a universal hierarchy. A useful default
inspection order is current owner instruction, current approved manual,
canonical assets/templates, design tokens or libraries, approved applications,
observed production, and finally inference. A lower source cannot silently
override a higher one. Equal-rank contradictions remain explicit and go to the
named owner.

Official systems demonstrate why exact scope matters:

- Germany describes its [Corporate Design](https://styleguide.bundesregierung.gov.de/)
  as the binding cross-media expression of the federal government's Corporate
  Identity and provides base elements, medium guidance, assets and tools.
- Canada's current [Federal Identity Program Design Standard](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/government-communications/design-standard.html)
  separates core symbols, colour and typography from product-specific
  application rules and names a coordinator route.
- The [European Commission visual identity](https://commission.europa.eu/resources/european-commission-visual-identity_en)
  distinguishes logo variants, media colour spaces, languages, permission and
  endorsement risk.
- ONS publishes a cross-medium [brand guide](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/brand-guidelines),
  requires canonical logo files, and prevents unauthorised subbrands.
- IBM separates the high-level Brand Center, IBM Design Language and Carbon;
  its [current FAQ](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/help/faq/) shows that
  brand definition, cross-medium expression and product implementation are
  related but different authorities.

### Create a system, not a sample board

Greenfield work requires approved purpose, positioning, audience/context,
name/verbal inputs, existing equity, differentiation need, touchpoints,
languages, rights, access, production and rollout constraints. Convert them
into one identity thesis, then define:

- recognition invariants and their priority;
- controlled variables and valid ranges;
- forbidden mutations;
- identity marks and lockups;
- type, colour, imagery, illustration, graphic-device, pattern, icon,
  data/diagram, motion and optional sound roles;
- composition, spacing and proportional grammar;
- compact, monochrome, reversed, small, dense, localised and missing-resource
  states;
- canonical assets, templates, tokens, owners, versions and deprecation;
- application mappings rather than one hero mockup copied into every ratio.

Alina Wheeler and Rob Meyerson's copyrighted *Designing Brand Identity*, sixth
edition (ISBN `9781119984818`), is a broad recurring process reference selected
for research, strategy, execution, launch and governance. Wally Olins' copyrighted
[*The Brand Handbook*](https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/wally-olins-the-brand-handbook)
covers core idea, architecture, programme, control and maintenance. Neither
book may be bundled or paraphrased as if it supplied universal recipes.

### Brand architecture and partnerships

Record each entity, audience-facing role, relation to the parent, naming
authority, identity freedom, endorsement, partner weight, touchpoints,
migration and exception. `Branded house`, `endorsed`, `subbrand`, `house of
brands` and `hybrid` are analysis options, not templates. Utrecht University's
[binding architecture](https://www.uu.nl/en/organisation/corporate-identity/brand-policy/brand-architecture)
shows how a master brand can prohibit unit logos while allowing controlled
sender blocks and content expression. Architecture must prevent reflexive new
logos, not generate one for every organisational label.

### Compliance proof

Combine deterministic and visual checks. Deterministic candidates include
foreign colours/tokens, wrong font files or weights, wrong asset hashes,
distorted aspect ratios, missing mandatory variants, exact name/credit failures
and brand-specific numeric rules. Visual judgment still owns hierarchy,
optical clear space, image language, proportional coherence and controlled
variation. Every finding cites the actual CI rule, version and locator. Do not
collapse them into an unexplained total brand score.

## Logo and identity-mark craft gap

The current Brand expert defines configurations and stress contexts but not a
professional mark-development loop. A separable identity-mark expert should
own only identity marks: wordmarks, symbols, monograms, emblems, combination
marks, responsive mark families and their optical variants. It should:

1. derive the mark's identification job from an approved name and identity
   thesis;
2. compare materially different formal mechanisms before polish;
3. judge figure/ground, silhouette, counters, joins, direction, weight,
   proportion, custom letter relations and optical correction;
4. test one-colour/reversed, small/large, low-resolution, stamp, embroidery,
   engraving and dense partnership contexts only when relevant;
5. distinguish a responsive variant from an unauthorised redraw;
6. preserve canonical assets when the incumbent relation is `conform`; and
7. bound distinctiveness to the inspected comparison field while Rights,
   Culture and counsel retain trademark, confusion and permission decisions.

No universal clear-space multiple, construction grid, minimum pixel size,
golden ratio, simplicity rule or concept count follows. Henderson and Cote's
logo research and later complexity/exposure work already in
`SRC-BRAND-EVIDENCE` show why recognition and attitude claims are task,
population, familiarity and exposure dependent.

## Communication-design contract

The Core needs only a compact record:

`communication job | sender/authority | audience/context/prior knowledge |
required proposition or interpretation | desired action/retention | evidence |
primary modalities and their relation | plausible misreading/consequence |
protected content | proof target | unknowns`.

Jobs can include identify, inform, explain, instruct, warn, orient, compare,
persuade, remind, or create an authorised expressive/affiliative effect. The
classification changes decisions; it is not a checklist every artifact must
satisfy.

Brief owns supplied outcome, audience/context, content, authority, constraints,
gates and success criteria. Concept owns subject-specific organising thesis,
meaning construction and materially different carrier mechanisms. Craft owners
execute. The communication contract prevents each expert from re-inventing the
message.

### Message clarity

- Separate main message, necessary context/evidence, action, prerequisites,
  conditions, risk and background.
- Order by the audience's task and exposure, not the sender's organisational
  chart.
- Allocate exact or nuanced material to text; concrete objects, actions,
  spatial relationships and visible comparisons may benefit from images or
  diagrams. No modality is inherently clearer.
- Specify whether text and image complement, identify, explain, constrain,
  contrast or intentionally repeat. Adjacency implies a relationship; make the
  intended one perceivable.
- Record denotation, likely connotation, required learned code, rhetorical
  relation and plausible counter-reading for consequential signs.
- Do not treat attention as comprehension, recall, agreement or action.
- Do not report cognitive load from visual intuition. Localise unnecessary
  search, split attention, competing entry points, inconsistent terms,
  unlabelled relations or transient dependencies and state the missing user
  evidence.

The [CDC Clear Communication Index](https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/pdf/clear-communication-user-guide.pdf)
is a research-based public-communication tool covering main message, action,
language, information design, state of knowledge, numbers and risk. It is
useful as scoped evidence, not a universal aesthetic score. W3C's
[*Making Content Usable*](https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/) supports clear
structure, chunks, images and instructions for cognitive accessibility but is
a Working Group Note, not WCAG conformance. ISO 24495-1:2023 is a copyrighted
plain-language standard limited primarily to text; Scribe retains wording.

### Instructional and explanatory design

This is independently routable when a visual artifact must teach a concept,
correct a misconception, explain a mechanism or support procedure execution.
Own the explanation objective, prior-knowledge/misconception frame,
visual-verbal coordination, sequence, guidance, cognitive-load causes and
comprehension proof boundary. Do not own curricula, source truth, long-form
wording, UI mechanics or LMS implementation.

Relevant methods include contextual coherence, signalling, spatial/temporal
contiguity, segmenting and prior-term preparation. They are evidence-bounded;
expertise and task can reverse their effect. Instructions additionally need
preconditions, action order, referent identity, decision paths, check states,
warnings before hazardous actions, recovery and completion. ISO 9186 separates
symbol comprehension from perceptual element identification and explicitly
allows supplementary text when a reliable symbol is unavailable. IEC/IEEE
82079-1, ISO 3864 and ANSI Z535 remain copyrighted, scope-specific current
standards and must be checked rather than reconstructed from memory.

### Advertising and campaign design

This is independently routable when a persuasive visual campaign, key visual,
advertisement or cross-placement creative system is open. University art-
direction programmes separate this practice from brand strategy, copywriting
and media planning. The leaf should therefore be named
`advertising-and-campaign-art-direction`, not the broader and more ambiguous
`advertising-and-campaign-design`.

It owns the visual creative platform, proposition-to-visual relation, visual
rhetoric, functional image-copy relation, brand/message linkage, campaign
invariants and controlled placement-native variants. Brand preserves identity;
Scribe owns wording; marketing/strategy supplies objective, audience evidence,
offer, positioning and KPI; media buying owns targeting, budget and
attribution; Source, legal and current platform authorities own claims,
clearance and policies. Conversion is never promised from a design render.

The working test chain is `entry -> ad recognition -> brand/product
identification -> message/offer takeout -> action/disclosure`. Attention is a
gate, not success. Pieters and Wedel's print-ad eye-tracking work supports
separating pictorial, text and brand attention but cannot establish persuasion
([DOI 10.1509/jmkg.68.2.36.27794](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkg.68.2.36.27794)).
Pieters, Wedel and Batra distinguish harmful feature clutter from potentially
useful structured design complexity, which rejects a universal "simpler is
better" rule ([DOI 10.1509/jmkg.74.5.048](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkg.74.5.048)).
Rosengren et al.'s meta-analysis supports evaluating creativity as originality
plus appropriateness and warns that ad-response effects are stronger than
brand-response and sales evidence
([DOI 10.1177/0022242920929288](https://doi.org/10.1177/0022242920929288)).

Creative variants must preserve a platform's relationship while recomposing
for real placement, exposure time, crop, sequence, sound-off/motion-off,
safe-area and accessibility conditions. They are not blind crops of one hero.
FTC whole-impression and native-ad guidance, the 2024 ICC Code, current UK CAP
rules and applicable EU/German authorities are release-time constraints, not
timeless aesthetic rules. The agent must stop when a dominant visual implies an
unsupported result or a disclosure attempts to reverse a misleading overall
impression.

Professional references are Robin Landa's copyrighted *Advertising by Design*
(4th edition), Pete Barry's copyrighted *The Advertising Concept Book* (3rd
edition), and current public curricula from Temple, ArtCenter and UAB. Open
process material includes USG's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 *Introduction to Advertising*,
BCcampus/KPU's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 *Open Guide to Integrated Marketing
Communications*, and the older CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 *Launch!*. Open process material
is marketing-heavy and insufficient by itself for art-direction craft.

### Packaging graphics and SKU systems

Packaging on an authoritative supplied dieline is not merely Fixed Media. It
adds a distinct causal system: a flat print surface becomes a three-dimensional
object with panels, folds, seams, opening and handling states; product, brand,
variant, quantity and claim hierarchies must survive both assembled views and a
SKU family; shelf, distance, thumbnail and e-commerce derivatives create
separate recognition conditions.

Admit `packaging-graphics-and-sku-systems`. It owns graphic mapping onto an
authoritative dieline, panel/face communication, pack information hierarchy,
SKU invariants and controlled variables, shelf/thumbnail intent, packaging-
specific e-commerce derivative intent, folded mockup proof and causal graphic
repair. It does **not** own dieline construction/tolerances, material/barrier/
closure/protection engineering, mandatory legal content, barcode data or
symbology, scan certification, sustainability truth, colour profile/trapping/
preflight/provider acceptance, or brand strategy.

The proof ladder separates authoritative geometry, flat artwork, assembled
orientation, primary/secondary/opening views, an identical-context SKU lineup,
relevant shelf/thumbnail/e-commerce diagnostics, physical mockup/proof, and
Production/regulatory/barcode acceptance. A polished 3D render proves none of
the latter three.

Standard references are the copyrighted books *Packaging Design* by Klimchuk
and Krasovec, *Package Design Workbook* by DuPuis and Silva, and *Packaging the
Brand* by Ambrose and Harris. Current GS1 General Specifications, 2D retail,
Mobile Ready Hero Images and Product Image Specification govern particular
barcode and digital-product contexts and must be rechecked live. EU food and
FDA labelling illustrate why required-content geometry is jurisdiction- and
category-specific rather than a universal front/back recipe. Orquin et al.
([DOI 10.1016/j.jbusres.2019.01.043](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2019.01.043))
and Clement et al.
([DOI 10.1016/j.jretconser.2013.01.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretconser.2013.01.003))
support contextual attention testing while rejecting an invented universal
"three-second" or "five-foot" law.

### Physical wayfinding and signage systems

Admit `physical-wayfinding-and-signage-systems`. Fixed surfaces, UI navigation
and cartography supply parts, but none owns the physical journey through a
built environment: destination hierarchy, decision points, approved-label
structure, sign-family roles, spatial cues, route confirmation, location
schedule, on-site prototyping and post-occupancy evaluation.

The leaf begins with people, tasks, origin, destination, familiarity, stress,
mobility/sensory/cognitive access needs and the environment. It inspects paths,
nodes, levels, entrances, landmarks, sight barriers, light and clutter before
adding signs. It owns the route model, destination-registry structure and
application of site/domain-owner-approved labels, macro-to-micro hierarchy,
decision-point information, orientation/direction/
confirmation/identification/directory relationships, map-to-place consistency,
multimodal wayfinding intent, route prototypes and maintenance/content
governance.

It does not own architecture, landscape or transport planning; regulated
escape/safety signage; statutory traffic signs; structural/electrical mounting;
photometric calculation; GIS/survey truth; destination naming authority;
translation; medical/legal naming; accessibility certification; or final
fabrication acceptance. Applicable
standards and qualified specialists remain mandatory.

The selected public basis combines ISO 28564 parts 1–4, ISO 7001, ISO 9186,
ISO 21542 and jurisdictional access rules with publicly accessible system manuals:
the NHS Wayfinding guide, Queensland Health principles, TfL Legible London,
SEGD Hablamos Juntos/Universal Symbols and evaluation framework. Copyrighted
standard works by Arthur/Passini, Calori/Vanden-Eynden, Gibson, Mollerup and
Lynch provide professional structure but cannot be bundled. O'Neill's built-
environment study ([DOI 10.1177/0013916591235002](https://doi.org/10.1177/0013916591235002))
and later healthcare/spatial-cognition reviews support environment-first and
route-test logic; their population and building biases prevent universal
numeric prescriptions.

No inspected public Agent Skill demonstrates physical wayfinding ownership.
Most use "wayfinding" as a synonym for website or app navigation. This absence
is not proof of novelty; it is evidence that route names need strong negative
cases.

## Expert boundaries: hard centre, soft edge

Sanders described information and graphic design as related parallel processes
inside communication design ([DOI 10.1177/154193128603001104](https://doi.org/10.1177/154193128603001104)).
ICoD likewise describes broad overlap. Erasing boundaries, however, creates
conflicting local meanings: [design as an interactive boundary object](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41469-020-00085-w)
can coordinate disciplines while also fragmenting them when each practice uses
the same word differently.

Star and Griesemer define boundary objects as adaptable to different viewpoints
while robust enough to retain an identity across them
([DOI 10.1177/030631289019003001](https://doi.org/10.1177/030631289019003001)).
Carlile's product-development study treats specialised knowledge as both an
innovation source and a cross-functional barrier and uses boundary objects to
represent, learn and transform knowledge
([DOI 10.1287/orsc.13.4.442.2953](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.13.4.442.2953)).
Design representations may also remain deliberately incomplete epistemic
objects while a team learns, then become controlled technical objects later
([Ewenstein and Whyte](https://doi.org/10.1177/0170840608083014)). Iterative
prototypes can perform the same coordinating role while retaining distinct
propositional, material, mechanical, experiential and behavioural purposes
([Heiss, DOI 10.1080/14606925.2020.1824412](https://doi.org/10.1080/14606925.2020.1824412)).
Research on design-system practice similarly reports a necessary tension
between stable organisational knowledge and product-specific flexibility; it
also finds component behaviour, documentation, governance and evolution to be
different concerns rather than one undifferentiated activity
([Lamine and Cheng](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10713)).

These sources do not prescribe this Skill's module list. The following is the
project's explicit architecture synthesis and must remain falsifiable:

| Boundary decision | Split into a specialist when… | Keep a soft overlap when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent cause | the task has a causal decision no existing leaf may safely change | another leaf only observes a consequence of the same decision |
| Routability | positive and near-neighbour negative prompts select it without a topic-word shortcut | it would load on most visual work or only restate a universal lens |
| Artifact/state | it owns a distinct record, invariant set or transformation | a compact owner-attributed field is enough to coordinate |
| Proof | it needs a distinct render, context, test or receiver to justify claims | it may flag risk but cannot make the specialist's claim |
| Stop rule | it must block a materially different qualified profession or authority | shared craft judgment can remain reversible and local |
| Token value | loaded rules materially change generation, diagnosis or repair | model priors plus a short focus instruction are sufficient |

Edges remain deliberately soft for perception and diagnosis because type,
space, image, meaning, medium and interaction affect one another. Centres remain
hard for canonical mutation, authority and proof. Soft overlap never grants a
second owner. The non-owning communication record carries field-level owner and
status. Observers append attributed observations or conflicts only; the
canonical field owner alone changes its decision or status. A governing source-
version change invalidates the affected projection until that owner rechecks
it, and an unresolved conflict propagates as unresolved rather than being
silently collapsed. The record cannot resolve disagreement or mutate a source
decision.

Applied to the Skill:

- any expert may observe a cross-domain effect;
- only the canonical owner changes its rule or parent cause;
- a valid local rule violation is repaired by its craft/medium owner;
- a faulty or contradictory system rule returns to the system owner;
- authority, rights, facts, standards and proof labels are hard boundaries;
- early roughs may preserve competing interpretations; production artifacts
  need fixed owner, version, constraints and acceptance state;
- one compact Design record is the boundary object; sibling files never become
  runtime dependencies.

Minimum handoff:

`concern | canonical owner | decision | intended effect |
authority/source/version | preserved constraints | allowed variation |
forbidden mutation | exception/owner/expiry | validation target |
evidence status | unknowns`.

CI adds `rule locator/scope/precedence`; marks add `canonical
asset/configuration/minimum-detail context`; communication adds `intended
interpretation/action/likely misreading`; production adds artifact/dependency
hashes and receiver condition.

## Spark routing proposal

GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark can be useful only as an optional text-only routing floor.
OpenAI currently describes it as a smaller, ultra-fast, 128k, text-only
research-preview model with a separate rate limit; pricing is not final
([official announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-3-codex-spark/),
[current rate card](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001415-chatgpt-rate-card-enterprise-token-based-pricing)).

Use at most a small disjoint route set: identity operation `create` with
incumbent relation `conform`, logo versus logo
placement, communication versus copy-only, instruction versus generic diagram,
campaign versus media-buying, Design/UI composition and production-only. Do not
use Spark for image input, visual judgment, artifact qualification or as proof
that a more capable model will pass. Cross-model transfer is empirical, not a
monotonic guarantee. Exact Terra High remains the behavioural qualification
target unless the user explicitly changes it.
