# Imagery and art-direction baseline

Date: 2026-09-01  
Last source verification: 2026-09-02  
Status: source-grounded pre-implementation baseline  
Related route: `imagery-and-art-direction`

## Claim boundary

The evidence supports a practical baseline for directing, selecting,
sequencing, generating, critiquing, repairing, documenting, and preparing
imagery. It does not establish expert-equivalent art direction, global rights
certainty, guaranteed multi-image consistency, or supplier-independent output
thresholds. Those remain tool-, jurisdiction-, medium-, and reviewer-dependent.

## Decision system

### Required inputs

1. objective, message, audience, desired response, and truth status
   (`documentary`, `representative`, `illustrative`, or `fictional`);
2. channel, size, aspect ratio, crop behavior, clear space, and actual supplier
   or platform specification where production readiness is claimed;
3. brand/system invariants, accepted references, locked attributes, and allowed
   variation;
4. source, creator, license/commission, releases/consent, modification and
   credit requirements;
5. contextual image role, adjacent content, and accessibility equivalent;
6. prohibited IP, representation risk, and generator/provider where relevant.

Unknown truth status, material rights, or output specification limits the work
to concept-only rather than silently implying publishability.

### Direction spec

Define the image's communicative role; subject/action/context;
realism/abstraction; framing and focal point; scale, depth, perspective, and
light; palette and tonal range; texture/mark-making; density and negative
space; crop-safe zones; and fixed versus variable properties across the set.

For a sequence, give each image a distinct job and inspect the entire contact
sheet. Consistency comes from a small set of declared invariants and controlled
variation, not from repeating “same vibe.” Remove unintentional redundancy and
vary adjacent scale, viewpoint, or density only when the sequence remains one
system.

## Application loop

1. **Frame:** determine what the image must do and whether it could be read as
   factual evidence.
2. **Direct:** translate style into non-identifying formal attributes rather
   than a living artist's identity.
3. **Acquire:** choose lawful source, commission, capture, illustration, or
   generation; rights/provenance gate production.
4. **Generate:** separate `change` from `preserve`; use an accepted master or
   reference where permitted; record tool/model, inputs, and edits.
5. **Discriminate:** check rights, truth, brief, accessibility path, and
   reproduction first; then semantic fit, focal clarity, distinctiveness, crop
   resilience, system coherence, representation, and sequence utility.
6. **Critique:** return observation → affected brief/system clause → impact →
   severity → smallest repair → preserve list → verification view.
7. **Repair:** change one failing axis where possible, avoid rerolling accepted
   content, and recheck the asset, all required crops, and the whole set.
8. **Deliver:** provide masters/variants plus provenance, credits,
   accessibility decision, disclosure state, and actual preflight limits.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Do not direct generation “in the style of” a living artist. Extract medium,
  era, geometry, line, texture, palette, light, composition, and emotional
  register without the person's identity.
- A reference image needs permission for the intended reference/edit workflow.
- Provider terms do not replace an asset-specific rights and jurisdiction
  check.
- Preserve Content Credentials/C2PA when available and maintain a separate
  asset ledger because metadata may disappear. C2PA can support provenance and
  tamper evidence; it does not prove truth or visual quality.
- Do not fabricate or materially alter documentary content while presenting it
  as captured fact.
- Alternative text or a longer equivalent follows contextual purpose, not a
  pixel inventory; decorative imagery is explicitly marked as decorative.
- Shared prompts do not prove set consistency. Inspect the full set.
- Reproduction requirements come from target medium and supplier, not a
  universal “300 DPI/CMYK” recipe.

## Principal sources and limits

- [BCcampus concept development](https://opentextbc.ca/graphicdesign/chapter/2-5-concept-development/),
  composition, colour-management, and preflight chapters: CC BY 4.0 except
  noted embedded material; directly reusable backbone with attribution.
- [MIT OCW camera and light course](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-10-001-making-science-and-engineering-pictures-a-practical-guide-to-presenting-your-work-spring-2016/):
  CC BY-NC-SA; reference-only for an unrestricted commercial Skill.
- [W3C responsive-image use cases](https://www.w3.org/TR/respimg-usecases/),
  [WAI images tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/), and WCAG:
  normative/informative web scope under W3C terms; use as standards reference.
- [OpenAI image prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide)
  and image API guide: useful current reference/edit workflow; model behavior
  and parameters are volatile and require current verification.
- [C2PA 2.4](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/C2PA_Specification.html):
  CC BY 4.0 provenance specification; not a truth oracle.
- [U.S. Copyright Office AI copyrightability report](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf),
  EU AI Act/Commission guidance, provider terms, and professional-body ethics:
  current jurisdictional/contractual reference only, never global legal advice.
- [IBM photography](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/photography/tips-and-techniques/)
  and [illustration](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/illustration/tips-and-techniques/)
  are brand-specific worked examples, not universal rules.
- [NPPA 2026 visual-integrity rules](https://bop.nppa.org/2026/technical-guidelines/)
  and [picture-editing criteria](https://bop.nppa.org/2026/picture-editing/)
  ground documentary integrity and sequence judgment only within journalistic
  scope.
- [AOI commissioning guidance](https://theaoi.com/resources/professional-practice/guide-to-commissioning/),
  [AIGA Design Business and Ethics](https://www.aiga.org/sites/default/files/2021-03/Design-Business-and-Ethics.pdf),
  and current professional codes are reference-only for brief, permission,
  commissioning, and imitation boundaries.
- [ICC](https://www.color.org/specifications/) and
  [Ghent Workgroup](https://gwg.org/technical-specifications/) specifications
  provide current production authority. The actual medium, supplier, and
  target standard still govern.

## Required qualification

Before the route is advertised above source-grounded baseline, run at least 12
blinded cases across photography, illustration, multi-image campaign, factual
imagery, responsive crops, print delivery, character/product consistency,
accessibility, rights/provenance, and surgical repair. Compare no-Skill and
module-assisted SOL on brief compliance, fatal-gate misses, focal/crop
integrity, three-or-more-asset consistency, critique-to-repair success,
provenance completeness, accessibility correctness, and reproduction fitness.
