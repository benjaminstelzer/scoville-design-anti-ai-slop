## Decision

The three qualifying repositories are `pbakaus/impeccable`,
`sickn33/agentic-awesome-skills`, and `jakubkrehel/skills`. Impeccable has the
strongest combined assessment, detector and test topology. The mirrored
`design-spatial` Skill has a useful annotated-measurement mechanism, but also
the most dangerous pseudo-objective geometry and universal gates. Better
Layout provides concrete web stress and repair examples, but no renders or
tests.

The current Scoville reference and audit remain stronger on cross-media
ownership, content-derived grids, negative-space failure modes, deliberate
exceptions, multi-page continuity, crop/trim/bleed authority, print proof,
real-content extremes and smallest-parent-cause repair. Candidate mechanisms
worth synthesizing are a manual assessment before a mechanical scan, annotated
geometry as a pointer rather than verdict, explicit tight-versus-generous
rhythm, content-driven breakpoints, pseudo-localization, RTL mirroring and a
clear `Not verified` state. No ranked candidate visibly proves better general
composition across several subjects and media.

## Candidate 1: Impeccable `layout`

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

The exact command preserves the incumbent visual world and switches emphasis
by visitor mode. It runs a manual layout assessment before a detector scan,
then records primary path, grouping, hierarchy, density, spacing rhythm and
adaptation behavior before editing. It asks whether repeated cards, columns or
sections are meaningfully equivalent, distinguishes tight and generous
intervals, tests long content and states, and requires rendered or source
evidence after repair.

### What is better than the current Scoville reference

- The two isolated assessments are a concrete way to keep detector output from
  anchoring compositional judgment.
- The spatial thesis makes primary path, groups, lead/support relation,
  density, rhythm and adaptation explicit before coordinates.
- “Is one spacing value repeated until everything has equal weight?” is a
  useful diagnostic for monotonous spacing.
- The command distinguishes Persuade and Experience from Operate and Read,
  which makes expressive versus stable layout an explicit context decision.
- Evidence must answer each verification item rather than return a bare pass.

The current Scoville reference already has relations before coordinates,
content-led grids, negative-space functions, intended-size and thumbnail
inspection, deliberate exceptions and parent-cause repair. The candidate adds
a stronger evidence sequence and a compact monotony/density interrogation.

### Adoption-priority result

The landing-demo source shows a clear desktop hierarchy, large breathing room,
grouped sections and one three-column card block. The exact file has no media
or container query, so it does not prove responsive recomposition. It is one
generic marketing subject and does not establish fixed-media, editorial
sequence, print, negative-space causality or subject-specific composition.
The test suite mainly proves detectors and infrastructure, not whether human
reviewers prefer or understand the resulting composition.

### Reject or re-verify

- Do not turn the suggested 4-unit spacing base, fixed role ranges, live
  density scale or fixed reading measures into Scoville rules.
- Repetition, cards and predictable structure are contextual. They are not
  defects merely because another mode permits expression.
- Detector coverage is not a visual-quality score. Trend bans and named-font
  rules in the wider package must not enter composition ownership.
- The exact command is web/product focused and has no multi-page, spread,
  binding, crop, trim, bleed or supplier-proof contract.

## Candidate 2: mirrored `design-spatial`

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

The Skill requires rendered screenshots, a fresh critique and a second render.
Its strongest section separates deterministic gates, likely defects and
aesthetic signals. `layout-audit.js` returns JSON and draws an SVG overlay on
the live page. The overlay makes the next screenshot show exactly where the
algorithm found a collision, contrast issue, small target, overflow, alignment
near-miss, spacing outlier or balance offset. The instruction says the human or
model must look at the annotation and can overrule intentional collisions and
irregular rhythm.

The repository is an aggregation mirror. Its exact Skill frontmatter names
`connerkward/ckw-design-skill` as source. Repository stars therefore measure
the broad catalog, not independent adoption of this mechanism.

### What is better than the current Scoville reference

- Annotated overlays localize a metric in the actual render instead of leaving
  an abstract JSON count.
- The gate, review and signal distinction is a useful evidence taxonomy.
- The Skill explicitly warns that alignment, balance and spacing metrics can
  flatten expressive work.
- Rendering at several widths and reinspection after repair fit the current
  Scoville proof floor.
- The task-order walk can expose an otherwise aligned layout that forces
  needless visual or pointer travel.

### Adoption-priority result

The deterministic script is inspectable, but no included annotated screenshot,
fixture result, multiple subject set or independent human evaluation was found
in the exact Skill directory. It does not visibly prove that its overlay finds
real defects at an acceptable false-positive rate. It also does not address
multi-page rhythm, print geometry or content-derived grid choice.

### Reject or re-verify

- Reject “no horizontal overflow, no exceptions.” Tables, maps, code,
  comparison views and canvases can require bounded two-dimensional access.
- Reject blanket `overflow-x: clip` as a design repair. It can hide required
  content and does not remove the parent cause.
- Reject universal 44 by 44 targets, 12 percent collision threshold,
  0.50/0.46 optical centre, density multipliers, alignment tolerances,
  coefficient-of-variation thresholds and balance acceptance bands.
- Contrast and target-size criteria require the applicable standard and
  platform scope. They are not universal because the script calls them gates.
- “Pixels win” is too strong. Pixel centroid does not encode semantic
  hierarchy, gaze, cultural reading, interaction, intentional imbalance or
  perceived visual weight.
- A mandatory separate subagent is not portable and does not prove independent
  judgment. Scoville may use another reviewer when available, but must retain a
  single-agent path and honest evidence label.

## Candidate 3: `better-layout`

### Claimed scope and observed mechanism

The Skill owns web interface grouping, alignment, reading order, progressive
disclosure, spacing and responsive adaptation. It preserves the incumbent
styling system, treats its values as starting points, uses logical properties,
derives breakpoints from content failure, tests pseudo-localization and RTL,
and reports severity plus `Not verified` states.

### What is better than the current Scoville reference

- Content-driven breakpoints and container queries are concrete
  recomposition mechanisms.
- Pseudo-localization, representative locales, zoom, RTL mirroring, safe areas
  and long unbreakable content make stress conditions operational.
- Good/bad examples show why fixed widths, physical properties and hidden
  overflow fail.
- The report groups repeated instances under one parent cause.

### Adoption-priority result

The references contain useful code examples but no visible artifact. They do
not show spacing rhythm, negative-space quality, hierarchy or responsive
character in a rendered page. The scope is web UI. It does not cover
editorial continuity, facing spreads, print page geometry, image crop systems
or deliberate cross-format exceptions.

### Reject or re-verify

- The 2x group-gap rule, 12/24 px control clearances, 16 px margins and one
  spacing step per hierarchy level are starting hypotheses only.
- “Space, then background, then separator” is a useful comparison order, not a
  universal preference. Dense tables, timelines and incumbent systems may
  need other grouping cues.
- “Most important near top and leading edge,” action-count rules and peek
  dimensions depend on task, language, platform and artifact.
- Sticky controls and wrapping are not automatically valid repairs. They must
  preserve focus, content, task and safe-area behavior.

## Mechanisms to synthesize and claims to withhold

### Adopt or test through original Scoville wording

1. Run an unanchored visual assessment before a mechanical scan, then explain
   where the evidence lanes agree or conflict.
2. State a spatial thesis with primary path, groups, lead/support relation,
   density, rhythm and protected adaptation behavior before choosing
   coordinates.
3. Treat geometry and overflow checks as annotated pointers. Separate binding
   functional failures from likely accidents and aesthetic signals. Preserve
   explicit human/render judgment for the latter.
4. Use content-driven breakpoints, container-aware variants,
   pseudo-localization, RTL mirroring, zoom and dynamic-content extremes.
5. Require observed render/source evidence for every verification claim and
   keep unrun checks visibly unverified.

### Retain from current Scoville instead of importing

- content-derived least-complex grid rather than 12 columns or one spacing base
- negative space as separation, pacing and emphasis, with diagnosed trapped or
  competing gaps rather than “more whitespace”
- distinct importance, narrative, task, visual, programmatic and focus order
- deliberate asymmetry, overlap, crop, density and off-grid work with a stable
  counterstructure and compensation
- smallest-parent-cause repair from requirement/content through relation,
  container, measure, grid, scale/density, crop/source and optical correction
- medium proof for responsive web, fixed ratios, multi-page/editorial and print
- actual platform, printer, binding, safe-area, crop, trim and bleed authority

### Reject from the executable package

- universal spacing ratios, scales, columns, breakpoints, safe areas and margins
- fixed optical centres, ink-density multipliers, balance targets, collision
  percentages or alignment tolerances
- “no overflow” without bounded two-dimensional exceptions
- blanket clipping, stacking, centring, asymmetry, whitespace or card bans
- F/Z scan paths, leading-edge priority or one primary action as universal
- detector totals or screenshots presented as hierarchy, user, market or print
  proof
- mandatory fresh subagents, browser dependencies or external catalogs as the
  only valid execution path
