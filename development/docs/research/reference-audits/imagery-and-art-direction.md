## 1. Current contract

### Activation, ownership, maturity, and size

The Direct Index activates this leaf for **imagery, art direction, generation, crops, and sequence**. `modules.yaml` routes the `imagery` and `art_direction` signals to a single `retained-floor` / `external-verification` module that owns `image_thesis`, `shot_or_generation_brief`, `image_sequence`, and `crop_system`. The executable leaf is compact—about 400 prose words and comfortably below the 1,800-token leaf ceiling.

Its listed sources are L-01, L-10, L-17, L-18, L-28, and L-36 through L-40. The module admission ledger additionally claims L-41 through L-45, but those newer sources do not appear in the executable header, `modules.yaml`, or the current rule-source map. This is a documentation/evidence-link drift to resolve during a later executable intervention, not permission to silently treat those sources as already shipped rules.

The only direct model evidence is the open imagery-system baseline (`SOL-B01`). All three arms already produced extensive art-direction systems with shot logic, crop planning, locality, consent, rights, anti-cliché controls, and production questions. This supports a retained professional floor and argues against broad photography or illustration teaching. It does not prove expert-equivalent photography, illustration, commissioning, picture editing, documentary verification, rights clearance, or generated-series consistency.

### Existing generation and commissioning contract

The leaf already requires a communication job, one image thesis, observable content before adjectives, and explicit subject/action/relationship/viewpoint/environment/light. It distinguishes sequence roles—opener, evidence, context, exchange, contrast, closer—and separates invariants from controlled variation. For people and factual work it requests real actions, names, roles, claims, releases, local evidence, and sources. For generated or edited images it records preserve/change regions, reference authority, prohibited inventions, review points, and provider limits.

### Existing critique, repair, exception, and verification contract

Critique covers specificity, focal point, viewpoint, light logic, depth, gesture, material truth, repetition, sequence, crops, text interference, stereotypes, tokenism, fabricated evidence, retouching, and consistency. Repair starts with the brief or selection rather than a stronger look. Deliberate noise, flash, blur, awkward crops, distortion, and artificial colour may survive when they strengthen the thesis without masquerading as evidence. Verification requires masters and every required crop at intended size, contextual alternatives, current provider/legal checks, and a hard stop on assumed rights or fabricated facts.

### Ownership boundary

Imagery owns the **visual decision and asset-level brief**. It may detect a missing consent, licence, source, disclosure, profile, or production condition and stop the art-direction path. It must not absorb the independent owners:

- `culture-ethics-and-provenance`: representation, consent/privacy risk, synthetic evidence, IP/jurisdiction;
- `sources-and-attribution`: source, licence, attribution, and current fact verification;
- `media-production-and-handoff`: editable master, export, preflight, render, and handoff evidence;
- `colour-and-reproduction`: profiles, gamut, colour conversion, and colour proof;
- `composition-and-layout`: parent spatial hierarchy when image repair materially restructures the artefact.

## 2. What is already strong

1. **One image thesis before style.** The subject and message determine the picture; a style label does not substitute for a visual proposition.
2. **Observable direction.** “Who or what, doing what, where, from which viewpoint, under what light” is operational for photographers, illustrators, image editors, and generators. It avoids adjective-only prompting.
3. **Sequence jobs rather than repeated heroes.** The opener/evidence/context/exchange/contrast/closer vocabulary gives each image a distinct communicative function while allowing the set to stay coherent.
4. **Invariants versus controlled variation.** Protecting defining action, hands, faces, objects, data, and copy space across crops is a professional series mechanism, not a look recipe.
5. **Truth outranks attractiveness.** The leaf explicitly rejects an attractive frame that changes a factual claim, loses defining action, fabricates evidence, or manufactures heritage.
6. **Generated-image control is edit-oriented.** Preserve/change regions, reference authority, prohibited inventions, and review points are stronger than a generic subject/style/composition/lighting prompt formula.
7. **Repair targets causes.** Rebriefing, reselection, or recropping precedes heavier grading or a more fashionable treatment.
8. **Exceptions are contextual.** Blur, flash, grain, distortion, or difficult crops are not defects by category. They remain only when thesis, meaning, access, and truth survive.
9. **Accessibility is contextual.** The leaf correctly rejects the idea that one isolated alt description settles every use of the asset.
10. **Provenance claim is bounded.** C2PA association and tamper history are not confused with factual truth, authorship, rights, consent, or quality.

These strengths already exceed most public image-generation Skills found in the bounded landscape. The intervention should preserve this compact art-direction logic and deepen diagnosis and proof, not add camera-setting recipes, style menus, or prompt incantations.

## 3. Missing professional capability

### Selection and sequence

The current role list is useful but does not yet state how to select among near-duplicates or diagnose a failing series. Professional picture editing judges **selection, redundancy, transition, pacing, context, cumulative representation, and the change in meaning caused by order**. A strong frame can still weaken the sequence when it repeats an established fact, front-loads spectacle, withholds needed context, or creates a false causal implication beside another image.

### Commissioning

“Generate or commission” currently emphasizes the picture but not the complete commission. The leaf needs a compact commission record: intended use and audience; dimensions and all crops; copy and safe zones; factual/representation constraints; reference purpose; rough and approval stages; number and owner of revision rounds; final media and editable deliverables; schedule and dependencies; fee/expenses boundary; licence media, territory, duration, exclusivity, attribution, and sub-licensing; model/property releases where applicable; cancellation/change authority; and who supplies or verifies claims. Exact contractual advice remains jurisdiction-specific.

### Photography formal variables

Viewpoint and light are named but the diagnostic variables are under-specified. A professional brief or critique should distinguish:

- camera position, distance, height, angle, field of view, and resulting spatial relationship;
- frame edge, horizon, foreground/background, occlusion, and subject-to-context ratio;
- focus plane, depth of field, motion rendering, timing, gesture, and gaze;
- light direction, apparent size/quality, contrast, colour, falloff, reflections, and consistency with the scene;
- exposure or processing that hides/reveals consequential information;
- background relevance, material/skin/product truth, and the difference between a candid event, constructed portrait, reenactment, product construction, and synthetic concept.

These variables are decisions, not universal settings. “Always nearest eye,” “golden hour,” “f/8,” “one over focal length,” “avoid direct flash,” or “never crop joints” are context-dependent shortcuts and can actively weaken the intended image.

### Illustration formal variables

The leaf lacks an equally explicit illustration vocabulary: literal–abstract strategy; metaphor and claim boundary; micro–macro viewpoint; silhouette; gesture; shape and countershape; line character; value and colour hierarchy; edge/detail density; texture/material logic; dimensional or perspective system; scale relationships; spatial layering; negative space; and fidelity required for the subject. Consistency should mean repeatable relationships and mark logic, not identical composition or a three-style menu.

### Crop, focal point, and text-over-image

Crop protection is strong, but there is no crop decision test: what meaning is removed, what new relation the edge creates, what remains recognizable at final size, whether eye line/action/horizon is changed, and whether the crop creates a false claim. Text zones must be checked against **every actual crop and the most conflicting background area**, not against the master or an average overlay. When platform technology permits, real text should remain separate from raster imagery so it can reflow, localize, resize, and meet contrast requirements.

### Consistency without cloning

The leaf needs a series matrix that locks only load-bearing invariants—subject identity, point of view, light/material logic, mark system, palette family, processing, caption/provenance conventions—and assigns deliberate variation in scale, scene, crop, density, rhythm, and image job. One reference image can anchor material continuity but must not force every layout, pose, or composition.

### Representation, consent, and documentary integrity

The current warnings need a mode gate before creation or editing:

1. documentary/news/evidence;
2. observed but commissioned nonfiction;
3. constructed portrait or demonstration;
4. disclosed reenactment;
5. illustration/diagram;
6. synthetic concept or fiction.

Each mode allows different direction and edits. A constructed portrait can be directed if identified; a documentary scene cannot be staged or repaired with invented people/objects; an illustration may synthesize but cannot be presented as observed evidence. Cropping, order, captions, retouching, and generated fill can mislead even when individual pixels look plausible. Vulnerable participants require a harm/privacy assessment, informed use context, and the appropriate culture/jurisdiction owner—not anonymous “diversity casting.”

### Generated-image prohibited inventions

The existing phrase needs an explicit list for factual or identity-sensitive work: no invented person, action, object, product feature, label, logo, text, number, location marker, cultural signifier, scientific structure, historical detail, injury, weather, provenance cue, or evidence of an event. A generator may also change content the prompt asked it to preserve. Therefore “do not invent” is a requirement, not proof: every protected feature must be compared to an authoritative input, and unresolved drift blocks factual use.

### Rights and provenance

The leaf needs to distinguish creator, copyright owner, licensor, source/supply chain, depicted-person/property rights, usage terms, model/property release status, credit line, adaptation, generated ingredients, and current destination licence. IPTC fields can transport descriptive and rights assertions; C2PA can bind signed provenance assertions and ingredients to an asset. Neither proves that the assertion is true or that permission exists. Metadata may be absent or stripped, so the project asset register remains canonical.

### Accessibility

Image purpose changes by context: the same asset may be informative, decorative, functional, contain text, or be complex. Alt text, extended description, adjacent explanation, caption, transcript, tactile/sonified alternative, or null alternative are therefore delivery decisions, not properties inferable from pixels alone. Meaningful text baked into an image also reduces reflow/localization/user customization and may violate scoped Web requirements.

### Effective resolution and reproduction

Pixel dimensions alone do not establish fitness. Effective resolution depends on placed/cropped output size; viewing distance, screening/print process, substrate, sharpening, resampling, compression, colour workflow, and provider specification determine acceptance. A linked master, placed rendition, responsive derivative, and final export may differ. The leaf needs to request the actual destination and require final-size inspection/preflight without importing a universal 300-ppi rule.

## 4. Rule-quality audit

| Rule or claim | Class | Audit judgment | Professional replacement or exception |
|---|---|---|---|
| State one image thesis tied to the communication job | Evidence-bounded design rule | Keep. It focuses selection and direction without prescribing style. | Permit more than one thesis only when the deliverable explicitly has separate audiences/chapters; split the series rather than blur them. |
| Describe observable subject/action/context before adjectives | Strong operational heuristic | Keep and deepen with formal variables and prohibited inventions. | Stylized or abstract work may omit literal scene detail, but must still specify the communicative relation and permitted invention. |
| Each sequence image has a distinct job | Strong picture-editing heuristic | Keep. | Repetition may be the deliberate evidence—typology, duration, accumulation—but the repeated relation and progression must be named. |
| One focal point per image | Contextual convention | Reject as universal. Multiple or distributed attention can communicate systems, crowds, simultaneity, or ambiguity. | Require intentional attention structure and a readable consequence, not a fixed count. |
| Rule of thirds / centered symmetry / odd numbers are inherently better | Cargo-cult heuristic | Reject. | Diagnose spatial relation, edge tension, hierarchy, and thesis; use a convention only when it produces the intended effect. |
| Golden hour, natural light, shallow depth, or all-in-focus is professional | Preference or brand convention | Reject. IBM’s contrary “natural, all in focus” rule is explicitly its own brand language. | Choose light and focus by subject truth, action, production feasibility, reproduction, and intended distance. |
| Focus nearest eye / never crop at joints / never direct flash | Numeric or categorical shortcut | Reject as a general rule. | Protect expression/action/identity; allow deliberate crop, blur, flare, flash, or occlusion when its consequence improves the whole and does not mislead. |
| A strong prompt proves a generated image obeyed it | False proof claim | Reject. Provider documentation itself records composition, text, and consistency limits. | Compare output to protected-content and invention checklists; regenerate or withhold when factual drift remains. |
| Generative fill, AI upscaling, or object removal is acceptable because the tool offers it | False authorization | Reject. Capability does not set documentary, evidentiary, legal, or contest policy. | Determine image mode and destination policy first. In documentary/evidence work, prohibit any operation that introduces or removes content under the governing standard. |
| Documentary images must never be directed | Overbroad | Refine. Candid events must not be staged, while identified constructed portraits, demonstrations, and transparent reenactments can have different rules. | Label mode and intervention; keep caption/process disclosure and destination policy. |
| Provenance metadata proves truth or licence | False normative overclaim | Reject. | Treat IPTC/C2PA as transport and association evidence; verify assertions, rights, identity, and current usage separately. |
| Consent or a release automatically makes representation ethical | False ethical overclaim | Reject. | Assess dignity, coercion, downstream risk, context, audience, privacy, and withdrawal/usage conditions with the proper owner. |
| Alt text belongs permanently to the image | False accessibility rule | Reject. | Author the alternative from image purpose in the delivered context; preserve source description and metadata as inputs, not final truth. |
| Put copy directly in generated/raster imagery for fidelity | Risky convenience | Reject by default on the Web and editable systems. | Keep text live when possible; if raster text is essential, verify exact wording, contrast, localization, accessibility alternative, and every crop. |
| 300 ppi at 100% is universally print-ready | Cargo-cult number | Reject. PDF/X itself does not set one universal quality threshold; process and market segment matter. | Check effective resolution at final size against provider/GWG profile for that destination, then preflight and inspect proof. |
| A consistent series repeats one layout or prompt | False system rule | Reject. | Lock selected formal invariants and vary composition by image job; inspect the set as a strip/contact sheet. |
| Blur, noise, grain, artificial colour, distortion, awkward crop are defects | Preference | Keep current exception logic. | Retain when causally tied to thesis and when factual, identity, accessibility, and reproduction floors survive with compensation. |

Consequential guidance should use the pattern **observation → likely visual/ethical/technical cause → meaning or production consequence → smallest coherent repair → exact evidence**. Taste disagreement remains labelled as preference; binding floors remain binding in their stated scope.

### Source synthesis and contradictions

- **Selection:** NPPA’s criteria establish a professional mechanism—selection, sequence, pacing, context—not a universal aesthetic order. Commercial campaigns, essays, evidence sets, and social sequences may use different pacing, but still need explicit cumulative meaning.
- **Documentary edits:** NPPA and World Press Photo both prohibit generative content in documentary competition, but their detailed allowance for AI-assisted enhancement differs. This supports a destination-policy gate, not one invented global manipulation rule.
- **Formal style:** IBM’s highly specific natural light, focus, grid, and palette choices are strong worked system examples and weak universal rules. MIT and open photography sources support variables and experiments instead of one brand outcome.
- **Generation:** Provider documentation shows how to request preservation and also acknowledges composition/consistency/text limits. It cannot prove preservation and cannot authorize factual invention.
- **Resolution:** Adobe offers a common production default; PDF/X/GWG evidence shows that minimum fitness varies by final size, screening/print process, and market segment. The leaf must request the real destination rather than ship a number.

## 7. Applied Dos and Don’ts

| Concern | Do | Don’t | Diagnose → cause → repair → proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Image thesis | State subject, action/state, relationship, audience effect, and why an image—not merely a style—is needed. | Begin with “cinematic,” “editorial,” “premium,” or an artist imitation. | “Atmosphere replaces the actual service relationship” → thesis is style-first → specify observable exchange and context → compare candidate images against the brief. [L-01, L-10] |
| Photography direction | Specify camera position/distance/angle, field of view, frame edges, focus/motion, gesture, background, and light behavior by intended consequence. | Apply fixed genre settings, golden hour, shallow depth, eye focus, or rule of thirds automatically. | “Hands prove the process but fall outside shallow focus” → focus choice contradicts evidence job → deepen/reposition focus → inspect critical detail at final size. [L-28, L-36] |

## 8. Architecture recommendation

### Bounded mechanism comparison

1. **Retain one leaf and deepen it with a shared image contract.** Photography, illustration, selection, crop, and generation share a communication job, formal variables, invariants, truth/rights/access gates, and final-output proof.
2. **Split into flat `photography-and-picture-editing` and `illustration-and-generation-direction` leaves.** This gives more craft vocabulary but creates ambiguous mixed campaigns, duplicates selection/crop/access rules, and still cannot own culture, sources, colour, or media production.
3. **Reduce to a bounded stub and defer craft to model knowledge/human specialists.** This preserves tokens and the proven neutral baseline, but drops the current actionable truth, crop, provider, and verification floors.

### Recommendation: retain one leaf, deepen within 1,800 tokens

Mechanism 1 best preserves routing and progressive disclosure. The load-bearing challenge is whether one leaf can honestly cover photography, illustration, commissioning, generation, and picture editing. It can do so only at **art-direction contract depth**, not as a substitute for a photographer, illustrator, picture editor, rights professional, accessibility specialist, or production operator. The claim ceiling and escalation gates are therefore structural, not footnotes.

The executable leaf should eventually be rewritten around six compact gates:

1. **Mode and job:** image purpose, audience, documentary/constructed/illustrated/synthetic mode, destination, authority, and one thesis.
2. **Formal direction:** shared relation plus medium-specific variables—photographic capture/light/moment or illustrative abstraction/mark/perspective.
3. **Selection/sequence/crop:** per-image job, distinct contribution, invariants/variation, text zone, protected content, and cumulative meaning.
4. **Commission/generate:** use/crops/approvals/deliverables plus reference roles, preserve/change, prohibited inventions, provider limits, and disclosure.
5. **Integrity/access/rights stop:** representation/consent, documentary policy, contextual alternative, licence/provenance gap, and correct owner routing.
6. **Inspect/prove:** master and all crops at final context, identity/fact comparison, text interference, strip/contact sheet, effective resolution, export/preflight/proof label.

The leaf should **not** contain exposure tables, focal-length recipes, camera shopping advice, style catalogues, prompt templates, fixed image counts, fixed aspect ratios, universal PPI, legal conclusions, or detailed metadata schemas. Current tool/legal facts stay in sources and are loaded only when the task requires verification.

### Routing and owner preservation

- Pure subject/shot/illustration/sequence/crop direction selects Imagery only.
- Representation, consent, privacy, synthetic evidence, or jurisdiction independently selects Culture.
- A source/licence/attribution/fact audit independently selects Sources; a prompt that merely says “use licensed images” does not force a source audit if verified receipts are supplied.
- Export/preflight/effective-resolution handoff independently selects Media Production; Imagery keeps only the final-size asset check and evidence request.
- Colour conversion/proof independently selects Colour; image selection based on colour mood alone remains Imagery unless colour-system work is material.
- Deep whole-design critique selects Critique; ordinary image-specific critique stays in Imagery.

This preserves the normal three-expert ceiling and avoids making every image request load five risk owners.

### Routing and regression tests

- `Create a photographic opener and three responsive crops` selects Imagery, not automatic Composition/Media/Culture/Sources.
- `Create a disclosed fictional generated illustration` selects Imagery; Culture joins only when synthetic-evidence, representation, IP, privacy, or jurisdiction is material.
- `Use an AI-generated image as documentary evidence` selects Imagery plus Culture; Sources joins only for a real source/fact/licence audit.
- `Verify whether this stock licence covers the campaign` selects Sources, not Imagery unless art-direction judgment is also requested.
- `Preflight linked image resolution for a print handoff` selects Media Production; Imagery joins only when crop/selection/visual repair is material.
- Current open imagery-system strength must not regress: locality, consent, rights, anti-cliché, crop, and production questions remain present without a broad tutorial.

### Claim ceiling

After a bounded intervention, the Skill may claim that it can frame and art-direct images; distinguish image modes; write better shot, commission, illustration, crop, and generation briefs; select and sequence by communicative job; identify causal image defects; preserve accepted formal choices; specify prohibited inventions; surface representation/access/rights/production gaps; and request appropriate evidence.

It must not claim photographer-, illustrator-, commissioner-, picture-editor-, accessibility-, legal-, archival-, colour-management-, or prepress-equivalent expertise; universal taste; guaranteed identity or series consistency; documentary truth from pixels/metadata; permission from provenance; ethical adequacy from consent alone; accessibility from isolated alt text; current legal/provider facts without verification; or print fitness from a file, pixel count, or generic PPI value. Open tests and one reviewer cannot support market preference, expert parity, or cross-cultural safety.

## 10. Priority (P0-P3)

### P0 — current guidance can cause material harm or false proof

- Add the documentary/constructed/reenacted/illustrated/synthetic mode gate and prohibit content invention/removal according to the named destination policy.
- Make generated-image prohibited inventions explicit and require output-to-authority comparison; a prompt is never proof.
- Separate IPTC/C2PA association data from truth, permission, consent, authorship, and quality.
- Add contextual accessibility and text-over-image checks for every delivered crop; do not freeze one alt description at asset level.
- Reject universal “high-resolution/300 ppi” approval; use effective final-size resolution and actual provider/preflight evidence.
- Preserve owner routing: imagery detects and stops; Culture/Sources/Media/Colour verify their concerns.

### P1 — missing applied capability blocks the all-round designer goal

- Add professional selection/sequence diagnostics: distinct contribution, redundancy, transition, pacing, context, order-induced meaning, and cumulative representation.
- Add the compact commissioning record with use, dimensions/crops, approval/revision, deliverables, schedule, licence/credit/release, and claim ownership.
- Add photography and illustration formal-variable vocabularies without default settings or style menus.
- Add crop/focal/text-zone diagnosis and a series invariant-versus-variation matrix.
- Add causal critique format and final master/crop/contact-sheet inspection.
- Resolve L-41 through L-45 evidence-link drift in a later executable change so ledger, module entry, header, and rule map agree.

### P2 — useful depth and efficiency

- Add IPTC 2025.1 as a candidate structured image-metadata source and keep the project asset receipt canonical.
- Add explicit handling of link integrity, alpha/transparency, compression, resampling/sharpening, and placed-size effective resolution at the imagery/media handoff.
- Add transparent constructed-portrait/reenactment exception examples so documentary rules are not overgeneralized.
- Add provider regression checks for identity, text, composition, and recurring series elements when image generation is actually used.

### P3 — defer or reject

- Do not add camera exposure tables, lens/gear recommendations, golden-hour schedules, crop-at-joint bans, or fixed genre settings.
- Do not add three-style menus, artist-style recipes, topic-to-aesthetic mappings, fixed image counts/aspect ratios, prompt magic words, or generated-text workarounds as general art direction.
- Do not split the leaf until token or routing tests show that the shared image contract fails; no current evidence supports the extra routing cost.
- Do not teach full contract law, releases, image licensing, ICC/prepress, metadata schemas, or platform APIs inside the leaf. Route current/high-risk details to their actual owner and authority.

**Overall audit result:** retain and selectively deepen the single leaf. Its professional advantage should be a mode-aware, inspectable image contract that connects thesis, formal variables, selection/sequence, crop, commissioning/generation, integrity, access, rights, and output proof—without pretending that prompt detail or a polished image establishes truth, permission, or production readiness.
