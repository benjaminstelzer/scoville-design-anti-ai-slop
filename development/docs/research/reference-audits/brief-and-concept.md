### Current operational rules

**Generation.** The leaf records purpose, audience/situation, message/content,
medium/dimensions, desired response, incumbent owner, constraints, evidence,
forbidden claims/assets and unknowns. It restates the problem without style
words, asks for two or three territories differentiated by organizing
mechanism, and records thesis, hierarchy, medium advantage, risk and invariant.

**Discrimination and selection.** It selects against communication,
specificity, feasibility, distinctiveness, access, evidence and production fit.
These are useful criteria, but there is no hard-gate/trade-off distinction,
criterion evidence, uncertainty state, instantiated comparison, format-set
test, or decision other than choosing one.

**Critique.** The style-stripping and subject/logo-swap tests locate missing
concept or subject evidence. They are fast discriminators, not conclusive
concept-quality tests.

**Repair.** It correctly repairs thesis or organizing mechanism before
components and preserves an existing strong concept. It does not map specific
failures to frame, carrier, selection, format, access or feasibility causes.

**Exception.** Style can sharpen but not replace concept; genre can be broken
when message and compensating cue survive. There is no explicit utility-artifact
exception where a quiet, system-consistent organizing rationale is sufficient
and an expressive signature would harm the job.

**Verification.** The selected direction is checked against required content
and owner constraints, with an honest no-strategy/no-user-research/no-
originality-guarantee boundary. It does not require a content manifest,
evidence-bearing rough, representative format proof, access/production owner,
or record of what remains untested.

## 2. What is already strong

1. **Ordinary clear briefs remain in Core.** Selective loading is preserved;
   the expert is not a mandatory preamble for every design task.
2. **The frame contains operational variables.** Purpose, audience/situation,
   required message/content, response, medium, dimensions, owner, constraints,
   evidence and unknowns are materially better than a mood-word questionnaire.
   [L-01, L-15, L-16]
3. **Invented strategy and research are explicitly forbidden.** This is a
   crucial professional boundary: a plausible persona, history or audience
   insight is not supplied evidence. [L-18, L-19]
4. **The problem is stated before style.** A one-sentence style-free
   communication problem helps prevent a palette or named aesthetic from
   becoming the answer before the job is known. [L-01, BF-01]
5. **Alternatives differ by mechanism rather than decoration.** Sequence,
   comparison, reveal, index, field, object, route, voice and transformation
   are genuinely more useful operators than “blue,” “serif,” or “minimal.”
6. **Each territory has a thesis and failure.** Naming the hierarchy, medium
   advantage, likely failure and invariant creates a falsifiable direction
   rather than an inspiration label.
7. **Selection is brief-relative.** Communication, specificity, feasibility,
   access, evidence and production fit prevent distinctiveness from becoming
   the only value.
8. **The subject-swap test detects generic templates.** It asks whether the
   subject supplies structural evidence rather than merely a replaceable logo.
9. **Repair targets the parent cause.** Changing the thesis or mechanism before
   polishing components follows the Core's causal-repair discipline.
10. **The claim ceiling is honest.** The leaf does not claim user research,
    brand strategy or guaranteed originality. Local SOL evidence supports this
    restraint: strong outputs in all arms did not establish a method winner.

The leaf is one of the best current stubs. Its weakness is not a wrong
philosophy; it lacks enough applied structure to discriminate mechanism from
surface, compare early evidence fairly, repair a weak concept, and prove that a
selected thesis survives real content and formats.

## 4. Rule-quality audit

### Classification

| Class | Rules that belong here | Override condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Binding constraints | Preserve required content, facts, claims, owners and hard constraints; do not invent user research, personas, strategy, history, feasibility, access, rights or approvals; do not ship a concept that fails a required communication/access/production gate. | Only verified new evidence or the authorized owner changes the requirement/brief. |
| Evidence-bounded rules | Parallel low-cost instantiation can improve comparison and reduce some fixation; examples can cause fixation; problem and solution frames can co-evolve; ideation sets have distinct quantity/variety/novelty/quality dimensions. | Findings are bounded to tested tasks, participants, domains, representations and measures; none guarantees better graphic concepts or originality. [BF-06 through BF-09] |
| Contextual conventions | Creative brief sections, problem statements, territory names, thumbnails/storyboards/wireflows, concept boards, approval gates, decision matrices, style-stripping and subject-swap tests. | Project scale, medium, team, incumbent system, audience, uncertainty and approval contract determine the useful form. |
| Heuristics and starting hypotheses | State the problem without style/solution words; explore mechanisms before polishing; instantiate alternatives at comparable cheap fidelity; use hard gates before trade-offs; test the most stressful formats; repair carrier before surface. | A clear locked brief, tiny reversible task, production emergency, mature system or evidence-backed single path may justify less exploration. Record why. |
| Preferences | Territory naming, number of options, sketch medium, matrix format, criteria weights, presentation order, mood-board density and whether rejected work is retained. | Preference never overrides message/content, user evidence, access, rights, production, incumbent ownership or the user's explicit request. |
| Justified exceptions | One direction only, deliberate genre break, style-led commission, familiar pattern, system-conforming utility concept, untested speculative territory, or concept that excels in one priority format. | State purpose and authority; preserve required communication/access; expose limitation; use compensating cues/alternate formats/prototype evidence; do not generalize the exception. |

### Slogans, cargo-cult numbers and unsupported universals to reject

- **“Always make three concepts.”** Two, three, five or twenty are neither
  diversity nor quality. Stop by distinct causal operator and decision value,
  not quota. [BF-09]
- **“Quantity breeds quality.”** Quantity, variety, novelty and quality are
  different measures; the original ideation work explicitly rejects simply
  summing them into one universal effectiveness score. [BF-09]
- **“Primary users are one persona.”** A persona without research is fiction;
  real user groups, tasks, barriers and contexts can be plural and asymmetric.
  [BF-02, BF-04]
- **“The client asked for a poster, so the problem is a poster.”** Artifact and
  solution requests must be separated from communication outcome and barrier.
- **“Concept equals one sentence.”** A sentence can state a thesis, but the
  concept is not demonstrated until subject evidence, carrier and audience
  interpretation/action form a repeatable relationship.
- **“Different font, colour and layout means different direction.”** Those can
  be three executions of the same mechanism. Conversely, materially different
  concepts may share a constrained visual system.
- **“A mood board is creative direction.”** It calibrates references/style. It
  does not define the subject-specific problem, mechanism, content relation or
  selection evidence.
- **“One signature element makes it memorable.”** A signature can aid recall or
  become detached decoration. Utility, safety and high-frequency artifacts may
  need familiarity and low friction instead. [BF-02, BF-04]
- **“More polished means more viable.”** High fidelity can hide a weak concept
  and bias comparison; fidelity should expose the selected uncertainty. [L-01,
  BF-08]
- **“Users choose the design.”** User observation can reveal comprehension,
  utility and response; it does not replace content, brand, access, technical,
  production or professional design authority.
- **“Score the matrix and pick the highest.”** Criteria/datum/weights can bias
  results; controlled convergence can trigger information gathering,
  development, combinations or new concepts rather than one arithmetic winner.
  [BF-10]
- **“If it works without colour/type, style is irrelevant.”** The stripping
  test diagnoses concept dependence; style, typography and imagery can still
  be legitimate carriers. The rule is not to remove them, but to name their
  causal job.
- **“A concept must work identically everywhere.”** The invariant should
  survive; composition, crop, interaction, pacing and carrier emphasis may
  adapt to each format.
- **“Parallel prototypes always win.”** The strongest experiment used novice
  designers and web advertisements; it supports a targeted comparison test,
  not a universal mandate for every medium/team/task. [BF-08]
- **“No close precedent found means original.”** Search boundary, reviewer
  perception and within-set difference do not prove global novelty, authorship,
  trademark availability or non-infringement.

### Contradictions and evidence limits

- Diverge/converge frameworks are useful orientation, but design is not a
  symmetric linear diamond. Early roughs can change the problem definition;
  the audit therefore requires an explicit frame-reopening record. [BF-01,
  BF-06]
- References can supply vocabulary and reveal conventions while also causing
  fixation. Abstract their role, compare several when useful, and test for
  copying; neither “research always” nor “avoid examples” is universal. [BF-07]
- Parallel prototyping has positive evidence in a bounded web-ad experiment.
  It supports a falsifier for comparable early alternatives, not forced visual
  divergence or multiple polished artifacts for every task. [BF-08]
- Quantity, variety, novelty and quality are separable research constructs.
  More ideas or a higher novelty rating does not prove usefulness, access,
  originality or legal distinctiveness. [BF-09]
- A selection matrix can structure disagreement and information gaps, but its
  datum, criteria, weights and apparent total can distort judgment. Selection
  remains a documented decision, not a calculation. [BF-10]
- Public-service and interactive-system guidance strongly supports user/context
  evidence, simple utility and early testing. It does not require every poster,
  cultural artifact or expressive commission to behave like a government
  transaction. [BF-02, BF-04, BF-05]

## 7. Applied Dos and Don’ts

| Concern | Do | Don't | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | State outcome, audience task/context, barrier, required message/action and requested deliverable separately; mark source/owner/status. | Do not repeat the requested artifact as the problem or invent research to complete the frame. | L-01; BF-01, BF-02 |
| Audience | Use observed/supplied needs, knowledge, barriers, environment and consequential access contexts; ask only direction-changing gaps. | Do not create a demographic stereotype/persona from model memory. | BF-02, BF-04, BF-05 |
| Content | Make an exact content/claim/asset manifest with hierarchy, owner and transformation limits before territories. | Do not test concepts on lorem ipsum or short ideal copy when real content exists. | L-01; binding Core rationale |
| Criteria | Freeze hard gates and comparative criteria from the brief before judging; attach owner/evidence and unresolved tests. | Do not add criteria after seeing a favorite or let “distinctive” outrank required communication/access. | BF-01, BF-10 |
| Territories | Change the organizing causal operator—sequence, comparison, reveal, route, index, transformation, voice, object/material, interaction/time—not merely treatment. | Do not call palette, font, mood or decoration swaps materially different concepts. | L-01; BF-09; bounded professional rationale |
| Carriers | Name how subject evidence becomes meaning/action through primary and secondary carriers; state the invariant. | Do not accept a slogan with no content relationship or observable consequence. | L-01; BF-01 |
| References | Give each reference a bounded structural job and record rejected/copy-risk traits; generate from the problem as well as precedent. | Do not clone a reference, average several into a trend centroid, or claim web search proves originality. | BF-07; L-18 |
| Instantiation | Give viable concepts comparable, low-cost, evidence-bearing roughs with the same real content; compare side by side before refinement. | Do not select prose territories or compare one polished comp with two labels. | L-01; BF-08 |
| Utility exception | Prefer quiet system/task logic when familiarity, speed, safety or repeated use is the communication job; waive expressive signature when harmful. | Do not force metaphor, novelty, motion or “memorable” decoration into every artifact. | BF-02, BF-04, BF-05 |
| Concept/style | Strip style to identify carrier logic, then test alternate treatment while holding the thesis; route named-style depth separately. | Do not dismiss style as irrelevant or promote a mood board to concept. | L-01; current ownership boundary |
| Selection | Apply non-negotiable gates first; compare trade-offs and uncertainty; choose select/develop/test/combine/defer/reject/reframe explicitly. | Do not sum a matrix and call the highest total objectively best. | BF-10 |
| Format set | Prototype the highest-risk formats/conditions and preserve thesis/invariant while adapting composition and carrier emphasis. | Do not require pixel-identical execution or approve from a single ideal hero. | L-01, L-19 |
| Access | Test whether the meaning/action survives relevant non-colour, nonvisual, reduced-motion, zoom, reading-order and cultural contexts; route exact rules. | Do not add accessibility after selecting a carrier that inherently excludes the required audience. | L-14, BF-04 |
| Feasibility | Label risk `unknown`, `plausible`, `prototype-tested` or `provider-approved`; test the uncertainty that could reverse selection. | Do not call an unpriced/unrendered/unlicensed concept feasible or reject a rough for lacking polish. | L-01, L-18, L-19 |
| Critique/repair | Trace observation → likely effect → frame/carrier/rough/selection cause → smallest repair → preserved strength → new proof. | Do not polish a weak mechanism, kill a strong thesis because of one rendering, or silently change the brief. | L-01; BF-06, BF-08 |
| Originality | Say subject-specific, different within this set, not found in the stated search, or perceived novel only when that evidence exists. | Do not promise globally original, unprecedented, trademark-safe or non-infringing. | BF-07, BF-09; L-18 |

### Failure → cause → repair map

| Observed failure | Likely cause | Smallest coherent repair | Recheck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Territories are palette/font siblings | Same mechanism generated repeatedly; diversity judged by surface | Return to problem evidence and change content order, audience action, carrier or meaning construction for the weaker territory | Pairwise mechanism matrix with style columns hidden |
| Thesis fits unrelated subjects | No subject evidence or category-specific tension carries the idea | Insert one verified subject process/object/language/data relation into the mechanism; reject if only logo/copy makes it specific | Subject-swap and evidence trace |
| Concept reads well but rough fails | Territory never specified a carrier or comparable real content | Build the cheapest carrier-specific rough; repair carrier/hierarchy, not description | Side-by-side real-content roughs at equal fidelity |
| One option wins because it is polished | Unequal effort/fidelity and premature serial feedback | Reduce to comparable fidelity or develop all surviving options to the same decision-relevant evidence level | Blind/order-balanced criterion review where practical |
| Required copy/claim disappears | No content manifest or concept assumes ideal brevity | Restore exact content, adjust hierarchy/carrier, or reject the mechanism | Literal/data/source parity plus intended-size render |
| Utility screen becomes theatrical | Distinctiveness/signature treated as universal gate | Reframe concept around task clarity, error prevention, familiar system behavior or progressive disclosure | Task time/error/comprehension and incumbent-system review |
| Concept collapses in crop/mobile/print/reduced mode | Invariant depends on one canvas, sensory channel or interaction | Move meaning to a more adaptable carrier; define variant-specific composition and equivalent | Highest-stress format/access set side by side |
| Selection matrix hides disagreement | Criteria/datum/weights imply false precision or unknowns scored as facts | Restore hard gates, mark `unknown/disputed`, record trade-offs and prototype the decision-changing uncertainty | Sensitivity review and authority sign-off |
| Stakeholder preference becomes user proof | Approval and audience evidence conflated | Label authority type, retain decision owner's call, and separately test comprehension/use with intended users | Decision record plus user/domain evidence |
| Production concern kills the idea too early | Unknown treated as impossible, or final fidelity demanded during exploration | Isolate the load-bearing production risk and run a cheap feasibility prototype/quote | Technical/provider receipt at declared evidence level |
| Surface repair cannot remove genericity | Parent problem is missing/false or carrier has no subject relation | Reopen the frame, correct evidence/criterion, regenerate affected mechanism | Updated brief event and new alternative comparison |

## 8. Architecture recommendation

### Considered mechanisms

1. **Deepen one `brief-and-concept` leaf.** Preserves problem–solution
   co-evolution in one payload and avoids router change. It still loads intake,
   audience/criteria framing and approval mechanics when a complete brief only
   needs concept alternatives; conversely, a contested utility brief would load
   territory/selection craft unnecessarily.
2. **Split framing from concept development/selection.** These are independently
   selectable task states with different artifacts, interventions and tests.
   They share a small feedback rule because roughs can reopen the frame. This is
   reflected in exact-domain prior art that separates kickoff brief from deeper
   direction and visual exploration.
3. **Retain a slightly stronger stub.** Most conservative response to strong
   SOL priors and no admitted topic payload. It would preserve the current focus
   but leave content manifests, concept carriers, utility exception, comparable
   instantiation, controlled selection and causal repair unspecified.

### Recommendation: two directly routed flat leaves (`P1`)

Exact professional coverage and correctness come first. The split is not a way
to force either leaf under 1,800 tokens; it follows independent routing signals
and artifacts.

**A. `brief-framing-and-criteria`**

- Route on a new explicit incomplete/contested-brief signal such as
  `needs_brief_frame`; do not load for ordinary clear briefs already handled by
  Core.
- Own `brief_frame`, `field_authority`, `assumption_unknowns`,
  `required_content_manifest`, `degrees_of_freedom`, `selection_criteria`,
  `approval_authority`, and `frame_revision`.
- Produce a compact evidence-status brief, not invented research or strategy.
- Initial cost estimate: about 1,200–1,800 tokens; exceed the target if needed
  to preserve field provenance, hard gates, utility/access/production context
  and frame repair.

**B. `concept-development-and-selection`**

- Route on `needs_concept` when materially different mechanisms, direction
  discrimination, selection or concept-level repair can change the result.
- Own `concept_territories`, `concept_carriers`, `mechanism_difference`,
  `rough_comparison`, `format_set_survival`, `concept_selection`,
  `concept_repair`, and `originality_boundary`.
- Include utility-artifact exceptions, comparable instantiation, hard-gate
  selection, uncertainty/test decisions and critique-to-repair.
- Initial cost estimate: about 1,700–2,500 tokens; exceed the target rather than
  remove a carrier, exception, format proof, repair path or claim boundary.

Both leaves remain direct and flat. Each repeats only: preserve required facts
and owner constraints; do not invent research/strategy; label unknowns; a new
rough may expose a faulty frame, which is recorded and routed back rather than
silently changing criteria. They never require or read one another. A task with
an incomplete brief and open concept choice selects both directly; a complete
brief selects only Concept; a locked utility artifact needing problem/criteria
clarification selects only Brief.

Ownership boundaries:

- Core owns ordinary brief capture, one selected subject-specific thesis, mode,
  rendering loop and general causal repair.
- Brief owns incomplete/contested framing and decision criteria, not user
  research, brand strategy, market facts or copywriting.
- Concept owns mechanism generation/selection/repair, not named-style history,
  domain craft implementation or production approval.
- `style-direction` owns named/period/vernacular visual DNA; style may carry or
  sharpen a selected concept but does not select the communication problem.
- Composition, typography, imagery, information, UI and motion experts
  instantiate and validate their own carriers when routed.
- Media Production and providers own technical deliverability; accessibility,
  rights, culture and evidence owners validate their consequential boundaries.

Expected load frequency: Core-only remains common; Brief is occasional at
kickoff/reframing; Concept is common for greenfield/high-visibility work and
rare for locked production edits. Typical combinations are Brief + Concept at
ambiguous kickoff, Concept + up to two domain experts during exploration, and
Brief alone for utility/problem clarification. Phase work when more domains are
needed and carry the compact decision record.

The smallest tested non-inferior payload is unknown. The current 467-token stub
has no isolated benefit or Terra case. Test coverage-complete leaves first;
then compress repetition or model-prior exposition, never criteria, exceptions,
repair, ownership or proof.

## 9. Tests and claim ceiling

### Smallest open Terra High falsifiers

| Case | Seed and task | Falsifies if | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1: incomplete utility brief | Given conflicting notes for a multilingual clinic check-in form with fixed design system, mandatory consent/error copy, observed low-literacy/barrier evidence, unknown device/printer constraints and an already requested “modern dashboard,” produce the decision-ready brief only. | It invents persona/research; treats dashboard/style as problem; omits required content/owners; forces expressive concept; confuses unknown with constraint; or does not define hard gates and success evidence. | Field-source/status trace, exact content manifest, owner/approval matrix, assumptions/unknowns, utility/access/production criteria and frame review. |
| G2: mechanism-diverse concept set and selection | From a complete supplied brief for one campaign across poster, social crop, email header and ticket, generate viable territories, instantiate comparable roughs, and recommend the next selection action. | Options differ only by style; concepts are slogans; real content changes; one option gets more fidelity; no utility/format/access/feasibility test; matrix total substitutes for judgment; or originality is overclaimed. | Pairwise mechanism/carrier matrix, content parity, roughs at comparable fidelity, criterion/gate record, format-set renders, access/production risk states and qualified reviewer decision. |
| C1: concept-set critique | Read-only critique of supplied territories containing a generic subject-swap idea, mood-board-only direction, copied precedent, over-concepted utility option, one strong but ugly rough, hidden mandatory copy and a post-hoc scoring matrix. | Findings focus on taste/components; strong thesis is discarded for polish; fixation/content/criteria/authority causes are missed; every expressive exception is condemned; or unrendered facts are asserted. | Localized observation/effect/cause/repair trace, brief/content diff, precedent boundary, equal-fidelity comparison and evidence labels. |
| R1: format/access/production repair | Repair a selected concept whose hero works on A3 but fails mobile crop, monochrome print, reduced motion, localization and current asset budget, preserving the thesis and required content. | It shrinks/crops blindly; changes the message; deletes content; adds an unrelated signature; declares provider/access proof without evidence; or fails to reopen the brief when the invariant cannot survive. | Before/after carrier record, content checksum, representative renders, access equivalents, asset/production receipt, preserved-strength note and frame-change event if needed. |

Run one open Terra case at a time with Core-only, the smallest directly routed
leaf set, and only a relevant comparator. Score useful difference, not more
territories, more prose or more visual drama. A concept payload earns admission
only if it increases material mechanism difference, content/constraint fidelity,
selection honesty or causal repair without degrading outcome or loading an
unselected concern.

### Deterministic checks

- every brief field has value/status/source/owner; unknown and assumed are not
  serialized as facts;
- exact required content/claims/assets and forbidden changes match the supplied
  manifest; no omissions or invented operational details;
- problem statement contains outcome/audience task/barrier/message rather than
  only a style or requested artifact;
- constraints classify invariant/requirement/preference/open/unknown and name
  change authority;
- hard gates and comparative criteria exist before selection and resolve to
  brief evidence/owner; post-hoc changes create an event;
- every territory has thesis, subject evidence, primary carrier, mechanism,
  audience effect/action, invariant, main risk and cheapest falsifier;
- pairwise difference record shows at least one material change in content
  relation/order, viewer action, meaning construction, carrier or format
  behavior; palette/font alone does not satisfy it;
- comparable roughs use the same real content and declared fidelity; file sizes
  or pixel polish are not quality scores;
- decision is one of select/develop-test/combine/defer/reject/reframe and
  preserves unknown/disputed criteria; no arithmetic total auto-approves;
- representative variants preserve thesis/invariant/content while permitting
  medium-specific composition; format selection derives from the brief, not a
  fixed device count;
- relevant access channels, incumbent system, asset rights, technology,
  performance, budget, fabrication/export and maintenance risks have evidence
  states rather than unsupported pass/fail;
- critique/repair records connect observation, effect, parent cause, smallest
  repair, preserved strength and new proof; frame changes append visibly;
- claim language matches evidence: `different within set`, `subject-specific`,
  `not found in bounded search`, `render-inspected` and `production-approved`
  are never interchangeable.

Deterministic checks can prove record completeness, content parity, declared
mechanism difference, criterion timing, artifact syntax, format variants and
evidence labels. They cannot prove audience comprehension, usefulness,
distinctiveness, feasibility, cultural meaning, creative quality, prior-art
independence, legal originality or that the chosen concept is best.

### Required human/domain authority

- client/content/business owner for purpose, required message/content,
  constraints, approval and authorized brief changes;
- qualified user researcher and intended/affected users for audience tasks,
  barriers, context and comprehension/utility claims;
- communication designer/creative director for concept carriers, mechanism
  difference, rough quality, selection trade-offs and repair;
- copy/content/domain/source owners for claims, terminology, hierarchy and
  message fidelity;
- accessibility specialists and relevant disabled users when a carrier or
  artifact makes consequential equivalence claims;
- brand/design-system owner for preserve/extend/exception decisions;
- technical, production, fabrication, provider and rights owners for
  feasibility, budget, assets, output and maintenance approval;
- counsel/trademark/prior-art authority for legal originality, protectability,
  infringement and clearance questions.

### Claim ceiling after passing

Passing these open cases can support only that the changed leaves improved the
tested brief provenance, material mechanism divergence, comparable
instantiation, selection, format/access/production reasoning and concept repair
for the exact tasks, formats, evidence and reviewers. It cannot support:

- guaranteed creativity, originality, novelty, distinctiveness or superior
  taste;
- exhaustive problem framing, user research, market/brand strategy or correct
  audience prediction;
- one universal number of concepts, one ideation process, one selection matrix
  or one concept definition for all design fields;
- a claim that parallel prototypes, references or a particular rough fidelity
  improve every team/task/medium;
- global prior-art absence, trademark availability, copyright authorship,
  non-infringement or legal clearance;
- comprehension, accessibility, production readiness or provider acceptance
  outside the exact people, formats, tools and evidence inspected;
- stakeholder consensus, business outcome, campaign effectiveness, market
  differentiation, expert equivalence or lower total inference cost;
- non-inferiority of compressed payloads before symmetric Terra and human
  review.

## 10. Priority

**Overall priority: `P1`.** The current stub is safe, specific and honestly
bounded; it does not create a general `P0` risk. Missing brief provenance,
content/criteria control, concept carriers, comparable roughs, utility
exceptions, format survival, selection uncertainty and causal repair still
block the all-round designer goal.

| Priority | Gap clusters | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Brief field authority; outcome/problem/solution separation; exact content/constraint manifest; concept/carrier definition; material-difference test; comparable instantiation; utility exception; hard-gate selection; format/access/production proof; concept repair; originality boundary | These decide whether the concept addresses the real job, survives execution and can be selected/repaired honestly. |
| P2 | Deeper facilitation/co-design, reference de-fixation methods, sophisticated uncertainty/sensitivity review, portfolio/campaign concept governance and richer decision records | Useful after the P1 frame–concept–proof spine passes generation, critique and repair. |
| P3 | Large rhetorical-device catalogue, style adjectives/axes, fixed territory counts, famous-designer lenses, creativity scores, universal workshop recipes or historic idea examples | Enrichment risks fixation/cargo cult and does not qualify professional outcomes. |

Conditional escalation: an unresolved mandatory message/claim, access need,
rights issue, safety/legal constraint, fabricated audience insight, destructive
production assumption or deceptive originality assertion is `P0` in a live
consequential task. The Skill must preserve/remove/narrow, request authority,
prototype the risk or stop; a compelling concept cannot compensate.

### Open limits

- No brief interview, audience research, concept territory, rough, comparison
  board, format set, accessibility task, production quote, asset check or
  selection workshop was executed in this audit.
- No client, user researcher, intended/disabled user, creative director,
  content/domain owner, brand owner, producer, provider or counsel reviewed a
  resulting brief or concept.
- The public Skill search was targeted and may miss private, non-English,
  newly published, differently named or service-integrated briefing/concept
  Skills.
- Public Skills are implementation claims, not independent behavior evidence;
  several encode fixed counts, style divergence, proprietary services, taste
  memory, named-designer lenses or web/brand assumptions that must not transfer.
- Empirical studies are bounded mainly to industrial/engineering design,
  novice web advertisements or specific protocols; they do not establish a
  universal communication-design creativity method.
- ISO, Design Council, GOV.UK, W3C, NASAD and public Skill/repository state are
  mutable/scoped. Reference-only or NC sources cannot be copied into the MIT
  runtime; release claims require current license/source review.
