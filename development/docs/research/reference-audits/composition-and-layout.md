# Reference audit: `composition-and-layout`

Date: 2026-09-02

Method: [Per-reference expert-depth audit method](../reference-audit-method.md)

Research mode: Scoville Research, mixed Development and Academic route, with a
bounded single-coordinator Brainstorm pass

Freshness boundary: public sources and repositories inspected through
2026-09-02

## Research contract and conclusion

This audit asks whether the current leaf gives Terra High enough applied
composition and layout capability to generate, discriminate, critique, repair,
and prove cross-media work without restating knowledge the model already has.
It covers graphic, editorial, web, UI-adjacent, and print layout. It does not
claim exhaustive coverage of visual perception, typography, accessibility,
print engineering, or user research. Public search terms were generic; no local
source text, identifiers, or private URLs were submitted externally.

**Conclusion:** retain one directly routed leaf and deepen it within the
1,800-token ceiling. The current leaf has a strong causal core and an unusually
good exception boundary, but it is too compressed to distinguish a design grid
from a CSS layout grid or print page geometry, to handle multi-page continuity,
to preserve programmatic reading order during responsive recomposition, or to
route crop/bleed/safe-area proof to the actual production authority. These are
`P1` capability gaps, not reasons to add a style curriculum or universal numeric
recipes. No public Skill found in the bounded search covers this full
cross-media scope credibly; the best mechanisms are partial comparators.

The bounded Brainstorm considered three architecture mechanisms in section 8.
It was one coordinated pass with no claim of isolated or independent
generation.

The Core selects this leaf for hierarchy, layout, spacing, grid, or an explicit

data, UI, and production leaves own their narrower concerns when separately

### Current operational rules

**Generation**

- Inventory consequence, dimensions and viewing distance, sequence or scan
  path, crops, fixed edges/safe areas, incumbent structure, and protected
  elements.
- Establish one primary entry, subordinate levels, and an exit or action.
- Choose relationships before coordinates: group, separate, align, and scale
  according to meaning and consequence.
- Use the grid as a repeatable relationship system whose columns, modules,
  margins, and breaks come from content.
- Treat negative space as separation, pacing, and emphasis.
- Compose with real copy, labels, data, and assets, including longest likely
  text and required crops.

**Critique**

- Inspect the actual artifact at intended size and thumbnail.
- Trace predicted first entry and subsequent reads, grouping, edges, line
  breaks, trapped gaps, collisions, and exit.
- Distinguish a weak relationship from cosmetic spacing.

**Repair**

- Repair the smallest parent cause: content order, container, measure, grid,
  scale, or crop.
- Do not distribute many local nudges when one parent correction owns the
  defect.

**Exceptions**

- Permit off-grid, asymmetric, dense, overlapping, or cropped work when intent
  is legible, required content survives, stable counterstructure exists, and
  the whole improves.
- Do not normalize deliberate tension merely because it violates a guide.
- Use Gestalt research as bounded perceptual evidence, not universal layout
  law.

**Verification**

- Render every target ratio or viewport.
- Check edges, overflow, title fit, minimum useful text, reading order,
  required content, and protected dimensions.
- Compare before and after at the same scale.
- Do not treat valid source syntax as proof of visual fit.

### Current evaluation boundary

The SOL baseline found strong poster, editorial, presentation, web, and UI
composition in neutral, Core-only, and generic-checklist arms. The generic
checklist produced no repeatable advantage. The relevant failures were instead
artifact-integrity and content-fit failures: malformed SVG (SOL-B03), clipped
title/labels (SOL-B04), collapsed hierarchy and invented operational content
(SOL-B06), clipped social copy (SOL-B09), and overlapping or clipped slide
labels (SOL-B11). Repeats did not establish a model-specific teaching defect,
so the retained functional floor was justified.

Terra High has one relevant open poster repair. It preserved copy, identity,
dimensions, XML validity, and an honest unrendered evidence status; later
rendering showed a clear editorial hierarchy without clipping. That is one
open transfer observation, not evidence of general editorial, responsive,
multi-page, print-production, or composition mastery. The current leaf is
therefore correctly not `admitted`, but the audit method must not mistake one
successful poster for complete professional coverage.

## 2. What is already strong

1. **Relations before coordinates is the right causal abstraction.** It resists
   premature pixel recipes and keeps hierarchy, grouping, alignment, and scale
   tied to meaning rather than decoration. L-01 and L-10 support this as scoped
   formal-analysis and production practice.
2. **The grid is defined as a relationship system, not a visible ornament.**
   The content-led choice of columns, modules, margins, and breaks agrees with
   the strongest open editorial foundation: grids organize relations and can
   support both stable and asymmetric work; they are not one fixed column count
   or spacing scale (L-01).
3. **Negative space has several explicit jobs.** Separation, pacing, and
   emphasis are more actionable than the usual “use more whitespace” slogan.
   The leaf also rejects the inverse error that filled space is necessarily
   information.
4. **Real-content fit is a first-class input.** Protecting long text and crops
   directly addresses observed clipping failures and is more useful than
   placeholder-perfect layout advice.
5. **Critique looks for parent relations, not only surface polish.** The
   weak-relation versus cosmetic-spacing distinction is specific and causal.
6. **The repair rule is unusually good.** Content order, container, measure,
   grid, scale, and crop form a plausible parent-cause set. “Smallest coherent
   cause” is safer than a list of local offsets and aligns with the Core studio
   loop.
7. **The exception rule is strong and source-bounded.** It preserves required
   communication, demands counterstructure, and refuses automatic
   normalization of asymmetry, overlap, crop, density, or off-grid choices.
   This is exactly the boundary missing from public checklist Skills.
8. **The proof floor is honest.** Target renders, edge and overflow checks,
   same-scale before/after comparison, and the explicit rejection of syntax as
   visual proof are directly supported by local failures.
9. **Gestalt is already bounded correctly.** The leaf says perceptual grouping
   is evidence rather than a universal command. That matches Wagemans et al.'s
   account of cue-dependent grouping and figure-ground organization (L-13),
   including the limits of turning demonstrations into a unified theory.

These strengths should be preserved nearly verbatim in mechanism, although the
final executable wording must remain original and compact.

## 3. Missing professional capability

### Hierarchy and reading intent

- The leaf names consequence and sequence but does not separate **importance
  hierarchy**, **narrative sequence**, **task order**, **visual entry**,
  **programmatic reading order**, and **focus order**. These may differ without
  all being wrong. A repair must identify which order is broken.
- “First fixation” and “next three reads” are currently phrased like observed
  eye-tracking facts. Without eye tracking they are reviewer predictions. The
  instruction should say “predict and trace,” then reserve factual fixation
  claims for measured evidence.
- It lacks an explicit hierarchy test: at intended size and thumbnail, identify
  the primary message, supporting groups, required metadata, action/exit, and
  any competing equal-weight element.

### Gestalt without law abuse

- The leaf correctly limits Gestalt but gives no method for conflicting cues.
  Proximity, similarity, common region, connection, continuity, and
  figure-ground can disagree; real images combine weak cues. The agent should
  name the cue responsible for a grouping judgment and treat it as a hypothesis
  to inspect, not declare “the law of proximity.”
- It does not distinguish **grouping** from **figure-ground** or from aesthetic
  preference. Wagemans et al. explicitly treat these as related but distinct
  perceptual-organization problems (A-CL-02).

### Grids, modules, margins, proportion, and scale

- “Let content determine columns, modules, margins, and breaks” is correct but
  underspecified. The leaf needs a compact selection method: identify repeated
  units, longest measures, dominant media aspect ratios, alignment anchors,
  binding or safe edges, and permitted spans; then choose the least complex
  grid that maintains those relationships.
- It does not distinguish a **conceptual design grid**, a **CSS Grid/Flex
  implementation**, a **baseline or modular grid**, and **print page geometry**
  (page, spread, inside/outside margin, gutter, trim, bleed). One may exist
  without the others.
- Proportion and scale have no explicit diagnostic. Relative scale should map
  consequence, distance, and available area; no special ratio is binding. The
  current source L-01 presents the golden section as a historical organizing
  system, while empirical work shows proportion preference changes with task
  and context (A-CL-09). The leaf should reject phi, thirds, and other ratios as
  universal aesthetic laws.
- Alignment needs shared-edge, axis, baseline, and optical-alignment cases, plus
  an explicit distinction between accidental drift and deliberate offset.

### Rhythm, density, and negative space

- The leaf has no macro/micro rhythm test across page, spread, section, group,
  and component levels. Repetition should create expectation; variation should
  signal hierarchy or sequence, not merely break monotony.
- Density is listed only as an allowed exception. It should be framed as the
  amount and consequence of information per available area, tuned to task,
  distance, medium, and comparison need. Sparse is not automatically premium;
  dense is not automatically cluttered.
- Negative space needs failure modes: trapped gaps, accidental holes, detached
  labels, false grouping, dead margins, and empty areas that compete with the
  intended figure. “More whitespace” is not a repair.

### Scan behavior and reading order

- The leaf needs the claim ceiling that web scan patterns depend on task,
  layout, content type, motivation, and writing direction. F-pattern, layer
  cake, spotted, commitment, zig-zag, and other patterns are observations in
  bounded studies, not templates to impose (A-CL-08).
- For web work it must preserve meaningful source order and, where operation is
  sequential, a coherent focus order. CSS visual reordering does not change
  speech or default sequential navigation (A-CL-03, A-CL-05).
- For print/PDF it must verify intended text threading, page-to-page sequence,
  folios, continuation cues, and tagged reading order when accessibility is in
  scope. A visually plausible spread is not reading-order proof.

### Responsive recomposition

- “Render every viewport” does not explain recomposition. The leaf needs a
  small operator set: resize within a relation, wrap, stack, regroup, reorder
  only when meaning survives, disclose, change crop/source, or provide an
  alternate two-dimensional view.
- Breakpoints should occur where real content or a protected relationship
  fails, not because a familiar device number was recalled. WCAG Reflow's
  320-CSS-pixel condition is an accessibility conformance scope, not a universal
  design breakpoint (A-CL-04).
- It needs an explicit check that responsive changes preserve information,
  functionality, hierarchy, source order, focus order, focal crops, and the
  deliberate character of the composition.

### Multi-page continuity

- No current rule inspects sequence as a strip, facing spread, or publication.
  Professional editorial work needs recurring anchors, parent/template logic,
  folios and navigation, text and image pacing, section transitions, and
  controlled variation.
- Consistency must not mean identical pages. The test is whether a reader can
  orient across pages while hierarchy and pacing change deliberately. L-01
  treats the spread as the primary unit in books and magazines; Adobe's parent
  page mechanism shows how repeated elements can remain centrally owned
  (A-CL-01, A-CL-07).

### Crop, bleed, trim, and safe areas

- The current leaf mentions crops and safe areas without an authority chain.
  It should ask for target ratio/page size, trim, bleed, binding, platform
  chrome, safe inset, live area, and supplier/platform specification, then mark
  unknown values unverified.
- Responsive crop may require a distinct art-directed source, not merely
  scaling the same image. Required subject, action, gaze, caption relationship,
  and text-safe region must survive (A-CL-06).
- Adobe documents 3 mm / 0.125 in as a common example and explicitly notes that
  providers may require more. The actual printer, platform, template, or venue
  specification owns the number. No universal safe area or bleed belongs in
  the Skill (A-CL-07).

### Real-content extremes

- “Longest likely text” is necessary but incomplete. Stress cases should cover
  minimum and maximum item counts, empty and missing content, longest
  unbreakable token, multi-line labels, translated expansion, RTL or vertical
  writing where relevant, data outliers, image aspect-ratio extremes, and
  unavailable assets.
- The agent should not budget a generic expansion percentage. It should test
  supplied extremes or clearly named representative cases and report what was
  not tested.

### Causal diagnosis, smallest repair, and proof

- The parent-cause list needs an order. A useful default is: wrong content or
  requirement -> wrong semantic/order relationship -> wrong container/measure
  -> wrong grid/track/span -> wrong scale/density -> wrong crop/source -> local
  optical nudge. This is a diagnostic sequence, not a mandate to change every
  level.
- The leaf needs a “preserve strengths” line so a repair does not flatten an
  intentional asymmetry while fixing an unrelated overflow.
- Proof must vary by artifact: source validity, rendered target size,
  thumbnail/page strip, responsive extremes, programmatic order and overflow,
  PDF page boxes and preflight, print proof, projection check, or platform
  preview. A render proves only that render; it is not a physical press proof,
  user test, or supplier approval.

### Escalation

The leaf should escalate rather than invent when the decision depends on a
printer/profile/preflight specification, a platform safe-area template, a
native reader for unfamiliar writing systems, an accessibility specialist,
domain-critical reading order, or observed user scan behavior.

## 4. Rule-quality audit

| Class | Rules that belong | Boundaries and rejected inflation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Binding constraints | Preserve required content, meaning, dimensions, protected crops/edges, meaningful programmatic order, focus operability, and actual supplier/platform production requirements. Do not hide required content to make a composition fit. | Binding only when supplied by the brief, incumbent system, applicable standard, or actual production authority. |
| Evidence-bounded rules | Related items can be grouped by proximity, similarity, connection/common region, continuity, or combined cues; web content in WCAG scope must preserve meaningful sequence and applicable Reflow; CSS visual reorder is not logical reorder. | Gestalt mechanisms describe perception, not universal commands. WCAG applies in its exact web conformance scope. Eye-tracking findings do not prove a particular user's path. |
| Contextual conventions | Grids, modular and baseline systems, columns, margins, parent pages, folios, recurring anchors, spreads, alignments, and repeated spacing can support continuity and hierarchy. | A convention is selected for the content, medium, production system, and concept. It is not mandatory merely because the artifact is “editorial.” |
| Heuristics or numeric starting points | A candidate spacing scale, column count, ratio, crop, breakpoint, bleed, or safe inset may be tried when the incumbent system or provider has none. The 320-CSS-pixel Reflow check is testable in applicable WCAG scope. | Label every number as incumbent, source-specific, scoped normative value, or provisional starting point. Adobe's common 3 mm bleed example is not a universal print rule. |
| Preferences | Symmetry versus asymmetry, visible versus invisible grid, sparse versus dense, even versus syncopated rhythm, centered versus edge-anchored entry, and strict versus loose alignment. | Preferences require a stated effect and comparison in the actual artifact. They never become defects by themselves. |
| Justified exceptions | Off-grid placement, overlap, crop, asymmetric balance, dense information, broken baseline, irregular rhythm, or deliberate visual/linear-order divergence. | Keep required communication and accessibility floors; declare intended effect; name the stable counterstructure; inspect target contexts; preserve an alternate meaningful order where needed. |

### Slogans, cargo-cult numbers, and unsupported universals to reject

- “Gestalt laws say users will group this correctly.”
- “Users read every page in an F or Z pattern.”
- “The golden ratio, rule of thirds, or optical center is inherently best.”
- “Professional layouts use 12 columns, an 8-point grid, 65 characters, or a
  2x inter-group gap.”
- “More whitespace is more premium” or “dense layouts are cluttered.”
- “Everything should align” or “asymmetry is more dynamic.”
- “Mobile is desktop stacked into one column.”
- “Three millimetres of bleed” or one fixed safe inset for every printer,
  binding, device, social crop, or projection surface.
- “No horizontal overflow” without preserving the bounded exceptions needed
  for tables, maps, code, comparison views, canvases, or other intrinsically
  two-dimensional content.
- Any metric for balance, alignment, rhythm, collision, or density used as an
  aesthetic oracle. Metrics can locate likely accidents; the render and stated
  intent decide whether the signal is a defect.

### Contradiction and gap resolution

- L-01 presents the golden section as historically broad and gives thirds as a
  layout technique. A-CL-09 makes any privileged-ratio claim context-dependent.
  Result: ratios are optional organizing trials, never Skill laws.
- L-01's grids support continuity and variation; A-CL-05 shows that a CSS grid
  adds technical source-order hazards. Result: distinguish design rationale
  from implementation and verify both visual and programmatic order.
- A-CL-04 gives a precise 320-CSS-pixel Reflow condition; public Skills often
  turn familiar device widths into design laws. Result: retain the scoped WCAG
  floor, but derive aesthetic breakpoints from content failure.
- A-CL-07 gives a common 3 mm bleed example while warning that printers may
  require more. Result: ask for the supplier specification and keep the value
  unverified until received.
- A-CL-08 supports multiple scanning patterns rather than a single path.
  Result: use predicted scan/reading traces as critique tools, not factual user
  behavior without measurement.

## 7. Applied Dos and Don'ts

| Concern | Do | Don't | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hierarchy | Map required content to primary message, subordinate groups, metadata, action/exit, and protected order. At target size and thumbnail, predict the entry and trace the next meaningful transitions. | Do not call predicted entries “fixations” or assume one path for every viewer. Do not create hierarchy by hiding required content. | L-01; A-CL-08; bounded review rationale |
| Grouping and Gestalt | Name the visible cue that creates a group and check whether another cue contradicts it. Separate grouping, figure-ground, and preference judgments. | Do not invoke a “Gestalt law” as proof that users will perceive the intended relation. | A-CL-02 |
| Grid, modules, margins | Derive the least complex spatial system from repeated units, text measures, image ratios, shared anchors, page/spread edges, and permitted spans. ## 8. Architecture recommendation

### Decision: retain one leaf and deepen within 1,800 tokens

Composition is one causal system: hierarchy, grouping, grid, margin, scale,
rhythm, density, negative space, exceptions, and content fit interact before a
medium-specific proof branch. Splitting them into multiple flat leaves would
force routine co-loading, create ambiguous ownership, and increase the risk
that a grid leaf “fixes” an exception owned by composition. The current direct
route and four owned concerns remain correct.

The deepened leaf should stay a compiler and verification contract, not become
a design textbook. A compact executable structure can be:

1. **Frame the composition:** consequence/meaning hierarchy; intended visual,
   narrative, task, programmatic, and focus order; target contexts; real-content
   extremes; protected elements/crops; incumbent spatial/production authority.
2. **Choose the spatial system:** relationships first; least complex suitable
   grid/axis/module; margins/anchors; relative scale; rhythm/density/negative
   space; explicitly distinguish screen, responsive, multi-page, and print
   geometry.
3. **Recompose, do not merely shrink:** state which relation fails and choose a
   resize/wrap/stack/regroup/disclose/crop/source/alternate-view operator while
   preserving meaning and order.
4. **Inspect and diagnose:** target size plus thumbnail or other diagnostic
   context; predicted entry/sequence; groups; alignments; gaps; edges;
   collisions; crop; overflow; page/spread continuity; identify the first
   parent cause.
5. **Repair and preserve:** change the smallest owning cause, keep strengths,
   and stop after the Core's repair-pass ceiling.
6. **Exceptions:** require intended effect, functional floors, counterstructure,
   contexts, and compensation; metrics remain signals rather than taste gates.
7. **Prove by medium:** web order/Reflow/viewport; static ratios and protected
   crop; multi-page strip/spread/threading; print trim/bleed/page boxes/preflight
   and supplier proof. State what remains unverified.

Source-map changes, if later authorized, should retain L-01, L-10, and L-13;
add L-14 for meaningful order and Reflow, L-37 for responsive crop/source, and
L-45 or a newly verified print-geometry source for the supplier/preflight
boundary. A-CL-08 and A-CL-09 are primarily myth/claim-ceiling checks and need
not become runtime links.

## 9. Tests and claim ceiling

### Smallest open Terra High falsification set

Run paired current-leaf versus candidate-leaf arms with the same Terra High
model, reasoning, public brief, source artifact, renderer, and deterministic
checks. Keep outputs blinded for human review. These are open development cases,
not sealed holdout items.

| Case | Minimal public task | What would falsify the proposed depth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generation: editorial continuity | Create a four-page mini-publication from fixed real text and supplied/licensed images: cover, one facing spread, and final page. The brief supplies page/trim/bleed, protected crops, one deliberate off-grid pull quote, long/short text extremes, and a required recurring navigation/folio system. | Candidate produces no clearer hierarchy/continuity than current, adds a generic grid or ratio recipe, loses content/crops, makes every page uniform, or cannot state unverified print authority. |
| Critique: responsive order and grouping | Critique supplied desktop/tablet/320-CSS-pixel renders plus DOM outline for a public article page. Seed one accidental false group, one crop failure, one visual/programmatic-order mismatch, one density cliff, and one intentional overlap that remains functional. No edit. | Candidate reports preferences as defects, invokes Gestalt/F-pattern as law, misses the order/crop/root cause, or recommends flattening the intentional overlap. |
| Repair: one parent cause across crops | Repair an existing poster/social family whose required copy clips in two ratios because the parent measure/grid is wrong; several child offsets disguise the cause. Preserve one documented asymmetric/off-grid element and all text. | Candidate nudges children, changes several systems, normalizes the exception, or claims success without target renders and same-scale regression comparison. |

### Deterministic checks

- Exact required-string, numeral, image/reference, page-count, and dimension
  preservation.
- Parse/source validity for the chosen artifact format.
- No required object outside trim/live/viewport bounds; no accidental collision
  above the fixture's documented geometry threshold; intentional-overlap IDs
  are allowlisted and still visually inspected.
- Correct page, trim, and bleed boxes where the format exposes them; value must
  equal the supplied fixture, never a hard-coded Skill default.
- DOM/source sequence equals the supplied meaningful order; sequential focus
  preserves operation; CSS visual reorder is recorded separately.
- At every supported viewport: required content remains available, non-exempt
  content does not require two-dimensional reading, protected controls/crops
  survive, and no unaccounted horizontal overflow remains.
- Repair diff touches the owning container/grid/crop source rather than a spread
  of unrelated child offsets, subject to the artifact format's limits.
- Candidate package remains below 1,800 `o200k_base` tokens and preserves the
  direct module ID, signal, ownership, and flat routing contract.

Deterministic checks can establish preservation, geometry, syntax, and declared
order. They cannot establish good hierarchy, appropriate tension, persuasive
pacing, or whether an exception improves the whole.

### Required rendered or production evidence

- Generation: exact-size pages, individual pages, facing spread, and a
  thumbnail strip; bleed/trim preview; PDF/preflight receipt if PDF is the
  deliverable.
- Critique: supplied renders at all widths plus annotated evidence locations;
  no new render is required unless the task explicitly authorizes one.
- Repair: before/after renders at the same scale for every target ratio and a
  thumbnail comparison; a new parse/preflight receipt.
- Print-ready or production-proofed wording requires the actual supplier
  preset/specification and, where material, a physical or contract proof. A PDF
  render alone is insufficient.

### Human and domain authority

- At least one qualified reviewer judges hierarchy, grouping, rhythm, density,
  negative space, deliberate exception, and multi-page pacing while blinded to
  arm.
- A second reviewer or disagreement record is required before making a
  preference claim; one reviewer can identify a visible defect but not
  consensus.
- Native-reader or script expertise is required where reading direction or
  writing-system behavior is consequential.
- Printer, venue, platform, or product owner supplies safe area, bleed,
  binding, crop, and output requirements. The model must not self-certify them.
- Usability or eye-tracking claims require observed users and an appropriate
  study. A predicted scan trace is not user evidence.

### Admission and claim ceiling

An isolated win is not enough. Admit only the smallest rule cluster that fixes
a repeated or cross-case gap without regressing neutral/base-model strengths,
and retain original receipts when a harness defect is corrected. The candidate
may claim source-bound professional **process coverage** and specific verified
artifact outcomes. These tests still cannot support:

- expert equivalence or general composition mastery;
- universal aesthetic, balance, grid, spacing, ratio, or scan-path laws;
- user preference, comprehension, conversion, or market-quality claims;
- global accessibility conformance from the tested layouts;
- press, binding, projection, platform, or device production readiness outside
  the exact verified specification;
- cultural, multilingual, or writing-system authority beyond the inspected
  cases;
- independence or originality of the bounded Brainstorm directions.
