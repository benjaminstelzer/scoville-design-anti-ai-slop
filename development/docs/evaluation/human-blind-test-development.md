Within each pair, fresh isolated generation contexts received the same brief
and common production protocol. One received the frozen current Skill and one
received no Skill. The requested model configuration was Astra Medium. These
were model-authored SVG designs rendered through Edge, not outputs from a
separate image-generation model. The fifth round used ephemeral Codex CLI
contexts, while the preceding rounds used collaboration agents. A separately
reported backend model identity was unavailable.

The review page used anonymous image numbers with rank and feedback fields.
The human reviewer had design and media training and years of practical design
experience. Separate condition-free AI reviews were stored before human
feedback, and the condition mapping was revealed afterwards. A missing rank
in the second round remains incomplete. Human and AI judgments were compared,
not merged into an invented consensus.

## Feedback and changes

The recurring development sequence was research, module revision, fresh paired
generation, blind feedback, and focused inspection or repair. Those five
activities were repeated as needed, not applied as a fixed generation quota.

- Spacing failures prompted explicit grids, type roles, region ownership and
  matched visual and measured checks, including artwork when text already fit.
- Clean local geometry did not always produce a convincing whole. Inspection
  now begins with communication, hierarchy and character before consulting
  design rationale, then proceeds to groups and details.
- Technically valid alternatives often differed in type, density, colour and
  illustration craft. Preferences remain preferences unless a concrete defect
  supports a stronger finding. A preferred component need not decide the whole.
- Useful icons sometimes had poor placement or an unnecessary surrounding
  shape. Icon construction and visual treatment were separated instead of
  turning that feedback into an instruction to remove icons.
- Dense advance rules could narrow exploration. Early choices may now remain
  provisional, with the system committed before extending repetition and
  checked at the actual rendered result.
- A small ornamental join escaped a later review. The final follow-up checks
  outer junctions and internal gaps together, including deliberate asymmetry.

## What this evidence supports

It documents iterative development with one human reviewer. It is not a
population preference study, an unseen holdout, or a controlled comparison of
old and new Skill versions. Briefs, package revisions and eventually transport
changed across rounds, so apparent differences between rounds do not isolate
the effect of one instruction change.

The final PLAN-0029 follow-up used authored compound-join examples, actual
render inspection and measurements. It did not repeat the paired generation
experiment. Structural tests verify package and helper contracts, not general
visual judgment or reliable defect detection by every host.

Raw session transcripts, anonymous assignment records and frozen working copies
remain in the local development archive. This public summary provides the
counts, procedure, changes and limits without publishing private session data.
