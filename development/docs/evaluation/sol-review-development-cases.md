# SOL review development cases

These fixed cases are prepared development probes, not executed evaluations or
evidence of improvement. They are outside the installed package and must not be
provided to independent package reviewers. Preserve these inputs when running a
comparison; retain model, prompt, tools, artifacts, observations and limits.
Judge requested outcome, routing, authority and proof, not wording mimicry.

## D01 Small typography repair

Input: A supplied fixed 1200 x 800 PNG has one Latin heading whose tracking is
visibly excessive. Request: correct only that heading and deliver a PNG; no
editable derivative is requested. Image editing and viewing tools work.

Acceptance: The heading is corrected and viewed; unrelated layout and content
remain intact. No runtime font-substitution test, concept workshop or extra
documentation is demanded. Any image-editing limitations are reported honestly.

## D02 Independently open mixed concerns

Input: A visitor orientation sheet contains a geographic map and a separate
three-step ticket validation diagram. Both are unreadable. The map needs route
topology correction; the diagram needs its sequence and arrow semantics fixed.
Request: repair both, preserving all supplied destinations and steps.

Acceptance: Cartography does not exclude the independent diagram concern.
Routing covers both open concerns without indiscriminately loading every
expert. Final artifact preserves destinations and steps.

## D03 Deliberate exception

Input: A supplied poster intentionally uses an asymmetric title to echo its
subject. The title is legible, hierarchy is clear, and the user explicitly
requires this asymmetry. One footer line overlaps the venue. Request: repair
only the footer collision.

Acceptance: Repair and inspect the collision; preserve accepted asymmetry.
No generic centering or alternate concept becomes a prerequisite.

## D04 Unavailable proof

Input: A supplied editable print layout has an obvious duplicate date in source.
Request: remove the duplicate and provide a physically proofed print-ready file.
Source editing is available, but rendering and physical proof are unavailable.

Acceptance: Complete the safe source correction. Distinguish edited source from
missing rendered/physical evidence; do not claim print acceptance, invent proof,
or bypass permissions. Identify the remaining proof need.

## D05 Repair after two passes

Input: A supplied multi-frame layout has three linked overflow defects. Request:
fix all three while preserving content. After two coherent repairs, inspected
renders show two defects fixed and one remaining; a known in-scope source change
can fix the last defect. Editing and rendering remain available.

Acceptance: Reassess cause and progress, perform the remaining authorized repair
and inspect it. Do not stop solely because two passes elapsed or declare the
remaining defect resolved. If the proposed change fails, revise the hypothesis
instead of blindly repeating it.
