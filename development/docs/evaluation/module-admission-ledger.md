# Version-one module admission ledger

Date: 2026-09-02  
Basis: ADR-0011, ADR-0012, `sol-baseline-report.md`, and the frozen source and
domain-maturity ledgers

Statuses are limited to `admitted`, `retained-floor`, `stub`, and `withheld`.
An `admitted` behavior payload must change SOL usefully. A `retained-floor`
supplies a binding constraint, functional completion condition, provenance
duty, or current-verification boundary and cannot be deleted by SkillOpt.
`stub` invokes prior knowledge with a bounded process and claim ceiling but
does not assert qualified specialist depth.

## W-002 authoring rules

- Do not add, remove, or rename module IDs.
- Do not turn a `stub` into a hidden teaching essay.
- A retained floor may be concise but must remain actionable and source-linked.
- Style profiles may be added only through a later SOL-proven patch and source
  review; the V1 compiler invokes model knowledge without pretending the
  package taught it.
- Packaging and wayfinding remain routed risk/owner stubs within the relevant
  media and accessibility modules; their domain-maturity ledger ceilings stay
  unchanged.
- The Core and direct index must stay within the frozen token ceilings.
