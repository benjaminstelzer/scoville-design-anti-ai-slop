# Add practical design guidance

## Goal

Turn the 2026-09-05 module audit into concrete, source-bounded design assistance within the existing 30-module Skill: teach the missing operations, demonstrate difficult choices, preserve useful conventions and exceptions, and verify the effect of retained changes in actual artifacts. Cover all 23 proposed module improvements plus the Core recommendation and preserve the seven modules without a substantiated extension need.

The complete owner, mechanism and verification mapping is in `docs/evaluation/plan-0007-practical-guidance-contract.md`. The retained audit is `docs/reviews/0022-practical-guidance-audit-20260905.md`. The user requested this fix plan and independent Astra and Fable review; that authorizes creating and refining these planning/review artifacts now. Review findings and their disposition belong in `docs/reviews/0023-practical-guidance-plan-review.md`, not in runtime instructions.

The user's 2026-09-05 follow-up authorizes direct implementation once the Plan is ready. After the completed Astra/Fable plan review, PLAN-0006 is preserved as draft with W-004 still paused and all original evidence intact, and PLAN-0007 is active at W-001. This preserves unfinished work instead of claiming its completion or cancelling it. Its unresolved DH5 and final Fable lanes retain their original meaning and do not count as PLAN-0007 evidence.

## Non-goals

No runtime edits or execution of the planned design cases during this planning/review request. No installation, commit, push, release, public-copy rewrite, sealed holdout access or historical-result mutation. No additional module, router/schema redesign, independent spacing owner, compulsory record for routine work, universal numerical aesthetic rule, general legal/cultural/script authority, or claim of measured model superiority. New modules or materially changed ownership require a separate decision. Source-family IDs and expert opinions do not prove individual prescriptions or observed improvement. Resource estimates remain advisory under ADR-0032; provider-enforced limits and evidence/authorization controls remain binding.
