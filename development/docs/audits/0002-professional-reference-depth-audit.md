# Audit: professional depth of the Design expert references

Date: 2026-09-02  
Status: W-011 consolidated audit; implementation not started  
Scope: `SKILL.md`, `modules.yaml`, all fourteen files in `references/`, the
source and rule ledgers, public Skill prior art, canonical curricula, and the
Design/UI/production ownership contract

## Result

The RC7 package has a sound progressive-disclosure architecture and a useful
professional safety floor, but its expert leaves are too compressed to be the
knowledge base of the all-round graphic designer requested for this project.
The main defect is not missing topic names. It is missing applied causal depth:
many leaves name variables without consistently teaching how to choose among
them, recognize a failure, identify the parent cause, make the smallest repair,
preserve the successful parts, permit a justified exception, and prove the
result in the final medium.

The successor should therefore deepen the retained craft leaves and split only
concerns with independently detectable task signals, different causal failure
models, different authorities, and independent tests. Correct professional
coverage comes first, selective routing second, and compression third. The
1,800-token figure remains a cost target, not a correctness ceiling.

The research supports a successor map of twenty-three flat, directly routed
expert leaves. It also supports one non-routed source index. No leaf reads a
sibling or depends on a live webpage. The high-priority capability is applied
Latin typography, composition, spacing, hierarchy, negative space, style-safe
generation, responsive recomposition, fixed/editorial medium intent, causal
critique, and rendered repair. Non-Latin coverage remains a detection,
rendering, no-Latin-default, and specialist-escalation floor.

## Evidence base and limits

This audit reconciles five independent evidence classes:

1. Fourteen file-specific audits in
   [`docs/research/reference-audits/`](../research/reference-audits/), each with
   the same current-contract, gap, rule-quality, prior-art, authoritative-source,
   Dos/Don'ts, architecture, test, and priority contract.
2. A canonical curriculum ledger containing 82 unique source records: 13
   curricula, 33 reference-only books, 7 public readings, 4 adaptable OER
   sources, 14 standards, 9 professional manuals, and 2 public-domain/CC0
   sources. It covers ten lanes and thirty cross-lane rule clusters.
3. A Stage-2 public-Skill comparison for every current reference. Stars rank
   only candidates that first satisfy exact-domain and E1-or-better evidence
   requirements. Evidence level, visual quality, licensing, and adoption value
   remain separate from popularity.
4. Fable 5.1 High's independent per-reference depth review, including its
   cross-reference and source-map findings.
5. The frozen RC7 and UI routing evidence plus the later Terra High open
   transfer cases. Those runs establish selected routing and outcome behavior;
   they do not establish general professional competence.

The curriculum is deliberately strongest in Latin typography and Western
graphic-design foundations. Canonical status means recurrent teaching,
field-defining influence, current standard authority, current synthesis, or
mature professional consensus. It does not mean universal truth, cultural
neutrality, empirical proof, or permission to copy. Closed books and public
readings are reference-only. Runtime wording must be independently authored.

## What is already strong and must be preserved

- One compact Core routes only material concerns and keeps the canonical task
  modes `generate`, `critique`, `repair`, and `style-direction` explicit.
  Research, decision, exception, and claim boundary are capabilities or test
  dimensions, not additional modes.
- Expert modules are flat and directly selected. There are no expert-to-expert
  chains, hidden dependencies, or requirement to load the whole curriculum.
- The incumbent product design system remains above either Skill. Design owns
  design definition and judgment; UI owns strict framework/system
  implementation and rendered interaction proof; UI retains a bounded
  standalone Greenfield fallback when Design is unavailable or inactive.
- Design starts from brief, content, audience, medium, authority, and intended
  effect rather than a fashionable style pack.
- Rules can be broken through a declared exception with an intended gain,
  preserved floors, compensating structure, an owner, a falsifier, and proof.
- Existing leaves already contain valuable floors: relations before
  coordinates, roles before faces and palettes, image thesis before prompting,
  supplied-data integrity, source versus rendition, source-first repair,
  evidence labels, authority boundaries, and claim ceilings.
- Source IDs are provenance pointers, not runtime dependencies. The agent gets
  the operative rule locally. A dead link reduces later verification but does
  not erase the rule from the Skill.
- The current style compiler correctly treats a style label as ambiguous and a
  style as a causal system rather than a bundle of motifs.

## Cross-cutting defects

### 1. Rule nouns are not yet a professional decision procedure

Terms such as measure, leading, tracking, grid, hierarchy, negative space,
fallback, gamut, sequence, state, proof, or authority are useful prompts but do
not by themselves teach applied judgment. Each accepted leaf needs:

- task inputs, authority, protected content, medium, and output conditions;
- formal variables and the relationships among them;
- generation moves and selection criteria;
- observable failure signatures and likely parent causes;
- smallest causal repairs and a preserve set;
- rule exceptions, compensating controls, and falsifiers;
- deterministic, rendered, human, and domain-authority evidence;
- a precise claim ceiling.

### 2. Rule strength is not visible enough at runtime

Every consequential instruction must be identifiable as one of:

1. binding constraint;
2. evidence-bounded rule;
3. contextual convention;
4. heuristic;
5. attributed preference;
6. deliberate exception.

Numeric guidance must name its source scope, population, language/script,
medium, task, and the observation that should override it. Values such as 45 to
75 characters, two typefaces, an 8-pixel grid, one contrast number, one bleed,
one rich black, or one breakpoint cannot become universal defaults.

### 3. Critique vocabulary and repair records are inconsistent

Core, ADR-0007, domain leaves, and the critique leaf must use one finding
record:

- target and evidence state;
- exact observation and stable location;
- likely effect and authority;
- cause state as confirmed, inferred, or unknown;
- verdict as `defect`, `tradeoff`, `attributed preference`,
  `intentional exception`, or `unverifiable`;
- severity and confidence as separate fields;
- smallest repair and protected dimensions;
- validation target, actual evidence, and remaining unknowns.

Critique must inspect the whole before parts, identify the parent cause before
patching symptoms, compare like with like, rerender the affected contexts, and
stop when the declared failure is resolved. More findings are not automatically
better critique.

### 4. Medium logic needs explicit ownership

Responsive web design is not a scaled fixed canvas. Fixed print and editorial
work are not responsive pages frozen at one viewport. Shared typography,
composition, colour, imagery, brand, and style rules remain in their craft
leaves. Two direct medium leaves should own transformation and medium intent:

- `web-and-responsive-design` owns intended reflow, reorder, disclosure,
  density, crop/source changes, navigation transformation, content pressure,
  and preservation across width, zoom, text expansion, orientation, and input
  context. UI owns framework breakpoints, queries, component semantics, focus,
  input, announcements, and rendered interaction proof.
- `editorial-and-fixed-media-design` owns fixed dimensions, viewing distance,
  pages, spreads, sequence, recurring anchors, pacing, folds/faces, trim and
  safe intent, and the design record for physical or fixed output. Media
  Production owns preflight, export, receiver specifications, and provider or
  physical proof.

Packaging and wayfinding remain bounded generalist floors in this release. The
Skill may design the communication layer and identify physical constraints, but
must not claim structural packaging, materials engineering, regulatory,
installation, environmental-safety, or specialist wayfinding competence.

### 5. Spacing and rhythm must not become a detached recipe module

An independent spacing leaf is rejected for the successor. Spacing expresses
the relationship it separates or connects, so an isolated module would load
routinely beside composition, typography, UI, and each medium leaf while losing
the parent cause.

- Composition owns semantic grouping, macro rhythm, density, margins, grid
  intervals, negative space, optical balance, and deliberate spatial tension.
- Typography owns glyph, word, line, paragraph, baseline, column, and break
  relationships.
- Design owns design-system spacing roles and intended relationships; UI owns
  their strict framework/token implementation when active.
- Responsive and fixed-media leaves own how those relationships transform or
  are proved in their medium, without redefining the base rules.

The applicable rule is not “more whitespace.” Every major interval or empty
region needs a relational job such as grouping, separation, emphasis, pacing,
framing, navigation, accommodation, or deliberate tension. Optical equality may
override geometric equality when the relationship is clearer and the protected
floors still pass.

## Typography decisions

Typography is the first implementation and evaluation priority. The successor
must support professional Latin generation, critique, and repair across display,
navigation, body, annotation, data, caption, action, and fallback roles.

### Typeface selection and combination

The Skill must never start with a font-count rule or a genre formula. It should:

1. assign jobs and required content conditions;
2. inspect repertoire, language, differentiability, optical sizes, available
   weights and widths, numerals, punctuation, features, fallback, rendering,
   licensing, and medium constraints;
3. compare voice and formal relationships including proportion, x-height, cap
   height, width, stroke contrast, stress, terminals, counters, apertures,
   rhythm, texture, and typographic colour;
4. choose sufficient role contrast while retaining enough shared structure or
   tonal coherence that faces do not collapse into each other or compete;
5. test one-family, proposed multi-family, and fallback specimens with real
   text at final size, measure, state, and output condition.

One family may satisfy every role. Additional families are justified only by a
real role, language, data, brand, medium, or technical need and by better
hierarchy/coherence in the rendered specimen. “Serif headline plus sans body” is
one possible relationship, never the rule.

### Typesetting and microtypography

The leaf must teach coupled decisions and causal repair for:

- font metrics and shaping before manual kerning;
- kerning, tracking, word spacing, ligatures, and display hand-kerning as
  distinct operations;
- type size, measure, leading, paragraph spacing/indent, and column count as an
  interacting reading system;
- alignment, rag, justification, hyphenation language/dictionary, consecutive
  breaks, rivers, last lines, and manual-break hazards;
- hierarchy through size, weight, width, style, placement, space, colour, case,
  and contrast without making every distinction simultaneously loud;
- punctuation, quotes, dashes, ellipses, spaces, fractions, dates, currencies,
  tabular/proportional and oldstyle/lining numerals, and locale-specific forms;
- widows, orphans, isolated headings, page/column breaks, baseline and optical
  edge decisions in the final page or screen context;
- actual-font and fallback renders, zoom/reflow/text-spacing stress, font
  embedding/subsetting, and print or supplier proof where applicable.

Measure ranges and other numbers may appear only as sourced starting
hypotheses. Actual language, typeface, reader, task, display, column, and medium
decide whether they work.

### Multiscript ceiling

The second leaf detects language, script, direction, repertoire, shaping,
marks, fallback, line breaking, and vertical/bidirectional requirements. It must
not transfer Latin casing, tracking, italic, hyphenation, justification, word
spacing, or line-break habits. It renders supplied text, reports unsupported or
drifting output, and requires a native reader or specialist when consequence is
material. It does not claim non-Latin art direction or expert multiscript
typesetting.

## Composition decisions

The composition leaf remains one causal system rather than separate grid,
whitespace, hierarchy, balance, and rule-breaking checklists. It should:

- define importance, narrative, task, visual, programmatic, and focus order;
- map semantic groups and choose the least complex useful spatial system from
  content, format, variation, and production constraints;
- use scale, position, alignment, repetition, interval, density, crop, overlap,
  figure/ground, and negative space as relational variables;
- distinguish balance from symmetry and order from uniformity;
- create controlled tension or off-grid exceptions only with counterstructure;
- stress real long/short/localized content, image ratios, empty/full states,
  edges, page/spread sequence, and alternate sizes;
- repair the first parent relationship that fails rather than normalizing every
  local gap;
- inspect intended size plus diagnostic thumbnail, strip/spread, narrow/wide,
  crop, and production views as applicable.

An interesting arrangement is not random novelty. It creates a legible
relationship, purposeful tension, pace, or subject-specific metaphor while
preserving content, access, and medium constraints. A conventional arrangement
may be the more professional result when the job requires speed, trust, or
dense comparison.

## Style decisions

The executable Skill should contain a style compiler, not an exhaustive atlas
of 1980s, Neon, Minimalist, Brutalist, Neoclassical, or other named recipes. For
each named or period direction, the compiler records:

- the selected meaning of the label, alternatives, source/date/status,
  confidence, and contradictions;
- dominant lineage, bounded secondary influences, original purpose, production
  technology, materials, and historical constraints;
- structural DNA across hierarchy, composition, spacing, typography, colour,
  imagery, symbols, ornament, texture, motion, and interaction where relevant;
- the subject-specific relation and any contemporary functional counterweight;
- incumbent brand/design-system invariants, allowed variation, cultural/rights
  conditions, and anachronism/revival/hybrid status;
- medium-native mappings, accessible equivalents, removal test, cliché test,
  and representative proof.

For an “80s neon” webpage, the compiler must first disambiguate possible
branches such as retro-computing/CRT, Memphis/postmodern graphics, new-wave
editorial, corporate broadcast, arcade, club/flyer, or later synthwave
reinterpretation. It then selects a source-bounded mechanism that fits the
brief. ASCII, VHS noise, neon glow, grids, and chrome are optional consequences,
not a mandatory symbol bundle. Removing decorative markers should still leave a
coherent hierarchy, subject relationship, and medium-native design.

The inspected Taste Skill material is useful as negative and bounded positive
prior art: reference-first inspection and visible examples are valuable, while
large fixed rule packs, exact token systems, mandatory section patterns, and one
premium-editorial definition of “minimalist” do not generalize. Named profiles
remain withheld until one profile can pass source, generation, critique,
repair, translation, and anti-cliché tests without becoming a costume recipe.

## Source architecture

The successor uses three layers:

1. **Runtime rule in the owning leaf.** The agent receives the actual decision,
   exception, repair, and proof logic without opening a link.
2. **Non-routed install-time index and rule-to-source map.**
   `references/source-index.md` keeps source IDs resolvable without acting as an
   expert route. Each consequential cluster resolves to source IDs, rule type,
   scope, limits, and current-verification trigger.
3. **Research/source ledgers.** Full bibliographic, edition, license, bias,
   currency, and reuse evidence remains outside ordinary runtime loading.

Broken or changed links therefore do not remove runtime knowledge. They do
block new source-dependent claims or release-sensitive verification until a
replacement/current authority is recorded. Source headers in each leaf must
list only IDs that resolve in the rule map. The non-routed source index remains
documentation, not a hidden expert dependency.

RC7's package validator is part of the migration: its hardcoded fourteen IDs,
1,800-token error, current source-leaf assumption, orphan rule, and old mixed
ceilings must be replaced by successor-aware checks before reference authoring.
The replacement keeps source resolution and dependency errors hard while
treating 1,800 as a target and validating recorded per-leaf and common-load
ceilings from the complete payloads.

## Evaluation required before a competence claim

### Early-fail development sequence

The earlier large-batch failure mode must not recur. For each implementation
wave:

1. validate schema, source IDs, generated routes, token counts, and read graph;
2. run a model-free route fixture before any model call;
3. run one open `gpt-5.6-terra` High canary;
4. inspect the terminal response, exact read graph, artifact source, render,
   deterministic checks, and provider token receipt immediately;
5. repair infrastructure or package behavior before the next call;
6. only then expand the same wave.

No group of dozens of CLI jobs may run before its first result is inspected.
The exact pre-registered packing and budget is in
[`docs/evaluation/open-successor-call-plan.md`](../evaluation/open-successor-call-plan.md):
22 coverage calls, 16 SkillOpt calls, 8 total reserve calls, a hard maximum of
46 requests, a 5.0-million provider-total token ceiling, a 2.75-million
uncached-input-plus-output ceiling, a direct-cost preflight, and explicit user
reauthorization before any increase. Routing positives and near-neighbor
negatives are model-free. The five wave canaries are already credited inside
the 22 coverage calls. `docs/evaluation/leaf-contract-matrix.yaml` maps all 23
leaves to all six required dimensions and the exact calls; its validator also
rejects future-wave canary dependencies and noncanonical modes.

### Per-leaf applied contract

Every changed leaf receives at least:

- one selective-routing positive and one near-neighbor negative;
- one generation or decision case;
- one critique case with seeded defect, tradeoff, preference, exception, and
  unverifiable state where applicable;
- one smallest-repair case with protected strengths;
- one exception or claim-boundary case;
- deterministic and rendered evidence suited to the medium;
- one owner-boundary check against Design, UI, Media, Sources, or domain
  authority as applicable.

The machine-checkable leaf-contract matrix maps every leaf to each required
dimension and exact call. Typography, Composition, Web, and Fixed Media are
exercised in `generate`, `critique`, and `repair`; Style is exercised in
`style-direction`, `critique`, and `repair`; the Critique leaf participates in
the applicable generation/exception, read-only critique, and repair contexts.
Lower-risk leaves may share cross-domain fixtures only when each leaf has
independently falsifiable acceptance criteria. Deliberate exception and claim
boundary remain dimensions rather than modes.

### Visual comparison material

Self-created fixtures and lawfully obtained comparison pairs may be used in a
separate local-only evaluation workspace. The public repository stores only a
fixture description, source/license receipt or private-manifest reference,
content hash, scoring rubric, anonymized result, and aggregate evidence. It must
not contain copyrighted book pages, third-party before/after screenshots, or
restricted datasets.

Pairwise evaluation must freeze content, dimensions, fonts/assets, renderer,
state, and medium. Reviewers compare the target relation rather than simply
choosing the more decorative output. Critical visual-quality judgments use
blinded qualified reviewers where available and report disagreement.

### SkillOpt

SkillOpt is used only after the coverage-complete candidate passes hard
behavior. It may remove repetition, model-prior exposition, and redundant
examples. It may not delete binding floors, applied repair knowledge, necessary
exceptions, owner boundaries, or proof requirements. Every proposal is tested
with Terra High on the affected routes, one call at a time, and promoted only
when it is non-inferior on behavior and materially better on selective context.

### Qualification ceiling

Passing the planned tests can support source-grounded and typed applied guidance
for the tested scopes, media, tools, languages, and reviewers. It cannot support
universal taste, expert equivalence, native-reader or font-engineering
qualification, global cultural authority, legal clearance, statistical or
cartographic correctness, supplier acceptance, physical safety, accessibility
for all users, market leadership, or untested style mastery.

## Review gate before implementation

The exact audit, module map, ownership decisions, implementation waves, and test
contract must be frozen by hash and sent independently to a fresh SOL reviewer
and Fable 5.1 High. Each reviewer must return `READY` or findings classified as
Blocker, High, Medium, or Low across:

- professional coverage and forgotten generalist capabilities;
- rule typing, source scope, licensing, and anti-recipe behavior;
- progressive disclosure and module independence;
- Design/UI/production/source/domain ownership;
- typography, composition, spacing, style, responsive, and fixed-medium depth;
- implementability, early-fail testing, SkillOpt, and claim ceilings.

Every Blocker and High finding is verified, repaired or explicitly disproved
with evidence. Fable independently reviewed the Plan and returned `REVISE`; its
persisted findings remain visible and were all reconciled. Provider budget and
session limits prevented a returned Fable follow-up, and the user explicitly
waived further Fable calls. That waiver is user authority, not Fable agreement
or product evidence. Implementation starts only after a fresh SOL continuation
returns `READY` on the corrected exact bundle with no unresolved Blocker or
High finding and all model-free/native checks pass. Medium and Low findings are
incorporated or recorded with rationale.

## Audit conclusion

The project should not discard RC7's modular foundation. It should replace its
compressed expert payloads with the twenty-three-leaf successor above, using
typed and source-bounded rules, one causal critique language, explicit medium
ownership, and proof that matches the artifact. The differentiator is not more
design trivia. It is the ability to design, distinguish, and repair actual work
without turning conventions into laws or expressive exceptions into excuses.
