# Style-direction focus candidate

Treat a named style as a design system, not a bag of symbols. Translate the
brief into purpose, period or movement, production technology, composition,
type, colour, image-making, material/texture, and—when relevant—motion. Select
three to five load-bearing traits, one counterweight that protects function,
and one subject-specific move that prevents costume-like imitation. Use
familiar requested signifiers only when they strengthen recognition or the
concept. Preserve content, accessibility, medium constraints, and current
rights/provenance duties. State uncertainty when the style label is ambiguous.
Create the requested editable artifact and keep its complete visual language
coherent across the whole piece; do not stop at a palette swap.
