# Scoville Design Anti-AI-Slop

Make visual decisions from the subject and task, not a reusable look.
Load knowledge only when it changes an open decision, diagnosis, repair or proof.

## Ownership

Design owns concept, art direction, hierarchy, corporate-design/visual-identity
definition, typography, composition, spacing, responsive transformation intent,
design-system definition and visual critique. Corporate Identity strategy,
culture, behaviour, naming and verbal identity remain authorised upstream inputs.
Preserve incumbent systems unless their authorised owner opens a change.

With the optional `scoville-ui-anti-ai-slop` implementation Skill active and
applicable, Design defines and judges; that partner implements
framework/component mechanics, states, semantics, accessibility, responsiveness
and runtime proof.
Never require or simulate another Skill. Optional active-applicable partner
coordination (including `scoville-scribe-anti-ai-slop` for wording fidelity) uses the Coordination module;
without a partner, handle authorised work directly within the same ownership floors.

## Studio loop

Choose `generate`, `critique` or `repair`. Critique is read-only; repair changes
only the authorised artifact and scope. Style direction is a domain, not a mode.

Records are working notes, not extra deliverables unless requested or needed for
an agreed handoff. Use `concern | decision/status | evidence status`. Expand only
open relevant fields. For solo phases as well as owner handoffs, carry the exact
source/authority, protected constraint and unknown needed by the next phase.
Preserve the canonical owner's status meanings: approval is not verification.

1. **Frame.** Resolve applicable purpose, audience, message, exact required
   content, subject evidence, medium/context, constraints, protected strengths
   and consequential unknowns. A settled small repair needs no full inventory.
   Do not invent strategy, facts, permission or approval.
2. **Route.** Select every materially applicable module through the direct index,
   without a fixed expert count or context/token gate. Load the relevant
   expertise for the actual decisions.
   Split phases only for task needs or actual capacity, carrying the record;
   headings do not unload context. Explicit user/provider limits, authorisation,
   evidence and safety controls remain binding. A mentioned topic alone is not
   an open decision. The boundaries below do not exclude independent concerns.

   | Module | Open decision that selects it | What does not select it alone |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Brief | Missing/conflicting fields or an open communication concern such as misreading or modality, even with complete supplied fields. | A settled brief or ordinary restatement needs no framing module. |
   | Generic | Interchangeability diagnosis or a thesis needing subject differentiation. | A legitimate quiet utility direction or settled incumbent execution needs no specificity intervention. |
   | Critique | A separate multi-domain finding/control lifecycle or challenged cross-domain exception. | Critique mode and owner-local checks, comparisons or exceptions stay with their owners. |
   | Composition | General hierarchy, grouping, spacing, negative space, grid or fit is open. | Web pressure belongs to Web; fixed specialist geometry/labels and Packaging panel hierarchy do not reopen Composition. |
   | Cartography | Map geometry, orientation, topology, extent, boundary or spatial encoding is open, including within a Wayfinding task. | Wayfinding alone or a map with those decisions settled does not select Cartography. |
   | Diagrams / Instructional | An independent relational diagram or explanation task is open. | A Wayfinding map task does not also imply either concern. |
   | Mark versus Concept / Brand | Mark form alone is open within a settled identity. | Mark work does not reopen Concept or Brand unless their decisions are independently open. |
   | Media Production | Source/derivative, export, preflight, provider or production handoff is open. | File writing or syntax checks alone are not production design. |
   | Colour | Colour or reproduction decisions are open. | A fixed medium alone does not select Colour. |
   | Coordination | An active-applicable partner needs an actual cross-owner exchange. | Partner availability, a name mention or solo phase work alone does not select it. |

3. **Make or inspect.** When concept selection is open, compare materially
   different mechanisms and resolve a subject-specific thesis before final
   execution, unless multiple directions were requested. A verified subject
   fact should visibly shape a relevant decision: hierarchy, type, colour,
   image or motion. Record which fact carries which decision. If swapping logo
   and nouns serves any unrelated subject, examine the relation, not just polish.
   Quiet utility, legitimate defaults and accepted directions need no novelty;
   "none yet" is an honest interim state, not automatically a defect.
   Create the requested editable artifact, inspect read-only, or repair within
   the authorised scope. Use real supplied content/assets. Mark placeholders and constructed material.
   Do not invent deliverables, states, media or permission outside the frame.
4. **Inspect evidence.** Use the artifact owner and proper renderer at intended
   size/context. Add diagnostic views only for a material risk (crop, alternate
   size/state, sequence, playback, separation or physical proof). If evidence is
   unavailable, do safe authorised source work, disclose the missing lane and
   withhold acceptance dependent on it.
5. **Judge and repair.** Preserve what works. Locate each material observation;
   distinguish confirmed/inferred/unknown cause and defect, tradeoff, attributed
   preference, intentional exception or unverifiable result. State the smallest
   repair and relevant proof. Change the parent owner before patching instances;
   rerender affected consumers. Comparisons hold non-intervention variables
   constant, not the authorised change and its declared dependent effects.
   After two coherent passes reassess cause/progress; continue necessary work
   while evidence supports it, change approach on repeated failure, and stop at
   completion or a real authority/evidence blocker. Never call a defect resolved
   while it remains. Detailed finding/control records belong to Critique.

## Rules, exceptions and proof ceiling

Distinguish binding constraints, evidence-bounded rules, conventions, heuristics,
preferences and deliberate exceptions. Numeric advice needs population,
language/script, medium, task and override evidence; no universal aesthetic law
follows from a starting range, token, remembered rule or one study.

An exception needs intent, gain, preserved communication/accessibility,
compensating structure, cost and falsifier. Compare a fair conventional control
when the exception is challenged; ordinary local exceptions stay with their
owner. Facts, rights, consent, current compliance and production constraints
cannot become aesthetic exceptions.

A render proves only inspected appearance. Syntax, interaction/playback, user
research, rights, accessibility, provider acceptance and physical proof are
distinct evidence lanes owned by their actual authorities. Report exact tests
and material gaps, distinguishing not-applicable from applicable but unverified.
No label implies the next evidence level. If a current standard, law, licence,
provider, jurisdiction or living-community claim cannot be checked, mark it
unverified and stop at that authority boundary; do not substitute model memory.

## Terms

- **Module / expert / leaf:** one linked reference and its domain role, not a subagent.
- **Owner:** the person/domain authorised to change a field's decision or status.
- **Observer:** reports attributed effects without changing another owner's decision.
- **Falsifier:** the observation that would overturn a judgment or exception.
- **Control:** a fair comparison preserving unrelated conditions.
- **Claim ceiling:** the strongest claim the observed evidence actually supports.

## Direct expert index

`draft` is development status, not admission or a quality verdict. Metadata
`evidence` values are identifiers for external development receipts, not files
included in this installed package. An empty list means none is referenced;
a nonempty list may reference failures or limited results, not a pass or general
qualification. Without the external record, its outcome is unverified. Do not
fetch receipts for ordinary use. The package does not establish measured benefit
over an unaided model. Route by open concern, not status.
